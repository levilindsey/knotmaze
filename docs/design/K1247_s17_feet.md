## Why

Stage 16 (merged, 541d22fb; its handoff is at the end of `docs/design/K1247_s16_continuous.md`) cut def's
jumps from 33 places to 18. A jump is a drawn vertex moving over 0.25 px between recorded frames while
every input within 2h moves under 0.1 px. They show as flicker at junctions, and they block K1268 (no δ
holds while the outline jumps). What's left:
- **The corner foot** (j91, j30, j87, j54, j42, j73, j181, j178, j135, j105, j90): a corner's foot is the
  nearest point on the border, which jumps across a concave vertex's bisector. `K16_CB=loc` removes them
  but moves the look at rest (cmp 670/354/134/16, 42 census crossings worse).
- **j176** (1.004 / 0.980 px): the cut length Lcut is chosen from positions far away.
- **j45** (0.727), **j0** (0.634), **j39** (0.358 / 0.334): not diagnosed.
- **K1288:** `addPointAtC`'s nearest-segment pick flips between two border branches about 0.01 px apart on
  a fold (muse_hash cyc10 #200, 1.311 px). The union check got worse with stage 16's tie spread
  (drawn > 0.25 px, still / cyc10 / cyc18: master 4 / 14 / 5, now 2 / 16 / 8).

Stage 16's lesson: a foot made continuous leaves the exact nearest point near a concave bisector, so the
look moves exactly where the old foot jumped. Judge that move against form B, not against master's pixels.
