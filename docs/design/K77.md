# K77 brief: sharp, wedge-like depressions in plucked threads

Levi, 2026-10-12: "I noticed with plucks, I sometimes see somewhat sharp
depressions in the thread where the pluck vibration effect is stronger. I
guess this maybe happens more around curves? In general, a depression or
thinning of the thread makes sense, but these depressions seem too sharp
and wedge-like."

## Context

- Render-only plucks: `src/render/pluck_overlay.ts` and
  `src/render/pluck_model.ts`.
- `.claude/rules/render.md` has the concave-side cap: 0.9 x the
  5-station circumradius minus the thread, slope-limited at CAP_SLOPE 1
  per world, with TIGHT_K stations kept intact around too-tight bends.
- `docs/history.md` has RENDER-ONLY PLUCKS (2026-10-11): the base ribbon
  collapses (widthMul) to what the overlay's opaque core always covers;
  soft nodes cap the swing at NODE_CORE h inside footprints and open at
  NODE_SLOPE beyond.
- A symmetric cap and a 3-station radius were tried and REJECTED: they
  carved hooks' blurs into lumps.
- The tapped lobe's amplitude floor rose from 0.45 h to 0.65 h on
  2026-10-12 (44885f3), so typical swings are about 44% wider. Find out
  whether that exposed or worsened the wedges: A/B the two floors.

## Method

1. Reproduce. Use `__knotPluck(chain, ctrl, opts)` on a settled `fw=0`
   page with a frozen paint clock, stepping the pluck phase. Survey
   straight lobes, curves, tight bends, near crossings (soft nodes) and
   near junctions; crop at DSF 2-3.
2. Attribute each wedge to its term. Candidates:
   - the base ribbon's widthMul collapse;
   - the overlay's core;
   - the concave cap and its slope limit;
   - the node caps;
   - the TIGHT_K ramp.

   Make the per-station profiles visible (amplitude, caps, widthMul) and
   correlate them with the pixels. Change nothing before the term is
   known.
3. Fix it at the root so a depression is a smooth, rounded thinning:
   limit the second difference or curvature of the collapse profile, not
   just its slope. Don't regress the rejected-cap cases (hook blurs at
   normal zoom) or the weave checks: partner casing bands unchanged, and
   every erase, fill and fillet buffer identical during a pluck.
4. Run the `pluck_model` unit tests (add a profile-smoothness test), tsc,
   vitest and the build. Make a before/after contact sheet for Levi.
