## Task 1: the exact outline

Run K's construction sampled offset polylines (100 points per round,
every 0.01h along the edges) and read curvature as signed Menger over
a 0.05h baseline, dropping points inside tubes or under a fill. The
exact outline here is traced from its pieces in closed form: each
segment's two offset lines and each vertex's arc of radius h, unioned
with the rounds' fills as `inFill` defines them. A fill/tube meet is a
line/circle or circle/circle intersection of the ball
(`|p − q| = r`) with a tube piece; its tangent jump is the angle
between the ball's tangent and the nearest centreline feature's
tangent (a segment's direction, or a vertex arc's tangent) at that
point; a piece's curvature is analytic (0 on a line, +1/h on a vertex
arc, +1/r on the ball arc, in the round's own sense). Exposure is an
exact distance evaluation at the intersection point itself (inside
another tube, or under a fill, through `lib_s1b.ts`'s checker).

Error bound of the measurement: there is no sampling step. A meet's
position, jump and curvature are float64 evaluations of the pieces
themselves (~1e-12h rounding), gated at explicit tolerances (a point
counts as on a tube piece when its centreline distance is within
1e-6h of h; exposure tests use form B's own 1e-7h). The only
sampled numbers left are the class attributions, which reuse Run K's
`bareMinK` on resampled edges exactly as Run K computed it, and the
SVG plots' arc polylines.

How it differs from Run K's, and whether class b survives: Run K's
695 sampled cusp corners sorted a 268 / b 267 / c 13 / d 146 / e 1.
On the exact outline, every exposed piece has analytic curvature
≥ 0 in the round's sense, so Run K's κ form, read on the pieces,
fails at 0 corners: there is no concave curvature to read. What the
sampling read as cusps at joints becomes tangent jumps at joints,
which criterion 1v gates only where a fill meets a tube. Class b
(the bare tube's own concave vertices and folds: 267 corners, 111
feet) does not survive: 0 of its corners fail 1v, and its feet are
tangent or buried on the exact outline, as Run K's list for S2
predicted. Its tube-edge joints remain as information (below), not
as failures.

## Task 2: the closed-form solve

`closed.ts` solves form B's rule with no candidate grid and no warm
start (every corner is a pure function of the built instance, so a
cold and a warm solve are the same solve). It takes the arm
polylines within their windows as form B builds them (extracted by
walking the segments the window touches: p50 13 vertices a window,
p90 38, max 77 over the 12 scenes), with h, theta, style and the
gate constants, and returns form B's `Corner` fields.

The distance to a polyline is piecewise — each segment's offset
line, a circle about each vertex — so a point at distance ρ from
both arms lies where two pieces meet: line/line is a bisector,
line/point an offset-line/circle pair, point/point a bisector
line/circle pair. V (ρ = h) is the iteration of that pair solve over
the nearest-feature pair, damped to steps of at most h as form B's
Newton is; where the seed's nearest pair has no point at ρ = h at
all (an offset line farther than h from the other piece's vertex),
at most a few damped Newton steps on the two feet move the seed
until a pair with a closed-form point takes over, all inside the
same iteration bound — the branch walk itself has no Newton step.
The walk then advances pair by pair: q(ρ) is the closed form within
a pair, the next pair starts where a foot leaves its piece (a
segment foot's t reaching an end, or a vertex foot's branch point
crossing an adjacent segment's direction; each crossing bisected on
that scalar, at most 14 pair evaluations, then one nearest scan
verifies the new pair against a folded arm), and where a pair's
locus ceases to exist the branch point jumps a hair past it by the
same pair iteration. Form B's gates (margin by the basin walk on
the window points, steep, cap and the tip-scaled root gate, as
`solveCorner` applies them) are evaluated at the points of an
adaptive Simpson integration of the running minimum S over
ρ ∈ [h, h+r0] (tolerance 5e-5h a panel), in place of master's 32
fixed nodes; ω and room follow form B's definitions (the candidate
scan for ω, the least slack met for room). Where the pair walk
stops short with S still positive, a stepped continuation
(64 panels, each branch point by pair iteration from the last one)
finishes the integral in form B's trapezoid-of-running-min form.

The most work a corner can take, as a function of the window
sizes: let nA, nB be the two windows' point counts. A foot can
change piece at most once per piece it passes, so pair intervals
are bounded by the pieces visited (hard cap 512 intervals); each
transition costs at most 14 pair evaluations plus 2 window scans of
O(nA + nB); each of at most 512 counted w evaluations costs one
margin walk of O(nA + nB); V costs at most 24 pair iterations.
These are the stated maxima (`closed.ts`'s MAX_* constants; the
quadrature's Simpson depth is 16). Observed over the 12 scenes:
at most 198 w evaluations and 60 pair breakpoints on any corner,
28-45 window scans and 40-73 w evaluations a corner on average.

Agreement with master's form B, per corner (V, q, feet and r within
0.01h; the max in device px at dpr 2 beside it):

V agrees exactly everywhere (ΔV = 0 on all 47,109 corners): the
closed-form root is master's root. The outs sit in r, and q and the
feet follow r along the branch. Each out carries its cause in
`out/scenes_s1c.json` and `out/recordings_s1c.json`, assigned
against a converged integral (256 uniform panels on the
nearest-following branch, form B's trapezoid form): of the 377
scene outs, 90 are quadrature (the 32-panel integral and master
agree, the 256-panel and the closed form agree past them — form
B's own discretisation), 67 are branch (32-panel and 256-panel
agree with the closed form against master: master's tracked
advance ends or sheets elsewhere), 70 are closed divergence (the
256-panel integral reproduces master where the pair sheet does
not), and 150 have all three apart. The stored recording outs
(first 200 a recording; 7,356 counted) split 432/117/251 the same
way over their sample. The worst scene out is `g41_ring_tw42` j9:
master r = 0.180h, closed 1.917h, 256-panel 2.000h — the branch
runs ungated where master's advance stops at its second node.

So the closed form is the more exact solve where the branch is
single-sheeted (the sweep stands agree to 0.0009h, task 4), and it
cannot reproduce master's rule where the rule is its tracking:
which sheet the ball grows along at a bifurcating foot, and where
its advance gives up, are artefacts of the 32 stepped nodes that no
branch solve recovers from the arms alone.

## What comes next

Not S2: the rebuild has nothing to build against until the rethink
answers three questions this run isolates.

1. The margin crease (criterion 1v). Master's band lets the ball
   overshoot another part of its arm by up to 0.029h (median
   0.007h over the 62 class-c failures) and draws a crease there
   (median jump 7.3°). Either the rule changes so the ball stops
   at margin 0 — the band Run K rejected because it cost rounds on
   the sampled measurement and moved the motion counts, a trade
   this run did not re-measure — or criterion 1v is restated in
   visible terms (a jump weighted by its overshoot depth, which is
   under half a device px at dpr 2 for all but the one class-d
   corner). Levi's call, with the class-b/d plots and the 1v list
   as the evidence.
2. The branch definition (agreement). Where the medial branch
   bifurcates, master's answer is whichever sheet its 32 stepped
   nodes happened to track. A production rule needs a
   sheet-independent statement (the converged branch, say) and a
   fresh agreement gate against that statement, or the rebuild
   inherits an answer that depends on the solver's step size.
3. The machine (cost and the unanswered GPU question). The closed
   form did not make the corner solve cheap in TypeScript, and
   incrementality does not rescue it (task 3). The standing route
   is the review's S2 item 2, still unmeasured: whether a
   per-pixel distance-field evaluation (the export carries no
   distance today) draws the same outline within the visible
   gates on the worst sites — `g41_user_tw42` j27, j35 and j28,
   the class-a sites of Run K, and this run's 1v worst — at a cost
   that fits. That measurement decides between simplifying the
   rule for exactness and changing the machine; building the fan
   before it would repeat Run K's and this run's result at rebuild
   scale.
