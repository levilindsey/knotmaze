## The rules this stage builds

**Fragile** (`fragile`, `fg`: off | crack | snap; default off):
- A run's crossings count completed moves along it, advances and
  backtracks alike. Snap breaks a run after its first crossing; crack
  cracks it after the first and breaks it after the second. A run breaks
  behind the player, once the move completes.
- A run that ends at an END vertex never breaks. It can only be walked in
  and back out, so breaking it strands at once without a choice, and the
  start's and exit's runs are such runs (a catch respawns at the start).
- Breaks are derived from the taps, so `replay()` rebuilds them. `reset()`
  clears them; a respawn after a catch keeps them.
- `choices()` leaves broken runs out, and `tapEdge` refuses one with
  "reject" (the pick never offers one, so this is defence only).
- **Stranded**: after each move, a breadth-first search over unbroken
  runs looks for a route from the head to the exit. With none, the game
  is stranded: every tap is a noop, the thread sings a falling glissando,
  and a banner offers Undo and Restart.
- **Undo** is `replay(taps.slice(0, -1))`, then the fog restored the way
  a reload restores it (`exploreRuns`, gameplay-fog.md's K100 entry),
  with the clock left running. Offer it only when the controller's taps
  hold the whole state: with the minotaur or the fire on, the banner
  offers Restart only. Say which in the handoff.
- The minotaur and the fire ignore breaks in this stage (Out of scope).
- Keep the rule data-driven: a pure "crossings before this run breaks"
  per run (Infinity for exempt runs), so K139's collapse sets it to 1
  without new machinery.

**Par** (`parMode`, `pa`: off | strict | strokes; default off; and
`parSlack`, `par`: [0, 1], step 0.05, default from step 5):
- Fewest runs F: a breadth-first search over runs from the start to the
  exit. Par P = F + round(parSlack x F), fixed at the solve's start.
- Strokes = `taps.length`: every accepted tap, backtracks included (and a
  wait, if the turn minotaur records one as a tap).
- **Strict**: the game is lost the moment no finish fits in the strokes
  left, that is when the fewest unbroken runs from the head to the exit
  exceed P minus strokes. This includes reaching P short of the exit.
  The `fireLost` pattern: a `parLost` flag, every tap a noop, an "Out of
  strokes" banner, Try again restarts.
- **Strokes**: nothing is lost; the HUD and the share show strokes
  against par: "⛳ +2", "⛳ E", "⛳ −1".
- With fragile also on, the search skips broken runs.

## Steps

1. **Fragile, pure.**
   - A pure module, `src/game/fragile.ts`: the break rule, crossing
     counts from a walk of taps, and the strand search. The controller
     holds the state and calls it.
   - Controller: `fragile` in its options, `brokenRuns()`,
     `crackedRuns()`, `stranded`, the `choices()` and `tapEdge` changes,
     and the reset, respawn and replay rules above.
   - Tests in `test/game/fragile.test.ts` and
     `test/game/fabric_controller.test.ts`: snap and crack thresholds,
     the END-run exemption, a backtrack counts, `replay()` reproduces the
     breaks and a replay of a stranded game is stranded, reset clears and
     respawn keeps, and Undo's replay restores the move before.
   - **Commit.**
2. **Par, pure.**
   - `runsToExit(walk, blocked?)` in `walk_graph.ts`: fewest runs from
     every vertex to the exit, as a breadth-first search.
   - A pure `src/game/par.ts`: P, strokes left, the strict loss test.
   - Controller: `parMode` and `parSlack` in its options, `par`,
     `strokesLeft`, `parLost`.
   - Tests: the search against brute force on small graphs, P at slack 0
     and 0.25, strict loses exactly when no finish fits (one stroke
     early, at P, with a break in the way), strokes never loses.
   - **Commit.**
3. **The params, end to end.** All three in one pass through the
   hotspot:
   - `PARAM_DEFAULTS`, the reader, `CODEC_PARAMS` (append), and
     `LIVE_EFFECTS`' `onClockChange` row (a mid-solve change starts the
     solve over, like the fire's keys).
   - Panel rows in the Game folder, fabric only: "Fragile runs" and
     "Par", and "Par slack (x fewest)" hidden while par is off (the sync
     block's hidden-row pattern).
   - None is daily-exempt: a fragile or par game is an ordinary design,
     so Undo raises no record question.
   - Re-freeze `test/ui/param_codec.test.ts`'s key-order pin and
     `test/ui/url_codec.test.ts`'s defaults table, and name them in the
     commit.
   - **Commit.**
4. **The page.**
   - **Gone thread.** Broken runs vanish through `syncExplore`'s burning
     branch: subtract their spans in every case, whatever `burntLook`
     says, and keep the fire's spans as they are. Check that the explore
     programs are in K307's warm-up, so the first snap compiles nothing
     cold. The pick skips broken runs, and the `litArms` gate refuses
     their arms (a one-line predicate change there).
   - **Sound.** A snap is a sharp, short pluck at the run's own pitch
     raised an octave. A strand is a falling glissando of five or six
     plucks over about 0.6 s, on `when`. Both obey the volume and sound
     settings.
   - **Banners.** Give `LossBanner` a title and an optional second
     button rather than a new class: "Stranded" (Undo, Restart), "Out of
     strokes" (Try again). The Burnt banner stays as it is.
   - **HUD and share.** Rows "snapped N" and "par P · L left" (strict) or
     "strokes S / par P" (strokes), each with its `STATS` line. Share
     tags "snapped N" and "⛳ +N", beside `minotaurTag`.
   - **Commit.**
5. **The numbers** (headless, in `.claude/probes/K137/`).
   - On today's generator, 30 day seeds plus 20 free designs, with K155's
     player profiles: the strand rate under crack and under snap with the
     END-run exemption, against the design's 0 of 50 and 20 of 30.
   - The runs each profile uses against F, and the strict finish rate at
     slack 0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5 and 1.
   - Pick the default slack where K155's middle profile finishes strict
     about half the time and its best nearly always. Put the table in the
     handoff for Levi, and change the default in place.
   - **Commit.**
6. **Verify in the page**, at 1400x900 and 411x753, with a query (a bare

   - a strict par loss, and a strokes finish whose share reads "⛳ +N";
   - stills in `.claude/probes/K137/shots/`;
   - `npm test` and `npm run build` pass. Only the codec and defaults
     pins grow, by the appended keys. The golden dailies, K87 and the

7. **Probe hook and lore.**
   - `__knotFabric()` reports `broken`, `cracked`, `stranded`, `par`,
