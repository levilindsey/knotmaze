## Hypotheses (the coordinator's guesses: test them, don't assume them)

- 5: K268's dynamic gap for a stub's overlap (`docs/design/K268.md`)
  cuts the walked thread where the stub's hidden part would pass over
  it, so the cut shows with nothing above it.
- 4: a tap picks hidden thread (`tapPluck`), and the pluck overlay
  draws the plucked span without the reveal's mask.
- 1-3: a stub's end and knuckle are drawn by more than one thing (the
  explore program's end look, the frontier's beacon, a fan or a joint)
  that line up only sometimes.

### Fix plans

Lines are b50e4f8's. Issues 1, 2 and 5 touch `explore_glsl.ts`, which
K290 3b is rewriting, and issue 4's first option touches K395's files:
the fix stage starts after both merge and re-finds these lines.

**1 and 2: fit the dots to the fade.**
- Change: kExploreAt (`explore_glsl.ts` l.287-337) also exports the
  fade's end ctrl (kExEnd, beside kExSolid at l.284-289 and l.331-332),
  and the main look (l.516-524, where exS is computed) passes the fade's
  world length `Lf = abs(kArcU(kc, kExEnd) - kArcU(kc, kExSolid))` into
  kExploreLook (l.395, the dots at l.417-426). Then no dot may cross
  s = Lf. Two rules, for Levi to pick:
  - (a) keep the fixed pitch (0.95 tw) and drop any dot with
    s + r > Lf (or shrink its radius to Lf - s, never below a minimum):
    the count still varies with Lf, but never by a half dot;
  - (b) N = max(1, round(Lf / 0.95 tw)) dots spread evenly over the
    fade, s_k = (k - 0.5) Lf / N, radius capped at Lf / 2N: a constant
    look per stub, one dot at the shortest fades.
- CPU twin: kExploreLookCpu (`paint_math.ts` l.1485) and the cut's call
  into it (exploreCutCoverCpu, l.1529-1564). Tests:
  `test/render/explore_cut.test.ts` (the K76 2d High contrast dots,
  l.471; the K268 cover vs chainVis, l.333).
- Verify: rerun `survey.mjs` on the four seeds (41 frontiers): 0 cut
  dots (today 10), every dot whole in debug view 6, and the count the
  rule predicts; plus a same-state crop of 2gozcj f3 and f6 (`zoomcrops.mjs`).
- Moves: the explore variants' shader text (one cold compile, K307), the
  K76 2d census and K268's shaped-cut counts (the cut follows the dots).
  Not the golden snapshot (knot mode, no reveal) nor the dailies (the
  reveal is off by default).

**5: cut a dot's shape, not a casing-wide band.**
- Change: exploreCutCover (`explore_glsl.ts` l.435-463) evaluates the
  look at the under fragment's own offset from the over thread's
  centerline instead of lat 0 (l.461), and returns the dot or cap shape's
  coverage widened by the casing margin: the cut becomes a disc of
  radius r + casing around each visible dot (and the cap's outline),
  not a band across the whole casing. The fade and halo looks keep
