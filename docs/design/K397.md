## Hypotheses (the coordinator's guesses: test them, don't assume them)

- 5: K268's dynamic gap for a stub's overlap (`docs/design/K268.md`)
  cuts the walked thread where the stub's hidden part would pass over
  it, so the cut shows with nothing above it.
- 4: a tap picks hidden thread (`tapPluck`), and the pluck overlay
  draws the plucked span without the reveal's mask.
- 1-3: a stub's end and knuckle are drawn by more than one thing (the
  explore program's end look, the frontier's beacon, a fan or a joint)
  that line up only sometimes.

  dot 1 whole, dot 2 cut flat on the side facing away from the cap).

From the code (`explore_glsl.ts`):
- kExploreLook, l.417-426: the dots sit at a fixed WORLD pitch
  `sp = 0.95 tw` (l.419) from the solid end, the nearest one by
  `kk = max(1, floor(s / sp + 0.5))` (l.422), radius
  `0.3 tw (1 - 0.4 u)` (l.423), dimmed by `1 - 0.55 u^2` (l.426). The
  look never reads where the fade ends: it gets only u (the fade's
  ctrl progress) and s (world arc from the solid end).
- kExploreAt, l.318-334: past the fade's end t clamps to 1, v is 0 and
  the item loses the `v > best` test, so kExTip stays -1, ev is 0 and
  EXPLORE_MAIN_GLSL (l.539-540) discards: a hard cut across the thread
  at u = 1, through whatever dot straddles it.
- The fade is a lattice length (`explore_view.ts` stub, l.200-236:
  `fLen = min(fade, ext - d0)`, floored at FADE_MIN 0.35), so its world
  length is set per run by the relaxed material (and by short runs).

Inferred: Levi's "1, 2 or 1.5" dots (issue 1) and the "partially
squared-off artifact at the far end" (issue 2) are the same rule seen
at different Lf: issue 2 is a dot cut flat by the fade's end. Levi was
zoomed in (at the phone's fit view, 25 device px per world, a dot is
about 8 px across).

From the code: exploreCutCover (`explore_glsl.ts` l.435-463; CPU twin
`paint_math.ts` exploreCutCoverCpu, l.1529) evaluates kExploreLook at

the whole slot's cut by it. So every under pixel within the casing
whose projection falls inside a dot's centerline extent is cut as if
the over thread were there at full width: a casing-wide band per dot,
with nothing drawn over most of it. K268's lore says it outright: "dots
and beacon cut a casing per dot".

### 4: a tap keeps the whole tapped lobe, and the overlay ignores the reveal

Observed (`pluck_stub.mjs`, 2gozcj, at 200 device px per world, each
pluck through `__knotPluck`, the path a tap takes from `renderer.pluck`
on; shots at t 0.06 and 0.2 against the same frozen rest):
- f5 (chain 49, Lf 1.145). A tap in the fade (ctrl 9.11, vis 0.844) and
  a tap on the solid part (ctrl 8.066) both pluck the same tapped lobe,
  s -0.42 to 1.24 from the solid end: from a node before the fade to the
  next node, past the fade's end (1.145). The shown-span clip ends at s
  0.45 (0.42 for the solid tap), and vis is 0.5 at s 0.58. At t 0.06,
  88086 px differ from rest; along the stub, 41218 on the solid part,
  23651 in the fade's first half, 14028 in its second half, and they
  reach s 1.06. With the overlay hidden (its base collapse kept) only
  615 px differ, all in the fade: the collapse shrinks the beacon dots.
  The no-collapse run matches t 0.06. A control after the pluck cleared
  differs by 0 px.
- f12 (chain 85, Lf 1.123). A tap in the fade (ctrl 63.12) plucks the
  lobe s 0.22 to 1.30, from a node inside the fade to one past its end:
  25810 px in the fade's first half, 17200 in its second, to s 1.02. A
  tap on the solid part (ctrl 64.13) plucks the lobe that ENDS at that
  node (s 0.22): 6031 px in the fade, none past it.
- Visibility along f5: 0.6 at s 0.5, 0.47 at 0.6, 0.046 at 1.0, 0.005 at
  1.1. The overlay draws hidden thread at full opacity through all of it.
- Crops: `out/pluck/2gozcj_f5_fade25_sbs.png` and
  `out/pluck/2gozcj_f12_fade25_sbs.png` (five panels: rest, t 0.06, t
  0.2, overlay hidden, no collapse). Both show a translucent blur of
  hidden thread over the whole fade and both dots, ending in a straight,
  squared-off diagonal edge near dot 2.

From the code:
- The tapped lobe ignores the span. `pluck_model.ts` segmentLobes,
  l.728-818: the comment (l.725-727) and the loops (the forward break at
  l.811) apply `span` to the NEIGHBOUR lobes only; the tapped lobe k 0
  is always node to node (l.768-776). tapLobes (l.948-980) calls it,
  and PluckOverlay.add (`pluck_overlay.ts` l.950-1040) builds the
  lobes.
- `ribbon_renderer.ts` pluck, l.4340-4395: l.4367-4371 clip the unit to
  exploreShownSpan (l.5621-5659: 0.25-ctrl steps while vis >=
  EXPLORE_PICK_VIS, 0.5). That clip reaches segmentLobes as `span`, so
  it trims only the neighbours.
- The overlay's visibility is the host's `visibility`, which
  makePluckHost (l.4486) sets to underGapVisibility (l.2039-2063):
  crossing under-windows only, never the reveal. PluckOverlay.visAt
  (`pluck_overlay.ts` l.2029-2036) multiplies it by the end nodes' hide
  zones. The reveal reaches the overlay only for still plucks
  (setExplore, l.1250: the shaped crossing cut).
- What a tap may pick is already gated: pickThread (l.4207-4241) skips
  points under vis 0.5 (l.4236); main.ts tapPluck is l.3718-3746, and
  pluckUnit is l.1521-1529.

Inferred:
- "Sometimes" is where the tapped lobe's far node lies: past the fade's
  end, the overlay draws hidden thread out to it (f5, and f12's fade
  tap); at or before the fade, nothing extra shows (f12's solid tap).
- The overlay's far end is the under-node clip of the crossing at that
  node (PLUCK_FRAG's clipAt; its CPU mirror is clipKeep, `pluck_model.ts`
  l.902-927): a straight cut. While a
  pluck rings, that reads like issue 2's squared-off end, so issue 2 may
  have a second, transient source here.
