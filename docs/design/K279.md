# K279: plucks you can see, feel and hear

Levi asked on 2026-10-14 at about 16:11 and 16:12 (verbatim):

> I want to make plucks visualizations more emphasized. Can we add a new
> non-physics-based shock-wave/ripple sort of undulation sort of effect
> around pluck points? This should be made more subtle around autoplucks
> than player plucks. Also, I wonder if there is something else more we
> can do to show the plucked segment vibrating. Maybe add a bit of the
> physics-based tug to it as well,.and also increase the default visual
> vibration effect?

> Also, I want manual plucks to be louder than auto plucks

Read as six asks:
1. **A ripple**: a render-only shock wave that spreads from the pluck
   point and undulates what it passes.
2. **Subtler for auto plucks**: the random and music modes' plucks get a
   weaker ripple than the player's own.
3. **More ways to show the plucked segment vibrating.**
4. **A small physics tug** on the plucked segment, on top of today's
   render-only vibration.
5. **A stronger default vibration.**
6. **Manual plucks sound louder than auto plucks.** Manual means a tap
   or a drag's release.

Two stages build it:
- **Stage 1** (this brief): the design, a prototype of every ask behind
  params, and a sheet for Levi to pick from.
- **Stage 2**, after Levi picks:
  - finish the picked looks, with the CPU mirror and parity tests where
    a look needs them (K152 (3));
  - keep or drop the other looks as he says;
  - the phone pass.

## Stage 1: the prototypes (brief, 2026-10-14 16:20)

### The work

1. **Design first.** Before writing code, append a short "Stage 1 design"
   section to this doc and commit it. For each ask it gives:
   - the looks and their params;
   - your recommended defaults;
   - how each behaves under reduced motion (K79's Motion setting), High
     contrast (K111), the physics-off mode (K3) and maze mode.
2. **The ripple** (asks 1 and 2).
   - Build two or three looks as the options of one dropdown param: off
     plus the looks, with your recommendation as the default. Examples:
     a ring that expands from the pluck point; a displacement wave that
     bends the threads it passes, render-only.
   - Auto plucks get their own strength param, below the player's by
     default.
   - Keep it cheap when music mode fires many plucks: cap the live
     ripples and measure the frame cost.
3. **The vibrating segment** (asks 3 and 5).
   - Propose what else could show the segment vibrating, for example the
     ghost's strength, the glow, or a highlight along the string's swing.
   - Prototype the best one or two behind params.
   - Raise the default vibration and show it before and after. Levi
     picks the final number from the sheet.
4. **The tug** (ask 4). A small physics impulse on the plucked segment,
   behind a param whose 0 is today's render-only pluck.
   - Player plucks only by default, so the random and music modes don't
     keep the knot awake.
   - None in the physics-off mode.
   - After a tugged pluck, the knot sleeps again within the K63 window.
5. **The volume** (ask 6). Auto plucks play at a gain below a manual
   pluck's, behind a param. Default about half (-6 dB).

Each new param is a viewer preference:
