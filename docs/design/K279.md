# K279: plucks you can see, feel and hear

Levi asked on 2026-10-14 at about 16:11 and 16:12 (verbatim):

> I want to make plucks visualizations more emphasized. Can we add a new
> non-physics-based shock-wave/ripple sort of undulation sort of effect
> around pluck points? This should be made more subtle around autoplucks
> than player plucks. Also, I wonder if there is something else more we
> can do to show the plucked segment vibrating. Maybe add a bit of the
> physics-based tug to it as well,.and also increase the default visual
> vibration effect?

> Also, I want manual plucks to be louder than auto plucks

Read as six asks:
1. **A ripple**: a render-only shock wave that spreads from the pluck
   point and undulates what it passes.
2. **Subtler for auto plucks**: the random and music modes' plucks get a
   weaker ripple than the player's own.
3. **More ways to show the plucked segment vibrating.**
4. **A small physics tug** on the plucked segment, on top of today's
   render-only vibration.
5. **A stronger default vibration.**
6. **Manual plucks sound louder than auto plucks.** Manual means a tap
   or a drag's release.

Two stages build it:
- **Stage 1** (this brief): the design, a prototype of every ask behind
  params, and a sheet for Levi to pick from.
- **Stage 2**, after Levi picks:
  - finish the picked looks, with the CPU mirror and parity tests where
    a look needs them (K152 (3));
  - keep or drop the other looks as he says;
  - the phone pass.

## Stage 1 design (2026-10-14 16:30)

Every new param is a viewer preference: the panel's Touch & feel folder,
`DAILY_EXEMPT_KEYS`, codec appends. The appends sit at 121-127, after the
burn race's 118-120 (K114), since the rebase onto 61c8859.

| Param | Key | Values | Default | Neutral (= master) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pluckRipple` | `prp` | off, wave, glint, ring | wave | off |
| `rippleStrength` | `prs` | [0, 2], the player's ripple | 1 | - |
| `rippleAuto` | `pra` | [0, 2], auto plucks' ripple | 0.35 | - |
| `pluckSheen` | `psh` | [0, 1] | from the sheet | 0 |
| `pluckTug` | `ptg` | [0, 1] | 0.3 (0 since the wake follow-up) | 0 |
| `tugAuto` | `pta` | on/off: auto plucks tug too | off | off |
| `autoPluckGain` | `pag` | [0, 1], auto plucks' volume | 0.5 (-6 dB) | 1 |

**Ripple (asks 1, 2).** One pixi Filter on the ribbon root (ribbons and
pluck strips; never the background, markers, fog or HUD). It attaches
only while a ripple lives, so off, or nothing live, renders exactly as
master. Its filter area is the union of the live ripples' discs, and one
pass handles up to 8 live ripples (a 9th evicts the oldest), so music
mode's bursts cost one pass. Each ripple starts at the pluck point (a
tap's point; an auto pluck's site, at its sound's delay), and its band
eases out to about 4 world units in 0.9 s while it fades.
- **wave** (recommended): render-only displacement. The band bends what
  it crosses radially (at most about a third of a thread width at
  strength 1) with a faint brightening on its crest.
- **glint**: no bending; the band brightens the threads it crosses, a
  light sweeping outward along them.
- **ring**: a soft translucent ring over the threads (alpha at most
  about 0.3), the classic shock-wave ring.
- Auto plucks scale by `rippleAuto` (and their own strength), the
  player's by `rippleStrength`; 0 makes no ripple.

**Vibrating segment (asks 3, 5).** Candidates: the ghost's strength
(`pluckGhost`), the size (`pluckSize`, though a tapped lobe is usually
clearance-capped, so it reads less), the whole-string sway
(`pluckWhole`), the existing glow (`pluckGlow` = with), and a new
**sheen**: bright bands at the time exposure's turning points, where a
real vibrating string spends most of its time and a long-exposure photo
of one shows bright edges. `pluckSheen` scales it; 0 is the old output
exactly. The raised default vibration is picked from the sheet (before =
1.75 / 0.35 / 0.5 for size / ghost / whole; the "after" candidates raise
the ghost and the sway first).

**Tug (ask 4).** `pluckTug` scales a displace-and-release on the plucked
lobe: the particles between the crossings around the pluck point (at
most about 6 ctrl each way) move toward the pluck's side by
A sin(pi u), positions and previous positions together (no injected
velocity), A at most about one thread width; then the sim wakes. Springs
and bending pull it back, so it reads as the thread twitching under the
finger. Player plucks only (taps and string-style releases) unless
`tugAuto` is on, so random and music plucks never wake the knot. The
knot must sleep again within the K63 window; measured on three designs.

**Volume (ask 6).** Auto plucks (random and music modes) play through
their own gain node at `autoPluckGain`; 0.5 is -6 dB, and 1 is master's
sound exactly. Their strength, which also sets brightness, is unchanged,
so only the level moves. Taps (0.55) and releases are unchanged.

**Modes.**
- Reduced motion (K79): no ripple (the still pluck's brightening, K104,
  stays the cue), no sheen (a still pluck has no time exposure), no tug.
- High contrast (K111): no change in stage 1. Stage 2 may tie ripple and
  sheen to it (for example, ring off).
- Physics off (K3): ripple, sheen and volume as usual; no tug (K3's
  physics-off pluck is pluck only).
- Maze mode: the same as knot mode. Markers and fog sit outside the
  filtered root, so the wave never moves them, and a pluck silenced in a
  junction core (K235) makes no ripple, since nothing plucked.
- Flash safety: the ring and glint stay at or under about 0.3 alpha,
  auto ripples weaker still. Stage 2 can add an area guard like
  `GHOST_AREA_PX` if the phone shows a need.

## Wake follow-up (2026-10-14 18:10)

The coordinator's question before shipping `ptg` 0.3: is the 4-10 fps
after a tap the probe window, a wake-path bug unique to the tug (a large
first dt after sleep, say), or the physics' real cost at these sizes?

### Setup

- `k279_wake.mjs` on 42a1684 (stage 1 rebased onto 61c8859), served on
  port 5743, under the perf lock (17:59-18:02).
- One headed off-screen window, 1400x900 at DSF 2 (canvas 2800x1800), RX
  6800 XT through ANGLE D3D11, the occlusion-safe flags and its own
  profile (deleted after). CPU at 3-5% of 24 cores mid-probe. No context
  loss, no page errors.
- Each design loads and sleeps, then wakes from that sleeping state
  three ways, twice each, each timed to the next sleep:
  - TAP: a real click on a node-to-node span, at ptg 0.3;
  - DRAG: a real press, 6 px past the slop then 10 px, held 120 ms,
    released;
  - BARE: `__knotSetPhysicsParam("slack", <its own value>)`, which wakes
    the knot without touching a particle.
- Per frame, from inside the page: the rAF interval, pixi's tick, ps.step's
  ms, dt and substeps, and renderer.update's ms.

### The answer

- **Not the window.** Asleep, the same window drew 17-18 ms frames (p95
  18 ms; dense 36 ms) with the CPU idle.
- **Not a wake-path bug.** Tap, drag and bare wakes cost the same per
  frame and per substep. The first awake frame of every wake steps a
  normal dt of 17-18 ms (2 substeps, 3 in one dense drag).
- **The physics' real cost.** One 1/120 s substep costs 8.2 ms (knot),
  10.5 ms (fabric) and 20-23 ms (dense), at or past the 8.33 ms real
  time allows. Real-time stepping then spirals: dt climbs 17 -> 35 ->
  53 -> 71 -> 89 ms to pixi's 100 ms cap within 2-6 frames, and every
  frame after that steps 12 substeps. A drag runs as slowly during its
  own gesture.
- Two smaller observations:
  - In dense the taps ran 6% and 19% slower than the drags (physics 4%
    and 13% a substep). Knot and fabric show no difference.
  - Once per page, the first tap's first frame spends 30-36 ms more in
    pixi's tick than a later tap's (knot 67 vs 31 ms, fabric 60 vs 38,
    dense 104 vs 72), so that wake reaches the cap a frame or two
    sooner. Probably the ripple filter's first compile (not tested).
