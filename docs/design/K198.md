# K198: the fit camera's pan, stage 1 (the pan clamp)

Levi, 2026-10-14 about 07:53, on K149's pan slack: "camera: I just want
to be able to pan such that the right edge of the maze is shown to the
left of the left edge of the panel. ... I guess we should prevent the
excess pan." K149 (merged 2026-10-14) let the zoomed fit frame overhang
every edge of the screen by 0.3 of the screen's size (`PAN_SLACK`), at
every zoom. This stage replaces that slack with the one overhang he asked

"The maze" below means whatever the fit camera frames: the maze, or the

## Goal

- **Panel open:** the horizontal pan may go left until the maze's drawn
  right edge (thread width included) sits a small gap left of the open
  panel's left edge. Start with the panel's own 10 px margin. Every part
  of the maze can then be brought out from under the panel, at every
  zoom. Nothing more: no further left than that, no further right than
  before K149, and no vertical slack.
- **Panel closed (collapsed), or a maze that already clears the open
  panel:** no slack, the rule from before K149. The zoomed fit frame
  covers the screen: pan in [size (1 - zoom), 0] per axis, so zoom 1 has
  no pan range.
- As a formula, with the fit view screen = zoom x fitScreen + pan:
  panX in [min(W (1 - zoom), panelLeft - gap - zoom x mazeRight), 0],
  where mazeRight is the maze's right edge in fit-screen coordinates, and
  panY in [H (1 - zoom), 0]. The clamp stays pure and tested in
  `src/ui/view_clamp.ts`; main.ts supplies the panel's state and rect and
  the maze's right edge.
- **The panel's state changes the range.** Opening the panel only widens
  it, so opening never moves the view. Closing it, or a resize, can leave
  the view out of range: ease it back in (like the reset, `viewHoming`),
  never snap, and don't leave it out of range until the next gesture
  (whose first frame would snap). Record the call you make.
- The phone (411x753): the open panel covers x 81-401 and the panel
  starts closed. The rule is the same, so with the panel open the maze
  can be panned until its right edge is at x 81 minus the gap.

## What must not change

- Home (zoom 1, pan 0) and its reset: a double-click or double-tap on
  empty space eases home, waiting for the pan as well as the zoom.
- Zoom about a point (pinch, wheel) keeps the point under the cursor.
  Zooming back out ends in range; with the panel closed, zoom 1 is a
  single point again, as before K149 (say whether that reads as a jump).
- The FOLLOW camera: no pan clamp (placeWorldAt clamps only its zoom),
  and it returns to the head 2.2 s after a gesture.
- The off-screen indicators place themselves against the chrome whatever
  the pan.

### The calls

- **The gap:** 10 px (`PANEL_GAP`), the panel's own margin from the
  screen's edge, as the brief suggested.
- **The maze's right edge:** the further of the threads' edge (particle
  maxX + threadWidth / 2) and a shown start, head or exit disc. The
  default `se` exit's disc pokes 10-14 px past the threads, so a
  threads-only edge let it touch the panel. Under fog, the fog box's
  right edge, which is what the fit frames. Knot mode: the threads. Not
  counted: the win ring (about 5 px past the disc, inside the gap), the
  head's glow and the minotaur.
- **The panel:** the Tweakpane wrapper's rect, closed when its width is
  0. Its height is ignored.
- **Closing, a resize and fit drift:** a memoryless allowance per side.
  Each fit-mode frame keeps 0.85 of how far the view is out of range
  (RANGE_EASE 0.15, the reset's rate), and drops it below 0.5 px.
  Gestures clamp to the range plus the allowance, so they are held
  outward and free inward, and nothing snaps. A resize holds its first
  frame (rate 0), then eases. A widening (opening the panel, a creep
  that grows the range) leaves the view where it is. Rejected: easing
  the view itself toward the clamped view each frame, because a gesture
  mid-ease would snap to the clamp.
- **Zooming out with the panel closed:** each wheel step slides the view
  in a little (64 px a 0.9x step at 1000x800, the last 45 px), as before
  K149. That reads as a continuous slide, not a jump. Homing stays in
  range: each frame the reset scales zoom - 1 and the pan by the same
  factor, and neither limit tightens faster than that.
