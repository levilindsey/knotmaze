# K104 brief: plucks you can see at a glance

## Why

Levi, 2026-10-13: "the pluck is still not good, in general. The main
problem is that it's way too subtle. Even maxing pluckSize to 3, I can't
tell when some segments are plucked. Can we modify this to include a
set-amount of whole-segment vibration effect? I do like the spatial
locality emphasis too though, but this should build on the rest. And,
as to the requested problem, the sharp kink collapse is better, but
still a bit too acute."

The pluck is render-only (K77): a time-exposure strip plus a collapse
of the base ribbon, in `src/render/pluck_overlay.ts` with the string
math in `src/render/pluck_model.ts`. K77's before/after sheet:
`.claude/probes/K77/K77_before_after.png` in the main checkout.

## Goals

1. Every pluck is plainly visible at the default zoom and pluckSize, on
   desktop and on a phone viewport. The whole plucked segment vibrates
   by a set amount from one end to the other, and today's local lobes
   stay and ride on top of it.
2. The collapse's bends are gentler. `COLLAPSE_ROUND` (1 h,
   `pluck_overlay.ts:145`) rounds the narrowed edge; K77's A/B found
   that 0.5 h left shoulders and 2 h erased most of the thinning.
3. Reduced motion (the Motion setting, `src/ui/motion.ts`) gets its own
   version: the pluck shows without displacement (for example a brief
   brightening of the segment), and the sound is unchanged.
4. K105 folds in: a pluck's sympathetic lobes scale from the tapped
   lobe's capped amplitude, not the pre-cap one (the
   `sympatheticAmplitude` callers, ~653), so a neighbor never out-swings
   the lobe that was tapped.

## Approach

1. Diagnose first. Pluck every segment of two or three designs (a
   default fabric design, a dense one, knot mode) and measure each
   pluck's peak visible displacement in CSS px at the default zoom, on
   desktop and on a 412x915 phone viewport. Find what hides the faint
   ones. K77 found the tapped lobe clearance-capped in 74 of 80 fabric
   taps, so the cap is the first suspect; segment length, the amplitude
   floor and zoom are the others.
2. Add the whole-segment term: the segment's fundamental (sin(pi s/L)
   between its two anchors) at a set amplitude in half-widths, scaled by
   pluckSize, under the same decay. If the clearance cap is what hides
   plucks, the set amount can't simply be capped the same way. Show the
   options with crops (a bounded overlap with neighbors during the ring,
   drawing the term's time exposure under the neighbors, a
   non-displacement cue on top), pick one, and list it as a decision for
   Levi.
3. A/B `COLLAPSE_ROUND` at 1, 1.25, 1.5 and 1.75 h on K77's kink sites,
   with K77's worst-second-difference metric and crops, and pick one.
4. K105, then the reduced-motion version.

No codec params: K79 stage B is appending codec params now. If a slider
would help (the whole-segment amplitude, say), put it in the report's
ledger items.

## Goal 1 design (stage 1b, 2026-10-13)

DIAGNOSIS (census smoke, `census/census_smoke.md`, master c521f23, full
motion): peaks are 2-3 CSS px (p10 0.36-0.54 h); the tapped lobe is
clearance-capped in 90-100% of taps, and the binder is almost always the
CROSSING PARTNER through the lobe's own end node, at capClearance's end
stations (x = 0.2 / 0.8, ~1.1 h from the node), already inside 2 h, so the
cap sits on its floor 0.35 h / shape (room ~0.37 h). pluckSize cannot
raise it, which is why 3 looks like 1.75. A lateral swing that close to a
crossing moves along the partner, which rules 3-4 already handle, so that
partner is a false neighbor; the cone rule only skips its points nearer
the node than the station.

THE WHOLE-SEGMENT TERM W (pure math in pluck_model, unit-tested):
- Span: the unit's vibrating extent [E0, E1] = min e0 .. max e1 over its
  drawn lobes (fabric: the walk run between its junctions' hide zones, or
  the +-6-lobe cap; knot: the lobe +-2 unit; pluckReach "lobe": the lobe),
  re-derived in every layout. Shape sin(pi (s - E0) / (E1 - E0)).
- Set amount: AW0 = WHOLE_AMP x pluckSize x gain x h, independent of
  length (Levi: "a set amount"); reduced motion x0.6 like the lobes until
  stage 2. Start with WHOLE_AMP 0.5 (0.875 h at the default 1.75, just
  under NODE_CORE 0.9 h; 1.5 h at 3) and pick from crops of 0.35/0.5/0.7.
- Same clock: e^(-t/tau) from onset 0, a lens (q = 0, no attack double
  image), its own ghost phase. KILL_PX counts it. A re-pluck keeps
  max(new AW0, the absorbed unit's current W).
- Plumbing (the handoff's four places): per station rawSum = lobe peak +
  W peak; the soft-node / tight-bend cap applies to the SUM (peakAt's
  softMin), and one factor f = pk / rawSum scales both terms, so pkOf,
  the strip extents, the concave caps and the collapse need no new logic.
  writeFrame: [lo, hi] = lobe envelope + [-w, w], w = aW f sin(..); the
  shader stays as is (the sum's dwell read as one arcsine over the
  combined range); the ghost adds w cos(ghostPhase + phaseW).

DECISIONS TO CROP (Approach step 2; same-state crops, full motion, t =
60 and 300 ms, pluckSize 1.75 and 3, desktop): a dense-fabric tap with a
parallel neighbor, an over-crossing and an under-crossing inside a run, a
run end at a junction, a knot lobe.
1. Interior crossings. W rides through them (the brief's "from one end to
   the other"); the static footprint patch then shows as a solid core
   inside the blur wherever W > ~0.3 h there, since the base is never
   narrowed in footprints (rule 2: over-fill windows would keep drawing
   it anyway). Crop "ride" (W capped only by NODE_CORE at footprints)
   against "pinch" (W soft-capped near ~0.3 h at interior footprints) and
   pick; rules 1-4 stay as they are in both.
2. Neighbors (the brief's three options), as a live `wholeMode` field like
   `collapseRound`: "overlap" (proposed default: W's own per-station cap
   that lets it run into a NON-partner neighbor's edge by up to ~0.6 h,
   only at stations past 2 h + 0.1 from their nodes, slope-limited like
   CAP_SLOPE, never below a floor of ~0.5 h), "free" (no cap, to show what
   the cap buys), "under" (probe-only: the overlay layer reparented under
   the ribbons; expect a halo with the static base reading through), and
   "cue" (no W displacement; the segment brightens toward white under the
   same decay; stage 2's reduced-motion version can reuse it).
3. Optional, if crops show the lobes still vanish: skip the end node's
   partner for stations within its CLIP_REACH in capClearance (lobes then
   reach their tapAmplitude); report it either way.

The pick (mode, WHOLE_AMP, ride vs pinch) goes to Levi as a decision; a
`pluckWhole` slider is a proposal for the ledger, not a codec param now.
