## The seven new params (design 4.2)

| Param (key) | Default | Range | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| minoStopMin (`mhn`) | 0.2 | [0, 2] | "Junction stop, shortest (s)" |
| minoStopMax (`mhx`) | 0.6 | [0, 2] | "Junction stop, longest (s)" |
| minoHuntStop (`mhh`) | 0.5 | [0, 1] | "Junction stop while hunting (x)" |
| minoTurnBack (`mrv`) | 1.5 | [0, 10] | "Turn-back margin" (was `REVERSE_MARGIN`) |
| minoTipReach (`mdr`) | 2 | [0.5, 4] | "Dead-end reach (x roam range)" |
| minoRoamNear (`mnr`) | 0.25 | [0, 1] | "Nearest roam (x roam range)" |
| minoTelegraphRuns (`mtn`) | 1 | [0, 3], step 1 | "Telegraph reach (runs)" |

- The keys were free in `src` at f68ee4b. Check them again before you
  append.
- The reader clamps the stop max to at least the min.
- None of them is daily-exempt or a maze key, so the golden dailies
  don't move.
- The rows show only with Real time on.
- `mtn` only feeds `plan(reach)`, which stage 2 draws.

## Steps

1. **Baseline.** Run the K80 sim harness at today's defaults on H1
   mazes, and record:
   - the greedy player's catches a game at pull 0.5 (1.66 when last
     measured);
   - the fleers' escapes (54-58%, or 75-77% with queued taps);
   - the still-player ratio (0.67 against a bound of 0.8);
   - the distribution of run lengths (section 3.4 assumed 5 units).
2. **Commit 1: the machinery, at zero** (design 3.3, item 1).
   - The `hold` tick counter (3.2), with its zero-tick rule, cleared by a
     respawn, a catch and `place()`.
   - The stop lengths come from a counter-based stream on the seed and
     the arrival count. They never draw from the sequential `rng`.
   - `roamEdgeFrom(v)`, `huntEdgeFrom(v)` and the reverse predicate are
     pure, and the chase calls them.
   - `plan(reach): ChasePlan` (2.2) is read-only and draws nothing.
   - Add the seven params end to end: `ChaseParams`, `chaseParamsOf`
     and the clamps, the codec append, the tuning rows, and
     `LIVE_EFFECTS` under the callback their rows run.
   - In this commit the stops default to 0 and pick-on-arrival is off.
     `K80_DIGESTS` must pass unchanged.
3. **Commit 2: pick on arrival and the stop defaults** (2.3 and 3.3,
   item 2).
   - Turn pick-on-arrival on, with no switch for it.
   - Set the stops to 0.2 / 0.6 / 0.5.
   - Re-freeze `K80_DIGESTS` and name it in the commit.
4. **The re-tune** (3.4).
   - With the stops on, sweep `minoHuntPace` from 0.7 to 0.9 and pick the
     value that keeps the baseline's rates.
   - Change the default in place and re-freeze whatever pins it moves.
   - If nothing in 0.7-0.9 restores the rates, report the table and
     leave the default as it is.
5. **Tests**, in `test/game/minotaur.test.ts`, the list in section 6:
   - every junction arrival holds within its range, and never at a tip;
   - a long stop replaces the junction stop;
   - a zero stop costs no tick;
   - spotting you keeps a junction stop but cuts a long one;
   - `replayChase` reproduces a chase with stops;
   - a roaming plan equals the runs it then walks when nothing
     intervenes;
   - a hunting plan's next run equals `huntEdge`'s choice for a player
     who stands still.

   Also update the codec and defaults pins.
6. **Probe hook.** `__knotChase()` reports `hold` (and the plan, if that
   costs a line).
   - Make this edit last, after rebasing on master: K578 also edits
     `probe_hooks.ts`.
   - In a browser, check a real-time chase on a fabric link: `hold` is
     above 0 at junctions and stays 0 at tips.
7. **Lore and handoff.**
   - Put the chosen defaults and the re-tune table in
     `gameplay-minotaur.md`, the codec rows in `gameplay-links.md`, and
     the rows in `gameplay-panel.md`.
   - Add one line in the design's section 8 that points at this brief's
     Handoff.
8. **Verify.** `npm test` and `npm run build` pass. The golden dailies,
   K87 and the byte_identity golden hold. `TURN_GAMES` and
   `LENGTH_GAMES` hold too, because this stage leaves the turn clock
   alone.
