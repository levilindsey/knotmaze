How it plays:
- It stops at every junction it reaches and never at a dead-end tip:
  0.2-0.6 s while it roams, half that while it hunts.
- A long stop (`pauseChance`) replaces the junction stop. Spotting you
  never cuts a junction stop short, but still cuts a long one (q4).
- A roaming leg picks its next destination as it arrives, before the
  stop, so `plan()` can show it during the stop.
- `plan(reach)` is read-only and capped at `PLAN_REACH_MAX` 8 runs. It
  returns `{lead, steps: [edge, farVertex][], dest, hunting}`, and it is
  empty in the lair until the last `LAIR_TELL` 0.5 s.
