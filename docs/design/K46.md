# K46 brief: a proposal for daily variety

Levi, 2026-10-12: the daily "should vary, but with some constraints on
which settings values are good. Please put together a proposal. I'll
likely want to iterate on this."

Deliverable: a PROPOSAL, `docs/design/K46_daily_variety.md`, backed by
measurements. Don't change `src/`; the implementation (`DAILY_V2`) comes
after Levi iterates. Context: the daily shipped in 3b3e028 (K20). Every
day is currently `DAILY_V1`: codec v3 defaults plus a seed derived from
the day number (`src/ui/daily_link.ts`). Since K56, a bare URL opens
today's daily, so the daily is the front door.

## What the proposal covers

1. **Dimensions that could vary,** each with its allowed values or
   ranges and the reason:
   - structure: shape, gridSize, weaveDensity, turnBias, branchDensity,
     fourWayFraction, dead ends and their density, start and exit
     anchors;
   - look: colorScheme, palette, hue and gamut, threadWidth, gap,
     cuspRadius, junctionBlend, the appearMode preset, the background
     effect;
   - play modifiers: fog of war, pathViz trail or string, the camera.

   Name the ones that should stay fixed and why. For example, moveSpeed
   affects the timer, so it should be identical every day.
2. **Constraints from known problems.** Read LEDGER.md residuals and
   CLAUDE.md:
   - K16: the slow settle at large grids, especially on phones;
   - K29/K30: wide threads (tw >= 0.72 never sleeps);
   - K11: ring plus gridAlign;
   - K12: star e-w stragglers;
   - shape minimum sizes (the tree reads best at gs 16+);
   - the shaped start/exit notes.

   Also cover combinations that clash, such as fog with trail mode, or
   very dense weaves on small phones.
3. **A rhythm, if it earns its place:** e.g. a weekly difficulty ramp,
   themed days, or shapes on weekends. Offer 2-3 alternatives with
   trade-offs, plus a recommendation.
4. **The selection algorithm:** deterministic from the day number alone,
   with recipe tables frozen in `DAILY_V2` so everyone gets the identical
   maze. It should include a validation step with deterministic rerolls
   (an optimal path-length band, a junction count, a settle-time budget,
   sleep reached) so no day is broken or wildly off-difficulty. Say how
   V2 starts on a future day without changing past dailies.
5. **Evidence:** extend `.claude/probes/K19-K20/scan_dailies.ts` (or the
   vite-node physics harness in `.claude/probes/K41/`). Scan candidate
   recipes across many seeds for maze metrics, sim time to sleep and the
   physics cost per step. Use the data to set the "good value"
   boundaries; the K41 harness reproduces browser physics exactly.
6. **A sample calendar:** the next 14 days under the recommended recipe,
   with each day's settings and a screenshot contact sheet (headed
   off-screen Chrome, real time, `?<token>&am=instant&snd=none` links).
   Levi will judge the variety by eye.
7. **Open questions for Levi,** short and concrete.

## Conventions

- Keep the probes and contact sheets in `.claude/probes/K46/`.
- Commit the proposal doc on a worktree branch; don't push. The
  coordinator lands it and brings it to Levi.
- Don't edit LEDGER.md: end the report with a short summary of the
  recommendation, the open questions and the doc's path.
