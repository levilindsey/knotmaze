## Questions (recommendations stated; the stage takes them)

1. **probeTris / paintMirror**: with reveal or with paint? Their
   callers are the paint parity tests and the probes; the mirror reads
   reveal state (`revealMulAtCtrl`, the gate clocks) and paint state
   both. Rec: they move with **paint_state** (their home in the parity
   tests' imports is the paint surface) and read reveal through the
   façade's `reveal` part, like every other cross-part read.
2. **The reveal gate in update()'s gap pass**: `update()` interleaves
   the gate with `computeGapSlot` (why R2 landed no `solve()`,
   `K5_K6_split.md:415-417`). Rec: the gate's *state* (gateLatch,
   tShow computation) moves to reveal_fx as `gateFor(c, ...)`-shaped
   readers; `update()` keeps calling the façade's one-line delegations,
   so the interleaving — and the byte identity — does not move.
3. **paintClock / pulseBorn accessors**: the design says façade
   accessors for both (2.3's table). Rec: `paintClock` keeps its
   `@internal ribbon` façade field as a getter/setter onto the part
   (probes and parts read `r.paintClock` today); `pulseBorn` becomes a
   façade getter. No call site changes shape.
4. **Order**: reveal_fx first, or paint_state first? Reveal reads
   paint uniforms (`paintScene`) but not paint state; paint reads the
   reveal animator (`tickPaint`'s `uKLook` line, `recolor`). Rec:
   **reveal_fx first** (its surface is smaller and self-contained),
   verified alone, then paint_state reads `r.reveal` the same way.

## Process

As R2/R5: bodies move **verbatim**, changed in three ways only — a
renderer member becomes `this.r.x`, a sampling helper `sampling.x(...)`,
and probed names keep one-line façade delegations the parts call
through the instance. A part never imports a value from
`ribbon_renderer.ts` (constants come from `paint_const.ts` or the
part's own exports).

Verification per file: `npx tsc --noEmit`; the paint parity suites
(`test/render/paint_*.test.ts`, the frozen-fixture mirrors), the reveal
and explore suites, `test/physics/byte_identity.test.ts`; the full
`npm test`; `npm run build`; and a page pass (a reveal link + a paint
link on the preview build, shots under `.claude/probes/K5/R3/`).
Nothing about behaviour, pins or digests may move: any test that goes
red is the move's bug, not a pin to update.
