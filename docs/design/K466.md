## Today

Under the thread reveal (`xp=threads`), each junction the player has
reached shows its arms for `revealDistance` (2.5 lattice units), and each
arm fades out over `revealFade` (1) into a frontier drawn in `revealLook`.
The default look is beacon: a cap and two dots sized to fit the fade,
plus a halo that breathes between 0.6 and 0.85 over 2.5 s (held at 0.8
under Reduced motion, drawn at 1 in white under High contrast).

When a frontier's fade lies under a visible over thread, the over pass
draws on top and hides the cap and dots, so the end reads as no end.
K459 (merged just before you) may have found this: its diagnosis of the
truncated dots (1x2y3z f1 and f6, "the dots end at about 0.85 of the
fade") is in the "Handoff" of `docs/design/K459.md`. Read that first.

Lore: `.claude/rules/render-explore.md`: the model and pieces (stage 2a),
the gates (2b), "In the game" (the halo's breathing), THE SHAPED CUT, and
THE DOTS.
