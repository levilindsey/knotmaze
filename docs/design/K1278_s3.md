## Why

Levi (2026-11-10): "seamless continuous perfectly smooth blending across junctions for segment color
blending and highlight pulses". K1278's seam test (`k1278s2_seam.ts --hist --fbstep`) measures the
outline's own paint pass (`draw.ts`, the prototype palette) with no pulses. What players see in
`rp=outline` is a different path: since K1291 s1 the app's fill program (`outline_app.ts` APP_FS) runs
today's paint per fragment (kSectionAtArc, kPaintAt, kComposePaintFlat, kGlow from paint_model's
textures) at each fill vertex's chain and arc, through `paint.ts`'s `appRunMap` / `chainArcMap`. That path
has never had a seam test, and pulses (the choice arms, the head's run) have only been checked at one
pinned clock. `.claude/rules/probes.md` warns: shoot two pulse phases (t 100 and 100.45), because at one
phase a run can have no crest (K299).

## Goal

In `rp=outline`, at rest: section colour and pulses are continuous across every face edge, triangle edge
and junction, with no step or kink that today's look doesn't have at the same place. Pulses carry their
travel through a junction as today's do. Today's look is the approved one for the paint (form B for the ink):
judge against it. The technique is yours.
