## The work

1. **The shine,** as `K335_confetti.md` item 4 specifies, on the rain
   and the finish spray:
   - Give each piece a unit normal `n` from its spin in the screen's
     plane and its flip about its long axis. A piece showing its back
     flips `n`, so both faces light.
   - Put one directional light at the upper left, toward the viewer.
     The eye looks down the screen's normal, `V` = (0, 0, 1).
   - Shade = ambient + diffuse·max(0, n·L), which replaces today's
     `0.55 + 0.45·w`, plus a specular flash (n·H)^p with
     H = normalize(L + V), mixed toward white.
   - Use two materials. Paper has a low `p` and a soft sheen. Foil has
     a high `p` and a bright, short flash. Each piece draws its
     material from a foil share.
   - Compute the shine on the CPU, per piece and per frame, into the
     tint the view already sets. Do not add a shader program: a new one
     compiles cold at the finish and freezes the win (K307,
     `.claude/rules/render-shaders.md`).
2. **Make it read.** Levi wants it visible. At the default:
   - a foil piece flashes close to white as it turns through the light;
   - a paper piece shows a soft sheen;
   - each flash is brief, so only a few pieces flash at any moment.
   Choose the defaults (strength, foil share, the two exponents, the
   light's direction) from frame sheets. Put two or three alternative
   strengths on a sheet so Levi can make the look call.
3. **Settings.** Add two rows to the confetti's panel folder:
   - the shine's strength (`confettiShine`, codec `cfh`);
   - the foil share (`foilShare`, codec `cff`), shown only while the
     strength is above 0. Use the panel's hidden-row pattern if one
     exists.
   Append both codec entries after the last index. An append does not
   bump `CODEC_VERSION`. Add both keys to `DAILY_EXEMPT_KEYS`
   (`src/ui/daily_link.ts`) as viewer preferences, like `cf` and `cfa`.
4. **Reduced motion.** Keep the diffuse term and drop the specular
   flashes, since a flash is what reduced motion avoids.
5. **Lives.** Leave every piece's life as it is, and
   `sceneAnimating()` as it is.

**What changed**

- `src/render/confetti_model.ts`. One directional light: LIGHT =
  normalize(-0.5, -0.62, 0.6) (upper left, toward the viewer), V =
  (0, 0, 1), H = normalize(L + V).
  - A piece's normal comes from its spin and flip, n = (sf sin rot,
    -sf cos rot, cf), and flips on the back face.
  - The tint is `litHex(color, k, s)`. k = 0.5 + (0.5 / Lz) max(0, n.L)
    replaces K140's 0.55 + 0.45 |cos flip|, so a face-on piece shows its
    own color. s = strength x peak x (n.H)^p, at most 1, mixes the tint
    toward white.
  - Paper has peak 0.3 and p 8; foil has peak 1 and p 64.
  - Each piece draws its material at spawn (pool field MAT 17, stride

    throws master's exact pieces.
  - `confettiPlan(amount, reduced, w, h, shine, foil)` carries both

    newest burst's strength.
  - `specular` and `shadeNormal` are the reference. pieceLook takes a

**Defaults, and what chose them**

The light, the exponents and the peaks came from the read census
(`.claude/probes/K337/census.mjs`: the pure model, amount 1, share 0.3,
six seeds). A flash is a foil specular above 0.5, near white is above
0.8, and paper sheen is above 0.15.

| Measure | Phone 411x753 | Desktop 1400x900 |
|---|---|---|
| Foil time spent flashing | 1.55% | not recorded |
| Foil time near white | 0.49% | not recorded |
| Flashing pieces a frame | median 2, p90 4, max 9 | median 2, p90 6, max 13 |
| Flash length | median 2 frames, max 4 | not recorded |
| Foil pieces that flash at least once | 81% | not recorded |
| Paper time in sheen | 13.8% | not recorded |

Foil p 32 doubled the flashing time (3.1%) and p 128 halved it (0.77%).
Those two are census numbers only.

`.claude/probes/K337/sheet/`, each at 1400x900 and 411x753:
