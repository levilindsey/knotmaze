The lesson: the tie window is what keeps the bar elsewhere. Narrowing it everywhere trades two smudges for 20-30 crisp
wedges. A fix has to tell a smudge's tie apart from the window's useful blend. The likely discriminator is two
members of one surface at equal depth with the same chain order (the smear) against two surfaces crossing (the
blend). I have not built or measured that.
