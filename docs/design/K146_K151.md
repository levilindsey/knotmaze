# K146 + K151: past dailies without credit, and the mode badge

Two small daily-side changes from Levi's notes of 2026-10-13 (verbatim in
`docs/design/levi_notes_2026-10-13.md`, "About 16:15 (past dailies)",
"About 16:25 (past dailies, the way in)" and "About 16:30 (the mode
badge)"). One Opus agent, one stage, two parts. Both touch the mode badge
(`src/ui/mode_badge.ts`, `modeBadgeFor`), which is why one agent does both.

## Part A (K146): past dailies, playable without credit

Levi: "There should be some way to play previous dailys, but not get
credit for them." Then: "Stats view is enough of a way in."

Today a past daily opens only by its link (`?daily=<n>`): the stats
view's History lists only days with a record
(`src/ui/daily_stats_view.ts`), so a missed day has no way in. And a late
first finish becomes the OFFICIAL result, counted in solved and best time
but not in streaks (`onTime`, `src/game/daily_store.ts`).

1. The way in: the stats view's History lists every past daily from #1
   (2026-10-12) to yesterday, unplayed days included; a tap plays that
   day (today's daily stays the bare URL). Keep it usable when the list
   is long (a year of days).
2. No credit: an attempt BEGUN after its day records a PRACTICE result.
   It shows in History (marked as practice), and stays outside every
   aggregate (solved, best time, streaks, the share grid's stats) and
   outside K144's future shared stats; it is never the official result.
   An attempt begun on its day and finished later still counts (K47:
   a reload resumes it).
3. The mode badge says so during a practice attempt, like the replay
   badge (`modeBadgeFor` / `modeBadgeText`): proposed "Practice · not
   scored", a tap explains it in a toast.
4. Decide what a practice finish's banner and share offer (share as
   practice, or no share) and say which you chose.

Files to start from: `src/ui/daily_stats_view.ts`,
`src/game/daily_store.ts`, `src/game/daily_attempt.ts`,
`src/ui/daily_link.ts`, `src/game/share_text.ts`, `src/main.ts`, and
their tests under `test/game/` and `test/ui/`. The binding daily rules
are the MAZE STATS + DAILY MAZE entry in `.claude/rules/gameplay.md`:
update it, since a late first finish stops being official.

The store is versioned. Pre-launch, nothing needs a migration (CLAUDE.md
"Pre-launch: no back-compat"), but prefer a change that reads today's
records as they are (an absent field = official), so Levi's own history
on his devices survives.

## Part B (K151): the mode badge

Levi: "Hmm, let's show the Daily overlay in the same spot as the free
play overlay. Also, when this overlay is clickable--to reset back to the
other mode-- we should fade-in an X to help indicate that clicking will
cancel. Right now it looks like a button that indicates what it will do
when you click on it. This is still aproblem we need to solve for phones
though..."

1. The daily's label (today the HUD title, `dailyTitle()` in
   `src/main.ts` about line 1946) shows in the badge's spot, where "Free
   play" shows off the daily. `modeBadgeFor` shows nothing during the
   official attempt today; it gets a daily kind. Check how the label fits
   on a phone (the badge already steps down to a shorter label, then the
   icon) and what the HUD keeps once its title moves.
2. A badge that a tap acts on (Free play: a tap opens today's daily)
   fades in an X on hover, so it reads as leaving the mode, not as a
   button naming its action. Badges whose tap only explains (Replay, and
   Practice from Part A) get no X. Whether the daily badge itself does
   anything on a tap is your call; say what you chose.
3. Phones have no hover (Levi's open question). Build the proposal, the
   X always shown on coarse pointers (`(pointer: coarse)`), behind one
   named constant, and flag it for Levi as K151 (3) in your report.

Files: `src/ui/mode_badge.ts`, `src/ui/maze_hud.ts`, `src/main.ts`, and

## Process

- One stage, Part A then Part B. Run `Get-Date` at the start and at each
  milestone. Commit WIP on your branch at every milestone and never push.
  Don't edit `LEDGER.md`.
- Lore: the daily rules in `.claude/rules/gameplay.md`, a history line
  for each part in `docs/history.md`, and the project structure lines in
  `CLAUDE.md` only if you add a file.
- Stop when the stage is done or two hours after your start, whichever
  comes first. A compaction is not a stop, and neither is context size:
  after a compaction, re-read this brief and `git log`, then go on. At
  the stop, commit WIP, append a "Stage 1 handoff" section to this brief
  (what is done, what is next, evidence paths), commit it and report.
  Start nothing beyond this stage.
- Final report: what changed and where, the calls you made (Part A 4,
  Part B 2 and 3), the screenshot paths, a playable link for Levi
  (`https://levi.dev/knotmaze/?daily=1` for a past day, the bare
  `https://levi.dev/knotmaze/` for today), a `Ledger items` list
  (follow-ups, residuals, decisions for Levi) and the handle fields
  (branch, last commit, evidence, next).
