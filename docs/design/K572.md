### Culprit 1: `3465090` (K8 G3-1)

What it changed in the render path (from the diff and `render-gaps.md`):
- A slot's GAP row went from 114 to 25 texels. The over chain stopped being
  a per-slot adaptive CPU polyline (`POLY_SPACING_MAX` 0.3, `POLY_SAG_TOL`,
  deleted) and became a range of the shared SAMP table (`sampAt`:
  `texelFetch` on `uSamp`, 2 samples per RGBA32F texel, spacing 0.09 world,
  stride at most 3 past 64 points, never stretched or cut).
- The search (`gapNearestFast/Full`, in the section program, its explore
  variant, the junction fans and the still pluck) calls `gapOverPt` twice
  per segment (int `%` wrap, float-to-int conversions, a dependent
  `texelFetch` each, no carried point). The old code read one polyline
  texel per segment from the slot's own row, on segments up to 0.3 long.
- Pruning stayed: 8 chunk bounding circles (texels 10-17), 2 protect SAMP
  ranges (texels 18-21), the `sure2` early exit.

### Fix guesses (untested; no code)

The search is 5.7x costlier per frame on PowerVR (10.1 -> 57.4 ms, knot).
- Carry the previous segment's end point (one `sampAt` per segment, not
  two) and take the int `%` wrap and the float-to-int conversions out of
  the inner loop.
- Give the search coarser steps on straight stretches (the old CPU
  polyline was up to 0.3 long, the SAMP table is 0.09 everywhere): a
  per-slot stride from curvature, or a second, coarser range kept for the
  search while drawing keeps 0.09.
- Reject before any fetch: a fragment outside the slot's bounding circle
  or every chunk circle should leave before the first `sampAt`.
- A per-pixel pre-pass (K320's split) removes the per-fragment search, but
  it is blocked on K550 (the split cut wider bars, then drew no gaps); the
  K550 kill agent was reverting it while I measured.
- Even `e83f203` spends 10 ms of 23 on the search, so a fix has a floor to
  gain below the base.
- For K568: do not redraw the gap search while confetti falls over a
  sleeping scene (cache the scene); the G3-1 fix lowers it too.
