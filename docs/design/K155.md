# K155 stage 1: a star rating for each finish

Levi's notes, verbatim in `docs/design/levi_notes_2026-10-13.md`:
- about 18:06: "Let's give a star rating (1 through 3 or 5 or something)
  based for the run. This can be calculated on time, backtracking, and/or
  efficiency, and/or other game mode specific metrics. We should give a
  tooltip/phone-friendly description of where the stars come from. I
  suppose basing it off of efficiency alone might be the clearest for the
  default maze solve."
- about 21:15: "Stars appearing in the share text makes sense."

K140 (finish confetti) runs beside you in its own worktree. It owns the
confetti render code and its params; you own the win banner, the daily
stats view and the share text.

## What to build

- A pure model, a new file in `src/game/`, with unit tests: a finished
  run's stats in, a star count and its explanation out. Proposed: one to
  three stars, one for any finish.
- The measure. The HUD's EFFICIENCY (`src/game/maze_stats.ts`) is the
  optimal route over the route you ended on, so backtracked detours don't
  lower it: a solve that wanders everywhere and then walks the best route
  scores 100%. K74's figure, a greedy walker's median at 3.12x the
  shortest route, is the total distance walked. Stars need a measure a
  wandering solve can't max out. Propose one (for example, the distance
  walked, backtracks included, over the shortest route), and phrase its
  explanation in the player's terms, matching what the HUD shows.
- Thresholds from simulated solvers, not feel. Two harnesses already
  exist, untracked in the main checkout; copy what you need:
  - `C:\Users\lsl\Repositories\knotmaze\.claude\probes\K74\`: `lib.ts`
    (`dfsWalk`, `scoreFarEnd`: a depth-first walker with memory, the way
    a player plays) and `metrics.ts`;
  - `C:\Users\lsl\Repositories\knotmaze\.claude\probes\K80\rt\`:
    `rt_sim.ts` and `rt_run.ts` (greedy, fleer and smart players against
    the real-time minotaur), written today against master.

  Run them at today's defaults and at the daily's pinned definition
  (`DAILY_V1` in `src/ui/daily_link.ts`) over 50+ seeds, and report the
  distribution of the measure with the thresholds you pick. Aim: a careful
  player can earn three stars, and a typical greedy solve earns two about
  half the time.
- K80's real-time mode (`clk=realtime`) rates its catches too. Propose how
  the catches combine with the route measure, from K80's simulation
  (`results.md` in the same folder: the greedy player's median is 0
  catches, 51% of games without one).
- Where the stars show:
  - the win banner (`src/ui/maze_hud.ts`);
  - the daily stats view (`src/ui/daily_stats_view.ts`): each History row,
    plus an aggregate if one reads well;
  - the daily share text (`src/game/share_text.ts`).

  A tap on the stars explains them, because phones have no hover; a hover
  may show the same text. Keep it short: what each star needs and where
  this run landed.
- Daily records (`src/game/daily_store.ts`): derive the stars from
  stored stats when the record has them. If it lacks a needed field, add
  it in the store's versioned way, with tests. Old records without it
  show no stars. Pre-launch, no back-compat is owed (CLAUDE.md "Key Design
  Decisions"), but the store must not crash on old data.
- A param only if one is needed (say, stars on or off). If you add one,
  append it to codec v4's table. A viewer preference goes in
  `DAILY_EXEMPT_KEYS` beside `showStats`; name it in your report for K57.
