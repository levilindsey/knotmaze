## The rules (recommendations; build on them)

### 1. Ariadne's spool (4.15)

- Param `spool` (`sp2`): off, or a slack in [0.1, 1]. The spool holds
  the optimal route's length times (1 + slack), measured the way par
  measures a route (`K137_s1.md`).
- The yarn is a stack of moves. A move that retraces the last yarn
  segment reels it in and gives its length back; any other move pays
  yarn out, loops included. Undo reels in too.
- A move that would pay out past the spool is refused, with a toast and
  a gauge flash, like other refused moves.
- The yarn shows while the spool is on (the string look, `pathViz`),
  and it shrinks as it reels in: today the string never picks up its
  yarn.
- HUD: a spool gauge (the share left). Share: "🧶 82% of the spool"
  (the share used at the finish). Stats: the share used.

### 2. Chalk marks (4.16)

- A viewer convenience, not a design param: a viewer pref `chalk`
  (default off) shows a chalk button, with the key C.
- The button toggles a tick on the junction the head stands on. Marks
  last for the solve and clear on restart. A long press is a tug today,
  so there is no gesture for it.
- The tick is a marker glyph on the junction web (`marker_glyphs.ts`,
  `marker_mesh.ts`).

### 3. Hints and a compass (4.28)

- Param `hints` (`hn`): 0 (off) to 5 hints a solve. A hint button (key
  H) lights the next good arm from the head, the first arm of the
  shortest walk-graph route to the exit (`distToExit`), for about two
  seconds, in the route-rim look the telegraph and the walker use
  (K525). Share: "💡1" (hints used). Stats: hints used.
- Param `compass` (bool, off): a small arrow at the head that points
  along the walk graph's next good arm, never in a straight line to the
  exit, updated after every move.
- With the minotaur, a hint and the compass still point to the exit;
  they do not route around it.

## Design (2026-10-21, the s2 agent)

- **The spool is the path stack.** In classic mode (not the trail look)
  a tap on the top of `pathEdges` pops it and any other tap pushes, so
  the yarn IS `pathEdges`: a retrace reels in, a loop pays out, Undo
  replays one tap fewer. The spool forces the string look and the stack:
  `pathVizOf(params)` (`game/spool.ts`) answers "string" while the spool
  is on, at every `params.pathViz` read. Measure: par's (runs, title runs
  included): capacity = `parFor(fewestRuns, spoolSlack)` runs, the yarn's
  length = `pathEdges.length`. Params `spool` (`sp2`, flag) and
  `spoolSlack` (`sps`, 0.1-1, default 0.3): call 1.
- **The refusal** is a new tap result, "spool": tapEdge refuses a push
  past the capacity after the burnt and smolder checks, changing and
  logging nothing; replay treats it as a failure. main shows a throttled
  toast and flashes the HUD gauge. The cut stays off with the spool (the
  `plain` test in setRules), as with par and fragile.
- **The reel-in look.** The renderer's newest yarn event is clipped to
  the dot in reverse while a backtrack retraces it, then popped at the
  move's end; restores rebuild from `pathEdges` under the spool.
- **Hints** (`hn`, 0-5) and the **compass** (`cmp`, its own flag: call
  2) share `game/hints.ts`: `nextGoodArm` = the head's live arm that
  minimizes its length plus the far end's walk distance to the exit
  (`walkDistances` with broken and cut runs removed; lowest id on a tie),
  -1 at the exit or with no route. They ignore the minotaur and the fire.
  A hint lights that arm for 2 s in the K525 rim look (cyan), ended early
  by a move; while it is lit it wins the one telegraph slot (a renderer
  wrapper keeps the base telegraph and puts it back). The compass is a
  small arrow beside the head along the arm's first stretch, hidden
  while the dot moves.
- **Chalk** is a viewer pref (`knotmaze.chalk`, panel row; never a
  link): a button and the key C toggle a tick glyph on the head's
  junction. Hint (key H) and chalk buttons sit in one group, bottom left
  above the cut buttons' row, clear of the move pad (bottom right): call 3.
- **Restart vs Undo.** Hints used and the chalk marks live on the
  controller but `reset()` leaves them alone (Undo replays through it);
  main's real restart clears them (`clearAids`). Hints, the compass and
  chalk are off in walker modes.
