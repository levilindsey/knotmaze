- Step 1: `tangentRoot` (glyph_graph.ts) pairs C and O by the medial axis.
  For each foot arc a on C, the medial point is M = C(a) + r n (n: C's
  normal toward O), with r = |M - O(b)| (Newton inside a bracket; O's
  nearest point by a dense scan and a ternary refinement). R is the medial
  point where 2r = the part (TAPER_PART_LU, where the inner edges meet).
  The centerline is fit through the medial points as ROOT_SEGS (4) Hermite
  cubics in a, G1 at J with the spur's tangent. At each of the
  ROOT_TABLE_N (64) samples, along the fitted path's normal, the half-width
  is where that line leaves each stroke's ribbon on its side (stepped out
  from r, then bisected). The ribbon takes the nearer one. One side lies on
  its stroke's outer edge, and the other sinks into its stroke by the
  `insetLU` below. A width that reached both edges bulged 0.03 LU out of
  one stroke, and a centerline on the two edges' midline diverged at R,
  where the edges' feet jump. `TaperReport` adds `bRLU`, `insetLU` and
  `overlapLU`.

**Step 3, the thorn at J: the ribbon renderer's miter at the body's cusp.**
Measured with `thorn.mjs` (`thorn_s2.txt`, `thorn_rows.json`) and
`raster.mjs` (`sheets/raster_a_J.png`):
- **The cusp.** The body (chain 0) turns 162 to 172 deg at ONE sample at J:
  - "a": i 47 at 169.8 deg (xh 2), i 70 at 170.9 deg (xh 3);
  - "ai": i 46 at 171.8 deg (xh 2), i 69 at 161.6 deg (xh 3).
- **The miter.** ribbon_mesh.ts `update()` sets the outer offset to
  halfWidth / cosHalf, with cosHalf floored at 0.5, so at a cusp the offset
  is 2h. It places the vertex along the sample TANGENT's normal. That
  tangent follows one leg, so the vertex lands 2h from J, perpendicular to
  the strokes and h past their edges:
  - "a" at xh 2: J + (0.222, 0.975) 2h, pointing down (y is down);
  - "a" at xh 3: J + (0.173, -0.985) 2h, pointing up.

  So the thorn points down at xh 2 and up at xh 3 and in "ai", as stage 1's
  zoom showed. At xh 2 the inner side also clamps, to 0.3h.
- **The raster.** It compares the uploaded fill vertices against the
  round-joined ideal outline. At J the only mesh outside the ideal is a thin
  triangle: 0.0113 LU² at xh 2 and 0.0153 LU² at xh 3, reaching 0.25 LU
  (= h) past the edge.
- **Not the root and not the fans.** The thorn is identical under tapered
  and notaper, because chain 0 is the same in both, so the root neither
  causes it nor covers it. It is not the junction fan either: the fans are
  hidden at J under the taper, and notaper draws the same thorn with them.
- **The renderer fix.** Above a turn of about 120 deg, don't miter: bevel
  or round the outer side, or at least clamp the outer offset to h at a
  cusp; here the spur or root covers the tip. This is in
  `src/render/ribbon_mesh.ts`, outside this lane, so I left it.
- **A builder fix would remove the cusp instead.** Two ways, neither
  attempted in this stage:

    root's width (the brief's example).

**For K620** (the root's paint, option A: each side takes its stroke's
paint and blends into the spur's at J). What `TangentRoot` gives it:
- **Which side faces which stroke.** `sideC` is +1 when C lies left of R
  -> J in GU (y up). The graph flips y, so in graph units the sides swap.
- **Where each side samples its stroke.** `footC(a)` and `footO(a)` give
  the arc from J along each stroke under each drawn edge. C's side samples
  C at footC, and O's side samples O at footO.
- **The arc from R to J.** It is the root's own parameter a (C's foot
  arc), from aR at R down to 0 at J. The table is uniform in a.
- **The lateral coordinate.** offset / (h + sepAt(a) / 2), signed by
  sideC. The centerline, which is the medial axis, is where the two
  strokes' paints meet.
- **What the medial root changes against stage 1:**
  - R moved: aR is 0.947 at xh 2, where stage 1 had 0.765.
  - The feet are asymmetric (bR is not aR), so the two sides reach their
    strokes at different arcs.
  - The O side lies inside O by the inset (at most 0.054 LU), so O's paint
    there should come from O at footO, not from the drawn edge.
  - Under tp60 at xh 2, footO is not monotone near R.
