# K580 stage 2: the medial-axis root

Ledger: K580. Parent: K115, cursive text. One stage, opus, 2 h cap.
Stage 1's brief and handoff: `docs/design/K580.md`. Read its "Levi's
call", "Where it stands" and "Handoff" sections first.

## Levi's calls

- Stage 1's call stands: the tangent join, and a spur that leaves the
  junction wider than normal, at a width that continues the outer edges of
  both merged strokes, then tapers to normal where they become collinear.
  The verbatim note is in `docs/design/K580.md`.
- **Pending:** the root's paint. Either each side of the root takes its
  own stroke's paint and blends into the spur's at J, or the strokes are
  drawn over the root. Build nothing that depends on the answer: this stage
  makes no paint changes.

## Where it stands

Stage 1 is merged behind `#glyphs2` (see K580's ledger line for the
commit). `tangentRoot` (`src/core/glyphs/glyph_graph.ts`) pairs the
arriving stroke C and the departing stroke O by equal arc from J. That
pairing skews the chord:
- at xh 2, the a fails `taper-g1`: the outline is 0.022 LU outside the
  strokes, against a bound of 0.01;
- the ribbons still overlap past R, by 0.26 LU at xh 2 and 0.15 LU at
  xh 3.

The thorn at J, the body's cusp, shows with and without the taper. The
"ai" notch is not attributed.

## Steps

1. **The medial-axis root.** Pair C and O by nearest point (or a true
   medial axis of the two curves) instead of equal arc. Keep R where the
   strokes sit `TAPER_PART_LU` apart, refit the midline, and recompute sep
   and the width profile.
   - Target: `taper-g1` passes at xh 2 and xh 3 on seeds 7 and 9, and the
     ribbons don't overlap past R.
   - Report the numbers in the form of stage 1's table.
2. **The part point.** Make stills of `tp52` (today) against `tp60`, at
   xh 2 and xh 3, at desktop and at phone scale, as one sheet for Levi.
   Don't pick between them; report the numbers.
3. **The thorn at J.** The body arrives heading right and leaves heading
   left. Find what draws the thorn: the ribbon's outer edge at the cusp, a
   miter, or the junction fan.
   - If the fix is in the glyph data or the builder, make it for the
     tangent join; for example, the oval could leave J along the spur's
     tangent and turn within the root's width.
   - If it belongs in the ribbon renderer, report the measured cause and
     leave it.
4. **The "ai" notch**, at the i's joint and at the a's J under `aland`.
   Attribute it. Fix it if the cause is in `src/core/glyphs/`; otherwise
   report it.
5. **Calibrate** `LINT.taperBulgeLU` and the crotch bound on the new
   stills: the largest values that still look clean, with the evidence.
6. **Tests.** Update `test/core/glyph_taper.test.ts` and
   `glyph_lint.test.ts`, and drop the known `taper-g1` failure if step 1
   fixes it. `npm test` and `npm run build` must pass, and the golden must
   stay byte-identical.
7. **Sheets.** Regenerate `{a,ai,ia,aa}_{desk,phone}.png` and
   `a_join_zoom.png` with stage 1's scripts, columns tapered / `notaper` /
   `aland`, plus the `tp52`/`tp60` sheet from step 2.

## Stop rule

Stop when the stage is done or at the 2 h wall-clock cap, whichever comes
first. Run `Get-Date` at the start and at each milestone.

When you stop:

- append a "Handoff" section to this doc: what was built, the numbers,
  the sheets' paths, what's left, and the item's handle fields;
- report.

is not a stop: after one, re-read this brief and `git log` before going
on. Any helper runs on sonnet with a 1 h cap under this same rule.

End your report with the item's handle fields and a `Ledger items` list.
