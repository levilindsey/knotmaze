## Why

s35 (merged at e203f246) measured the routes. Nothing reaches the 3 ms gate without a per-frame deadline or GPU
solves. A pool of outline workers is the only route that raises the redraw rate now with low risk and no change
to the look: s35's Chrome prototype (`k1247s35_pool.*`) took def from 14 to 45-52 Hz and fat to 57 Hz at K=5, at
84-89 ms latency, which is below today's app. GPU placement (R1) and frame() cuts (R2) come after this and cut the
work per update inside the pool.

## Goal

`rp=outline` in the app runs its outline on a pool of K workers, and the drawn output for any scene is
bit-identical to one worker's output. The technique is yours; s35's outline is:

- **Dispatch** (`outline_app.ts`, around line 1749): K workers, each with its own OutlineModel and structure. The
  newest scene goes to any idle worker. A reply applies only if its id is newer than the last one applied.
- **K:** from `navigator.hardwareConcurrency`, capped at 4. Measure K = 3, 4 and 5.
- **Structure:** worker 0 builds first and serves, and the others build after its first reply. Building on all K
  at once took 812-863 ms at K=5, against 466-507 at K=1 and the 410 ms bar.
- **Main's budget:** the post (0.8-0.9 ms) and the upload (1.6-2.2 ms per reply) now land on most frames, against
  the 8.5 ms mid-drag bar. Send the scene as transferable typed arrays.
- **Restructures:** each worker restructures on its own `diagramKey` change or throw.
- **Heap:** measure the heap of the K models.

Leave the KF_INC flags and the request's `exact` in place (they are off). Removing them touches `outline.ts`,
where K1314 s13 is working; that is its own item after s13 lands.

**What landed** (aca4db2c, 64c878ff, 11965303, 688c0b5a, 95ddc57f and the final commit):
- `src/render/outline/outline_pool.ts`: `OutlinePool`, K workers, each restructuring on its own. Main sends
  the newest scene to an idle worker (worker 0 first) and draws a reply only if it is newer than the one
  drawn. Scenes go as transferable Float64Arrays (`packScene` / `unpackScene`); `diagramKey` runs on main.
  `outline_step.ts` is one worker's handling as a function, so the test runs K virtual workers on it.
- `outline_build.ts` + `outline_build_worker.ts`: build workers (`opb=<n>`, 2 by default with the pool).
  Main gathers the input (`gatherSceneInput`) and transfers it. A builder runs `buildFieldScene`, the look
  patch and `chainArcMap`, then packs the scene back; main hands it to the pool as before. The s18
  capture (`__knotOutlineCapture`) forces the main path.
- `outline_app.ts` stats: `latencyMs` (input posted, or pool send without builders, to reply drawn),
  `latencyPoolMs` (pool send to drawn), `gatherParts`, `buildMs`, and `buildSame` / `buildDiff`. The last
  two come from the builder identity hook, `window.__knotOutlineBuildCheck = true`: main rebuilds every
  scene it sends a builder and compares the reply as JSON. `frame_loop.ts` reads `opk=<n>|auto` and `opb=<n>`.
