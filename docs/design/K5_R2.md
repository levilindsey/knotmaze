## Questions (take the recommendation unless the coordinator says otherwise)

1. **The G3-0 probe hooks** (4924-5182) sit in explore's region but read
   no R2 field: they read explorePieces and exploreUpdatePending, call
   setExplore and update, and read about 22 reveal and paint privates.
   Rec: they stay on the façade, unmoved. Moving them would tag fields
   that R3 and R4 re-home anyway. R5 can give them a home (say
   `ribbon/scene_probe.ts`).
2. **exploreVisAt, exploreShownAt and `get exploreOn`.** 2.3's table
   puts them in explore's interface, but they read only shared fields
   (explorePieces, exploreWhole, exploreByChain, ps, and the imported
   pieceVis), and both parts, the façade (pickThread 3903, rebuildFillets
   6750) and the app call them. Rec: they stay on the façade with
   EXPLORE_PICK_VIS, by 2.3's helper rule (381-383): callers in two parts,
   and they read the façade's state, not only their arguments, so they
   are not `sampling.ts` material. computeGapSlot's and rebuildFillets'
   visibility reads then take no extra hop. Alternative: move them behind delegations and
   re-time under the 5% rule.
3. **Call paths.** Rec: a call to a name probes use goes through the
   instance: `this.x` on the façade (call sites unchanged), `this.r.x` in
   a part. A probe's patch then catches every call, including a part's
   calls to its own methods (renderEmergent to computeGapSlot, setExplore
   to applyExploreGaps, applyExploreGaps to exploreCutAt,
   syncExploreJoints to exploreJointShown, exploreJointShown to
   exploreGateVisAt). This extends 2.3's patch rule, which names only
   another part's copy. Other calls go to the part: on the façade
   `this.crossing.renderEmergent/renderMarkerGaps(...)` and
   `this.explore.exploreJointsKey()`, inside a part `this.x`; parts
   reach each other only through the façade.
   - The façade keeps a one-line delegation (name, modifiers, signature,
     pointer comment) for each moved public method (setExploreContrast,
     setExplore, exploreGapState, exploreJointState) and each probe name
     (computeGapSlot, assignGapIds, applyExploreGaps, exploreCutAt,
     exploreJointShown, exploreGateVisAt, exploreShownSpan).
   - A delegation or forwarder that a part calls, or that nothing in
     `src/` calls (neighborGaps, whose only callers move), drops
     `private` for `/** @internal ribbon */` with a "probes use it"
     note; tsc decides.
   - Moved methods only their part calls leave nothing behind (buildPoly,
     polyDist, protectReach, twinNeighbor, syncExploreJoints). A part
     method the façade calls loses `private` (the undo restores it).

   Alternative: only the five patched names go through the instance:
   fewer non-private members, but a later probe that patches another
   name silently misses the part's calls.
4. **Constants two parts use.** Rec: each moves with the part that owns
   its meaning, exported; the others import it. Nothing imports a value
   from ribbon_renderer.ts, which would turn the type-only cycle into a
   runtime one. crossing_gaps exports CUT_BIAS, EMERGENT_POOL,
   MARKER_SLOTS, JOINT_CLEAR_REACH_SCALE and GATE_LEAD (the slot solve's
   look-ahead, by its comment); explore_gaps exports EXPLORE_JOINT_MIN and
   imports MARKER_SLOTS. Alternative: one `ribbon/consts.ts` for every
   constant two modules use, which R3-R5 would grow.
5. **passInfo and emergentBase.** The inventory counts both as
   crossing's own (670-675, 682), but the constructor writes them
   (1197-1210, 1217), 2.3 lists passInfo as a shared scene field, and
   emergentBase is the slot layout beside markerBase (5 probe files read
   it). Rec: both stay on the façade, `/** @internal ribbon */`;
   renderEmergent moves, so 2.3's "the emergent pool" is renderEmergent
   and EMERGENT_POOL.
6. **GapSource.** 2.3 and 5.3 name R2's seam `GapSource`, with
   `solve(fillHalf, gap)` writing the slots and underWindows. But
   update()'s gap pass interleaves the reveal gate (gateCache, gateRev,
   gateLatch, passShowTime: R3's state) with computeGapSlot, so a solve()
   holding that loop would move reveal code into crossing_gaps. Rec: no
   solve() in R2. The seam is the part's surface as it lands
   (computeGapSlot, renderEmergent, renderMarkerGaps, assignGapIds, and
   the slot table and underWindows every reader uses); G3-1 edits inside
   it, and R3 can fold the loop into a solve() once the gate is a part.
