The rules as built:
- The extras step after the minotaur on every accepted move, a slain
  minotaur's included. On a sprint's skipped move every monster stands,
  but a sleeper still wakes and a follower still sets off.
- A sleeper wakes when you move along a run that touches its junction,
  and neither moves nor catches on that move. Awake, it is a hunter with
  the minotaur's pace, sight, accuracy and contact rule, on its own
  counter stream, with no telegraph.
- A follower starts waiting when you leave its junction, appears there 1
  or 2 moves later and walks your trail a run a move. At each junction,
  with chance 0.25 (FOLLOW_SHORTCUT, its stream), it steps along its
  shortest route to you instead. Walking back into it, or waiting while
  it is one run behind, is a catch. While you are on the entrance it
  walks home and sleeps.
- A catch by any monster sends you to the entrance, the minotaur to its
  lair (or where it stands, lair off) and every extra home asleep;
  `woken` keeps counting. Hearts and minoRestart apply as to the
  minotaur's catches. The toast: "Caught by a sleeper!" or "Caught by a
  follower!", then the hearts or restart text.
