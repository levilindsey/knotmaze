# K148 + K149: the player's off-screen indicator, and pan room

Two small camera changes from Levi's notes of 2026-10-13, "About 16:25
(camera)" (verbatim in `docs/design/levi_notes_2026-10-13.md`). One
Sonnet agent, one stage, two parts.

## Part A (K148): an off-screen indicator for the player

Levi: "When panning away from the current position, we should also show
an offscreen indicator for it."

- The head (the player's position) joins the finish as a K73 indicator
  client: `src/ui/offscreen_indicators.ts`, registered in `src/main.ts`
  next to the finish's (`indicators.add`, about line 1850) and the
  minotaur's (about line 1911).
- It wears K83's head glyph (`src/render/marker_glyphs.ts`:
  `markerGlyph`, `markerGlyphSvgFit`; see how the finish's icon is made).
- It shows whenever the head is off screen in the maze (fabric mode with
  a maze), during play and after a finish.
- A tap eases the camera back to the head and resumes the follow (in
  follow mode the pan suspends the follow: re-engage it at once; in fit
  mode, frame the head). Reuse the tap path the finish's indicator uses
  (`frameWorld`) where it fits.
- The finish has a viewer setting (`settings.finishIndicator`). Whether
  the head gets one too is your call; no codec params either way. Say
  what you chose.

## Part B (K149): room to pan the maze partly off screen

Levi: "Also, we should support panning the maze a bit more off-screen at
furthest zoom. Especially to get it out from under the menu."

- `clampView` (`src/main.ts` about line 638) clamps the pan so the maze
  always fills the view: at zoom 1 (the fit zoom) there is no pan range
  at all. Allow slack at every zoom: a share of the screen per side,
  enough to drag the maze clear of the open panel (desktop: the panel at
  the right; phone width: measure where it sits).
- Check the follow camera (`applyCam`), the fit reset (`resetCamera`,
  and whatever returns the view home), pinch and wheel zoom about a
  point, and the indicators' placement with the maze panned off.
- Pull the clamp's math into a pure function with unit tests if that is
  the clean way to test it.
