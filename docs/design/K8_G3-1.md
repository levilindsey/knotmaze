## Texture sizes against the Pixel 11's MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE of 8192

Observed by a Node census of the scene build (`texture_census.ts`, whose
header says how to run it, and its outputs: from the docs agent's
worktree); it matches G3-0's browser counts and swept 319 links at gs 24.
Rows are 1024 texels wide; GAP's height is the next power of two >=
crossings + 69, at least 8 (gapTextureFor, gap_mask.ts 744).

- Only GAP's height passes 8192, and G3-1 leaves it as it is (gs 72 fills
  16384, the desktop's maximum: `K8_stage1.md` 426). Inferred, not tested:
  past 8192 the phone's allocation fails and every gap vanishes.
- GAP's width drops from 114 texels to about 24 (2048 rows: 3.7 to 0.79 MB).
  SAMP stays small (176 rows at fabric gs 48) and adds a sampler to four
  programs: keep each at 16 or fewer (the Pixel has 32; `K8_stage1.md` 432).

stamps you checked; every result names its host (the worker or `phw=0`).
