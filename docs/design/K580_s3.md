# K580 stage 3: no miter thorn at a sharp turn

Ledger: K580. One stage, opus, 2 h cap. Stage 2 measured the defect: see
the Handoff in `docs/design/K580_s2.md`, its steps 3 and 4, and "What the
stills show". The design is `docs/design/K580.md`.

## Decided

- Fix it in the renderer, not the a's strands. A renderer fix covers every
  sharp turn: the a's J, the i's joint in "ai", the aland spikes, and any
  knot or fabric sample that turns as sharply. Stage 2's two builder
  alternatives are out of scope.
- tp52 stays. The taper does not change.

## The defect

- **Where.** In `src/render/ribbon_mesh.ts`, `RibbonMesh.update()` (about
  lines 195-270) miters every sample:
  - the outer offset is halfWidth / cosHalf, with cosHalf floored at 0.5,
    so past a 120 deg turn the offset stays at 2h;
  - the vertex goes along the sample tangent's normal. At a cusp with
    unequal legs, that tangent follows one leg instead of bisecting the
    turn;
  - so the outer vertex lands 2h from the sample and h past the stroke's
    edges. That is the thorn.
- **The a's J.** Chain 0 turns 162-172 deg at one sample: "a" i 47 (xh 2)
  and i 70 (xh 3); "ai" i 46 and i 69. The raster finds 0.011-0.015 LU² of
  mesh outside the round-joined ideal, reaching 0.25 LU (= h) past the
  edge.
- **The i's joint in "ai".** A bare wedge of the ideal: 0.017 LU², 0.18-0.23
  LU deep, at (4.552, 1.613) for xh 2. It sits between chain 0's 130 deg
  miter edge (i 148 at xh 2) and the round piece at the joint. That
  piece's centre sits about h up the stem from chain 0's cusp.
- **Under aland.** Smaller spikes at its sharp samples, 0.009-0.026 LU².
- **Not the root and not the fans.** notaper draws the same thorn.

## Steps

1. **Reproduce.** s2's probes are untracked in the main checkout's
   `.claude/probes/K580/`: `raster.mjs`, `thorn.mjs`, `shots.mjs`,
   `sheets.ps1`, `join_zoom.ps1` and the `sheets/`. Copy what you need
   into your worktree's `.claude/probes/K580/`. Re-run the raster once on
   your base to confirm s2's numbers. Then count how many samples turn
   past 90, 120 and 150 deg at rest in the regular scenes (step 6's A/B
   scenes, plus a kinky knot). The counts say which scenes the fix can
   touch.
2. **The fix, in `RibbonMesh.update()`.** Past the threshold, the outer
   side stops mitering and follows the round-joined ideal.
   - **Recommendation:** give a sharp sample a few extra strip vertices at
     its centre, with the normal swept across the turn: a round fan inside
     the strip. A bevel along the true outer bisector is the fallback if
     the strip cannot take extra vertices cheaply. It flattens a cusp's
     tip by about 0.29h, so measure it before choosing it.
   - **Identity:** below the threshold, every vertex stays bit-identical,
     so scenes without sharp turns render 0 px different. Put the
     threshold where the current clamp starts (a 120 deg turn) unless
     step 1's counts argue otherwise; say which you chose.
   - **Continuity:** a physics knot can sweep a turn through the
     threshold, and the outline must move continuously, with no pop. For
     example, the extra vertices could start collapsed on the miter
     vertex and spread to the arc.
   - **Use the true outer bisector of the two normalized legs**, not the
     tangent's normal, for every sample past the threshold.
   - **Keep the concave-side clamp**, and check that it still holds at a
     cusp. At xh 2 it clamps to 0.3h there.
   - **No per-frame allocation in steady state.** A sample that crosses
     the threshold mid-drag must not reallocate on every frame.
   - **Shading:** the new vertices carry the edge value of the
     across-width coordinate, so the casing and the highlight stay
     continuous.
   - **Target:** at the a's J and at the i's joint, no mesh more than 0.01
     LU outside the round-joined ideal, and no bare region of it deeper
     than 0.01 LU. s2 measured 0.25 LU and 0.23 LU. 0.01 LU is stage 2's
     visibility threshold (`taperBulgeLU`).
3. **The i's joint.** If the bare wedge survives step 2, find where the
   joint's round piece takes its centre.
   - If the centre comes from the glyph's junction data, fix it in
     `src/core/glyphs/`.
   - If it lives in `src/render/ribbon/` (K525's lane), stop at that point
     and report the fix you would make. The exception: K525 has merged
     (`git log master --oneline` shows "K525 merged"). Then rebase onto
     master and make the fix.
4. **Other copies.** `src/geometry/bend_radius.ts` mirrors update()'s
   concave-side clamp, and the pluck overlay may build its own strip.
   Keep them consistent where they must agree with the mesh. If a copy is
   in another agent's files, report it.
5. **Tests.** Write a unit test of the vertex computation (split out a
   pure function if `RibbonMesh` needs a GPU context, as `bend_radius.ts`
   does). It covers:
   - a hairpin with unequal legs;
   - a 130 deg turn;
   - a 100 deg turn, whose vertices are pinned to master's values;
   - a sweep through the threshold in 1 deg steps that bounds the largest
     vertex jump.

   Check each against the target in step 2.
6. **Verify.**
   - **The A/B.** Run a same-state pixel A/B of master and your branch
     with K550's harness, `.claude/probes/K550/kill/ab_probe.mjs`. Copy it
     and its `png.mjs` into your probes and change its ports from
     5250/5251 to 5312/5313. Builds come from `scripts/preview.ps1
     -BuildOnly`. The scenes are knot, maze, reveal and burn, at dsf 1
     and 2, with its same-build control.
     - Where step 1 counted no sharp sample, expect 0 px.
     - Attribute every changed region to a sharp sample, from a crop. A
       thorn that is gone is the expected change.
   - **The K580 sheets.** Re-shoot "a" and "ai" at xh 2 and 3 (tapered,
     notaper, aland), desk and phone-scale, as s2 did. Re-run the raster
     and thorn numbers too. Put before and after side by side in
     `sheets/s3_before_after.png`. Use a free port near 5310 for your
     server.
   - **The stem.** s2's `raster_a_stem.png` shows a sharp spot on the a's
     stem that nobody examined. Say what it was and whether the fix
     removed it.
   - **The suite.** `npm test`.
   - **The cost.** update() runs on every ribbon every frame. Show with a
     node micro-benchmark on a fat knot's chains (before and after) that
     the change adds no measurable cost.
7. **Lore.** Update the miter-clamp entry in the ribbon lore. It attaches
   when you open ribbon_mesh.ts.

## Stop rule

Stop when the stage is done or at the 2 h wall-clock cap, whichever comes
first. Run `Get-Date` at the start and at each milestone. Commit WIP to
your branch at every milestone, green or not, and never push.

A compaction is not a stop: after one, re-read this brief and `git log`
before going on. Keep reads narrow (Grep -n, then Read with
offset/limit). Any helper runs on sonnet with a 1 h cap under this same
rule.

When you stop:
- commit WIP;
- append a "Handoff" section to this brief: what landed, the numbers
  (raster, A/B, benchmark), the sheets' paths, the residuals and the
  handle fields;
- report.

End your report with the item's handle fields and a `Ledger items` list.
