# K409: the fire's widths in world units, the Gone ember, the ember over the smoke

Ledger: K409, with K410 and K599. This is K290's last stage (burnt
threads). One stage, opus, 2 h cap.

## Levi's calls (2026-10-20, the report card: every recommendation)

1. **World units.** The ember and the band get fixed widths in world
   units, matched to today's median, so the average look doesn't move.
   This applies under both looks.
2. **Under Gone, the whole ember goes on the unburnt side.** The glow is
   drawn ahead of the cut, about 0.8 ctrl long at today's median (0.25
   ahead + 0.55 behind) instead of the 0.25 that shows now. Under Char
   the ember stays on both sides of the cut.
3. **The dark band stays under Gone,** as it draws today, ahead of the
   fire under both looks. The notes that say "under Char only" are wrong:
   fix them (K599).
4. **Draw the ember over the smoke.** The smoke still softens the scene
   but never covers the glow.

## Where it stands

The facts are in `docs/design/K114_burn.md`, Stage 3c handoff, item 4
("The widths"), with "For the next stage" below it. In short:

- **The constants** are in ctrl, in `src/render/explore_glsl.ts`:
  `EXPLORE_FRONT_BAND` 0.7, `EXPLORE_EMBER_AHEAD` 0.25,
  `EXPLORE_EMBER_BEHIND` 0.55.
- **Why ctrl is a problem:** a ctrl stretches with the thread, so on the
  phone's fit camera one ctrl is 2.67 / 4.84 / 7.01 CSS px over Daily
  #12's 29 fronts (min / median / max) at s 8.82 CSS px per world unit.
  The median is about 0.55 world; the thread is 0.52 world wide.
- **Under Gone,** the ember is a rim of about 0.84 CSS px at a cut end,
  and the FX smoke hides a third of it (87% at the lit fuse).
- **The method that measured it:** a same-state diff on one frozen state
  (`fw=0`, every clock pinned, the FX truly stopped, a B-against-B
  control), in `.claude/probes/K290/stage3c/` (main checkout, untracked):
  `width_probe.mjs`, `measure.mjs`, `ahead_probe.mjs`, `make_rows.mjs`,
  the phone scripts, and the sheets in `out/sheets/`.
  - "For the next stage" explains why hiding the FX container does not
    stop the FX, and how a true FX-off frame is made. Use that method.
- **Lore:** `.claude/rules/render-explore.md`, THE BURN and THE EMBER
  DRAWS; it attaches when you read the explore files.

   - match today's median, and record the world values and how you

   - Find the cheapest correct route: an ember-only pass or overlay drawn
