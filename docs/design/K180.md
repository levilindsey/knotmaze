## What to build

Three params in the panel's Touch & feel folder. They are viewer
preferences: append them to codec v4's table after K140's 99-100
(appends only), add them to `DAILY_EXEMPT_KEYS` (`src/ui/daily_link.ts`)
beside the other Touch & feel prefs, and update
`test/ui/daily_link.test.ts` and the codec pins. Name their keys in your
report for K57.

- `pluckWhole`, a slider for W's amount. Default 0.5 (today's
  WHOLE_AMP); 0 is the pre-K104 pluck, bit for bit. Pick a range that
  shows Levi both ends (about 0-1.5) and say why.
- A crossings dropdown, mapped to `wholeNodes`: through (default), ride,
  pinch.
- A glow dropdown, the cue (`CUE_WHITE`) on a full-motion pluck:
  - off (default): today's look;
  - instead: today's wholeMode "cue" (the cue replaces W);
  - with: W plus the cue's brightening;
  - fallback: the cue only for taps whose W cannot show, by K104's
    census criterion (peak under 0.6 of the half-width or under 2 px,
    including sound-only taps in junction cores and taps bound by hide
    zones). This needs an estimate at pluck time: validate it against
    K104's census and report how often the two agree.
- Neighbors stay "overlap", with no param ("current is good").
- The still pluck (reduced motion) is unchanged by all three.

  WIP on your branch at every milestone and never push. Don't edit
  `LEDGER.md`.
- Lore goes in `.claude/rules/render.md`'s pluck section (the params, the
