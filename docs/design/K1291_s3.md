## Why

Stage 2 (f6e89576) made `rp=outline` draw only the revealed stretch, in every appearance mode and in the
thread reveal (`xp=threads`), mostly at stage 1's at-rest footprint level from f50 on. Three things are
left, in this order:
1. **Specks ahead of the front.** An arc piece (a junction corner) is tagged with chain A while it runs
   from chain A's sA to chain B's sB, so it reads only A's reveal: dashed border specks draw ahead of the
   front in the trace modes (`.claude/probes/K1291/out/s2/crops/muse_trace-burn_f50_4_past.png`).
2. **The main frame got slower:** at rest 8.0-8.4 ms against 6.0-6.6 before stage 2, mid-drag 11.1-14.4
   against 9.0-11.7. Not yet measured where (guess: the reveal code runs in the fill and border shaders
   when nothing is revealing).
3. **The rainbow front:** today's rainbow widens the thread at its front; the outline doesn't (IoU
   0.65-0.78 at f20, and at f50 on def and muse).

## Goal

In `rp=outline`: nothing draws past today's front, at corners included, in the appearance reveals and the
thread reveal; the main frame back to stage 1's cost when no reveal runs; then the rainbow front's swell.
Today's look is the approved one: judge against it. The technique is yours (stage 2's idea for 1: carry
chain B and sB per vertex, draw only where both arms are revealed).
