# K81 brief: the traveled highlight as a smooth arc through junctions

Levi, 2026-10-12, with a screenshot (`.claude/probes/K81/
levi_junction_nub.png`; probes are untracked, so the main checkout holds
it): "I've been trying to think of a better way to render intersections
like this for the traversed-path highlight. We show a discontinuous flat
nub across the bottom side of this intersection. Could we round that out?
Or blend it a bit? Ideally, the yellow highlight should indicate whether
the player traveled, so a smooth continuous arc from right to left would
probably be best."

## Diagnosis to confirm

A joint's web takes its color from an angular blend of the bracketing
arms (the edge-aware junction field; since K4 the fans read per-arm
material strips). In the wedge between a traveled (gold) arm and an
untraveled arm, the gold-to-section transition therefore runs as a
straight line across the wedge, which is the flat nub. Gold also fills
wedges the route never crossed.

## Goal

At every traversed junction, the gold reads as ONE smooth band from the
arrival arm to the departure arm at thread width, continuous with both
arms' gold. The rest of the web keeps the untraveled material, with a
smooth, anti-aliased boundary. Non-route paint (section colors, pulses,
hover) must stay as it is today.

## Approach to evaluate

- Per traversed joint, a route curve: for example a quadratic or cubic
  from the arrival arm's edge point to the departure arm's, tangent to
  both arms. Evaluate the distance to it in the fan shader (and in its
  CPU mirror); gold applies inside half the width plus an AA band.
- The route through each joint comes from the gameplay paint declaration
  (`setPaint` and the path's runs), including the head junction. A
  backtracked joint holds two route bands or none: decide.
- Trail (recentPath) mode fades the gold along the trail. The band must
  follow the same fade.
- Carry the idea into G4's SDF webs (K9): the route as the smooth union
  of the traveled pieces only.

## Verification

- Same-state crops of every traversed junction kind: 3-ways, 4-ways,
  straight-through and turning routes, the head junction, backtracks and
  trail mode. Pin the paint clock.
- The census against the CPU mirror stays within 2/255. Untraveled
  joints and non-gold paint must not change.
- GPU per program under the perf lock, and the phone gate (K10).

## Scheduling

How it works: per routed record, a ROUTE block per band at `routeBase`
(R0 = nv, m, ia, ib; then nv <= MAX_ROUTE_VERTS vertices x, y, tau, 0:
arm A's centerline in from its band end, the cubic corner, arm B's
centerline out; tau = arc along the band, corner from -d to +d). Each arm
of a routed record writes its composed strip row (stripRow) AND an
untraveled one (stripRow + 1). The fan shader: `routed = nRoutes > 0 &&
hd.z > 0.5`; outside every band the web shows the arms' UNTRAVELED paint
(pulses, hover, glow kept, no gold); inside a band, the band's arms'
COMPOSED paint (A -> B blended over tau in [-m, m]), one-pixel AA edge at
thread half-width. Cover strips (one per routed arm, out to
min(want, coverCap)) redraw the arm's ribbon next to the web with the
same field, so the band continues past the web edge; a strip fragment is
`fract(vRec) > 0.125`, its reach pad is ROUTE_BAND_PAD + 1 px.

### Decisions (list them for Levi; also in junction_routes.ts header)

1. Backtracks: the band follows the path STACK like the gold; a popped
   pass's band disappears. A joint truly passed twice holds two bands,
   newest drawn last; a record keeps the newest MAX_ROUTES = 4 distinct
   arm pairs (a repeated pair is kept once).
2. Head: a one-arm band (arrival arm into the joint center, round end)
   under the K83 marker. Start, exit and dead ends are free tips, not
   junction records: they keep their caps and get no band.
3. A move in flight: both ends carry a band (the gold slides along it).
4. A routed web shows the untraveled material outside the bands.
5. Caps are dropped at routed junctions (the band replaces them).
6. The band reads the arms' composed rows, so it follows K93's and trail
   mode's fades with no clock of its own (the coordinator's K93 rule).
7. Corner: d scales with the turn angle, capped so the turn's centerline
   passes within 0.9 fh of the joint center, stays 0.5 fh inside the
   cover caps and 0.25 fh inside the wedge rim (turnFits bisection). A
   straight route is the two centerlines joined.
8. Width: thread width in webs (centered one-pixel ramp); cover strips
   pad 0.5 px.
