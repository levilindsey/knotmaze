# K488: the finish indicator's tap becomes a peek

One build stage, Sonnet, a one-hour cap. Ledger item: K488. Written from
`docs/design/K465.md` (Handoff, "THE FINDING").

## Levi's words (2026-10-15, relayed in the ledger)

The finish indicator's tap becomes a peek: zoom out to frame the exit,
hold about 2.5 s, ease back, with a label to match.

## Today

- The finish indicator's tap calls `frameWorld` (main.ts): it frames the
  exit WITH the head (pad 1.5, margin 70) and starts a `camTween` with
  `PEEK_HOLD` 1.3. `placeWorldAt` (src/app/camera.ts) clamps the result
  every frame, so the framing rarely survives:
  - fit camera: the scale may not go below the fit frame's scale and
    zoom 1 has no pan range, so at the start of a thread-reveal maze
    (the fit frames only the seen thread, at the 90 px/world cap) the
    tween ends where it began and the exit stays off screen (K465:
    `2gozcj` at 411x753, the exit about 8 viewports away);
  - follow camera: the zoom-out floor (`min(fit, follow) * 0.85`)
    leaves the exit off screen too (K465: s 31.6 -> 26.9, held 2.6 s).
- The label says "Tap to bring it into view", which is untrue in both
  cases above. Under fog the tap only toasts, by design (direction
  only); that stays.

## The work

1. **The peek plan (pure, `src/ui/finish_peek.ts`):** center the exit
   at `min(current scale, followScale)` - never zoom in - with a 0.6 s
   ease out, a 2.5 s hold and a 0.6 s ease back to the exact pre-tap
   framing. Reduced motion jumps (durations 0) but still holds.
   Unit-tested in `test/ui/finish_peek.test.ts`.
2. **The camera (`src/app/camera.ts`):** a `camTween` may be `free`:
   `placeWorldAt` then places the point at the asked scale without the
   fit clamp, and a tween with a `back` target holds at its end for
   `hold` seconds (camera dt) and then tweens back. Gestures still
   cancel any tween (`touchCamera`); an interrupted peek is out of the
   fit range, which the range ease then glides home (the zoom clamps
   at once - noted in the lore). `converged()` already waits on any
   tween, so the frame loop stays awake through the hold.
3. **main.ts:** the finish indicator's tap (not under fog) runs the
   plan; its label becomes "Tap to peek at the exit." `frameWorld`
   remains the minotaur's framing, unchanged, with `PEEK_HOLD`.
4. **Verify:** the full suite and `npm run build`; a probe at 411x753
   and 1400x900 (fit and follow) on a thread-reveal link: the tap
   centers the exit at the planned scale, holds about 2.5 s and eases
   back to the pre-tap view. Screenshots in `.claude/probes/K488/`.
5. **Lore:** gameplay-indicators.md (the finish's tap and label; the
   K465 residual closes) and gameplay-camera.md (the free tween).
