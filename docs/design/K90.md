# K90 brief: a physical minimum bend radius (stage 1)

Levi, 2026-10-13, answering G3's choice between a filled lobe and a pinch
at over-tight folds: "Pinching is probably better, I think? But I may be
misunderstanding. But, ideally we'd have a more physics-based constraint
to prevent loops from becoming too tight in the first place?"

## Goal

A thread never folds tighter than half its rendered width, so the
renderer's fold fallbacks (today's concave-side miter clamp; G3's pinch,
K8) have nothing left to do. Stage 1 measures how often and where folds
go over-tight today, and prototypes a constraint that prevents them
without a pump. Shipping is stage 2, after Levi has seen the numbers.

## What exists (read first)

- `applyTurnRadiusFloor` (`src/physics/particle_system.ts`, ~2594) runs
  inside the constraint loop at R = 1.15 x half width, stiffness 0.5: a
  per-triple chord floor on the particle polyline. Full authority was
  tried in P3.34 and pinned a flat 3.4e-2 pump for 3+ minutes on a
  wounded tangle; a fold the tangle squeezes cannot open unless the
  tangle yields (`.claude/rules/physics.md`, the P3.34 helpers entry).
- The ribbon draws a centripetal Catmull-Rom through the particles,
  resampled by arc length (`sampleClosedSpline` / `sampleOpenSpline`,
  `ribbon_renderer.ts` ~2263). The spline can turn tighter than the
  polyline, so measure what the ribbon extrudes, not the particles.
- The concave-side miter clamp (`.claude/rules/render.md`) is the
  correctness layer today; physics floors were aesthetics.
- Kink eyes: kinkSep = max(2.6, 6.2 x threadWidth) ctrl, the rest-material
  eye ratchet, across-eye pressure and the fairing exemption hold loops
  open (physics.md, KINK EYES). Same-chain collision skips +-2 ctrl, so
  collision cannot see folds shorter than that. The ctrl metric cannot see
  compressed segments either (53% of rest measured inside a squeezed eye).

## Stage 1a: census

- Metric: the minimum turn radius of the rendered centerline, per chain,
  as a fraction of the ribbon half-width. Report the count and location
  of samples below 1.0 (the strip self-overlaps) and below 1.15 (the
  floor's target), and what holds each fold: a kink eye, a crossing
  clamp, a collision squeeze, a junction, an anchored tail or a grab.
- States, separately (fresh, at rest after a small perturbation, and
  after long use fail differently): a fresh settle at rest; during and
  after the gesture battery (`.claude/rules/probes.md`, P3.40 recipe);
  after violent play (the K63 harness workloads, `.claude/probes/K63/`);
  knot mode and maze mode; default, fat and thin widths; the P3.31-P3.34
  user link (`docs/history.md`).
- Where variants are compared, freeze one settled state and re-run the
  variants from it.

## Stage 1b: prototype

Evaluate, in this order:
1. Material, not position: an under-radius fold gets arc length (a
   rest-length ratchet, as the kink-eye ratchet does), so it opens by
   having enough thread rather than by pushing against the tangle.
2. A one-sided, rate-limited bend constraint over a 3-5 particle window,
   active only below R_min, with a per-substep correction cap. A clamp
   that raises a floor against unknown geometry needs a rate limit, or
   some seed turns it into a pump that never sleeps.
3. Spline-side: if the census shows the folds come from Catmull-Rom
   overshoot while the polyline is fine, the fix belongs in Layer 2.

Whatever is chosen must yield when the geometry cannot give: a
constraint that demands the impossible is a pump by construction.

## Guards (all must hold)

- Sleep: `test/physics/sleep.regression.test.ts` and the K63 battery
  (`.claude/probes/K63/`): every seed still sleeps, at about the same sim
  time.
- `test/quiescence.regression.test.ts` (the loud-attractor guard).
- Kink-eye sizes: the K41 node census (`.claude/probes/K41/`) and K49's
  in-page `kinkCensusPage` (`.claude/probes/K49/lib.mjs`). Eyes must not
  shrink.
- The gesture battery re-sleeps.
- The knot-mode golden (`test/physics/byte_identity.test.ts`): if the
  constraint never activates on the golden's state, the golden stays
  byte-identical. If it moves, report why and by how much; never
  regenerate it silently.
- Physics step cost, measured under the perf lock.
