that Stage 1 gives the hover.

## Stage 1 (K93): the trail fade and the hover's reach

- **The trail fade** (trail mode, `trl`). Since K4 the fade is linear,
  and a span's paint position p is linear between its two ends, so the
  run that holds the fade start fades all the way from the head junction
  instead of from the fade start. Split that span in `buildTrail`
  (`src/render/fabric_maze_renderer.ts`): solid gold up to the fade
  start, then linear. Render lore: `.claude/rules/render.md` (the G2
  entry's K75 passage: "K4's linear fade widened it ...").
- **The hover's reach.** The hover on a choice run (a run leaving the
  player's junction) stops at `pulseReach` along the run and fades out
  toward that end, as the pulses do (K75 made `pulseReach`).
- Gold mode does not change: a same-state A/B gives 0 px.

## Stage 2 (K145): the hover glow (a later launch)

- Hovering a run that is not a choice at the player's junction lights a
  subtle glow along both edges of the thread, inside its bounds, fading
  quickly toward the center line. It fades with distance along the thread
  from the hover point, out to `pulseReach`, and toward the run's
  junctions. The choices keep their stronger hover and get no glow.
- A touch press shows it too, until the finger lifts (the tap-pluck
  still fires on release) or the press becomes a tug or a pan.
- What exists: the pointer move in `src/main.ts` asks `fabric.edgeAt`,
  which picks only choosable runs; `setHover` tracks one run; a touch
  press on a thread sets `pendingPluck` with no hover at all. The glow is
  a new G2 paint term plus its `paint_math` mirror (the hovered run and
  the hover point's arc position), measured along the thread like the
  pulses.
- A strength param (0 = off), default on and subtle, appended to the
  live codec table (`CODEC_PARAMS`, K132) and added to the daily's
  exempt preferences (`DAILY_EXEMPT_KEYS`; Levi reviews that list later,
  K57).
- Verification: the mirror's parity tests and a census; at strength 0,
  0 px against master; a sheet of the glow under the mouse and under a
  touch press; the paint shader's GPU time at rest and mid-hover, under
  the perf lock.
