# K580 stage 5: the glyph tuck waist and the tapered a's J

Ledger: K580 (stage 5). One stage, opus, 2 h cap. Stage 4 rounded every
glyph cusp (K675) and showed the real render covers the raster's
crescents. Two bites remain, both measured with `real.mjs`. Read
these sections of `docs/design/K580_s4.md`'s Handoff first: "The real
render", "Defect 1, the i's joint" and "Residuals". The design is
`docs/design/K580.md`.

## Decided

- tp52 stays, and the taper does not change.
- **The target is stage 3's, measured with `real.mjs` against U** (the
  untucked full stroke from J), and still against T (the tucked ideal):
  - no bite deeper than 0.01 LU;
  - no spike more than 0.01 LU outside the ideal (`taperBulgeLU`);
  - at xh 2 and xh 3, tapered and notaper, "a" and "ai".
- Scenes without glyph text render 0 px different: knot, maze, reveal and
  burn. Maze bridges need the tuck, so any change is glyph-only.
- **Don't retry centring the junction round piece at J.** Stage 4 built
  and measured it (`real_s4_exp/`): no change at xh 2 and worse at xh 3.

## The defects

1. **The tuck waist.** It is 0.017-0.030 LU against U, at three places:
   - the i's joint: 0.027 / 0.018 LU (xh 2 / 3);
   - every stem foot: 0.028-0.030 at xh 2, 0.014-0.018 at xh 3;
   - the notaper J: 0.023-0.024 / 0.016-0.017.

   The cause is `tuckFusedTips` (`ribbon_renderer.ts`, about line 4299).
   It starts the spur flat at T, 0.75h up its own stroke. The cap cover is
   a 1.06h disc at T, and the body's disc sits at J. The waist is the gap
   between the two discs.
   - Inferred: this is the stem notch the Pixel showed in K662.
2. **The tapered a's J.** It reads 0.031 / 0.035 LU for "a" (xh 2 / 3) and
   0.059 / 0.034 for "ai", against both ideals. The cap cover is skipped
   there (`taperHideJn`).
   - ai's excess over "a" at xh 2 comes from the centre rule (stage 4's
     experiment).
   - The rest of the bite is not attributed.
3. **The notaper J paint seam.** The stills show a lighter disc and a
   wedge outline. Possibly K678 or K620; not attributed.

## Steps

1. **Reproduce.**
   - Copy stage 4's kit from the main checkout's `.claude/probes/K580/`
     into your worktree's `.claude/probes/K580/s5/`: `real.mjs`,
     `real_table.mjs`, `run_real_exp.ps1`, `run_s4.ps1`, `s4_sheet.ps1`,
     `static.mjs` and `serve.mjs`, plus whatever those import.
   - Re-run `real.mjs` on your base (master with stage 4) for the before
     numbers, against U and against T.
2. **The waist (defect 1).** Build stage 4's rec.
   - **Rec: a glyph-only stadium cap cover.** A capsule from T back to J,
     radius 0.995h, clamped into the spur's strip the way today's disc is.
   - **The alternative:** no tuck at glyph spur tips.
   - Either one needs a per-chain glyph flag that notaper mode has too
     (`taperJn` exists only in tapered mode). The glyph graph
     (`glyph_graph.ts`, `src/core/glyphs/`) sets it, and the renderer reads
     it.
   - Measure both routes if the first misses the target. Keep the better
     one.
3. **The tapered a's J (defect 2).**
   - Find why `taperHideJn` skips the cap cover there, and what draws the
     bite.
   - Fix it inside the same glyph-only scope, or stop and report the fix
     you would make, with the measurement.
4. **The paint seam (defect 3).**
   - Attribute it with a tint or a toggle on one frozen frame.
   - If the fix is in your files, make it. Otherwise report it with the
     crop.
5. **Tests.** Write unit tests for the flag (which chains carry it, in
   tapered and notaper) and for the cover's geometry (the capsule's ends
   and radius, and the clamp).
6. **Verify.**
   - **The A/B.** Same-state, with K550's
     `.claude/probes/K550/kill/ab_probe.mjs`: master against your branch,
     knot, maze, reveal and burn, at dsf 1 and 2, with the same-build
     control. Expect 0 px everywhere.
   - **The numbers.** `real.mjs` and `real_table.mjs` against U and T at
     every stage 4 window.
   - **The sheet.** Re-shoot stage 4's four sites, master beside your
     branch, as `sheets/s5_before_after.png`.
   - **The suite.** `npm test` and `npm run build`.
   - **The bench.** If code in `RibbonMesh.update()` or the per-frame
     renderer path changes, re-run stage 3's `bench/bench.ts`.
7. **Lore.** Put the fused spur tip entry in
   `.claude/rules/render-ribbon.md`, or wherever stage 4 wrote it, and the
   glyph flag in the glyph lore.
