## The defects

1. **The tuck waist.** It is 0.017-0.030 LU against U, at three places:
   - the i's joint: 0.027 / 0.018 LU (xh 2 / 3);
   - every stem foot: 0.028-0.030 at xh 2, 0.014-0.018 at xh 3;
   - the notaper J: 0.023-0.024 / 0.016-0.017.

   The cause is `tuckFusedTips` (`ribbon_renderer.ts`, about line 4299).
   It starts the spur flat at T, 0.75h up its own stroke. The cap cover is
   a 1.06h disc at T, and the body's disc sits at J. The waist is the gap
   between the two discs.
   - Inferred: this is the stem notch the Pixel showed in K662.
2. **The tapered a's J.** It reads 0.031 / 0.035 LU for "a" (xh 2 / 3) and
   0.059 / 0.034 for "ai", against both ideals. The cap cover is skipped
   there (`taperHideJn`).
   - ai's excess over "a" at xh 2 comes from the centre rule (stage 4's
     experiment).
   - The rest of the bite is not attributed.
3. **The notaper J paint seam.** The stills show a lighter disc and a
   wedge outline. Possibly K678 or K620; not attributed.
