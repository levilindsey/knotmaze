# K74 brief: what makes a good maze, measured against ours

Levi, 2026-10-12:

> "In general, we need to take a step back and think about good maze
> design. Please do some research. One of the things that comes to mind
> is that there shouldn't be too many junctions near the finish,
> otherwise it's too easy to find a path through by just moving naiively
> in the most direct direction to the end. After understanding
> characteristics that lead to good maze design, I want you to research
> and plan possible ways that we could alter our maze formulation to
> achieve some of these good design characteristics.
>
> A related consideration is our dead ends. They are less stubby, but
> still too stubby in general. We should do something to ensure longer
> dead end paths with no junctions--possibly with a junction-removal pass
> after?"

And later: "it looks like adjusting some of these 'Maze' params do give
maze characteristics that are probably 'better maze design'. But lmk."

## Work

1. **Research.** Cover:
   - maze texture (Pullen's Think Labyrinth; Buck's *Mazes for
     Programmers*);
   - difficulty and complexity studies (McClendon, and solver-based
     metrics);
   - puzzle and level-design writing (decision density, decoys, a
     solution that moves away from the goal, resisting "head for the
     exit");
   - braided mazes versus perfect ones.

   Keep the sources.
2. **Measure our mazes** with a vite-node harness in `.claude/probes/K74/`
   (modeled on K46 and K63): 50+ designs at the defaults and the DAILY_V1
   days, `deadEnds` on, and K46 recipes. Measure:
   - junction density against distance to the exit and the entrance;
   - greedy "head for the exit" walkers (solve rate, cost vs optimal);
   - solution-path decisions and away-from-goal stretches;
   - dead-end junction-free lengths.

   FIRST, sweep the existing knobs: junctionSpacing, exitClearance,
   entranceClearance, branchDensity, fourWayFraction, deadEnds.
3. **Plan formulation changes.** Candidates:
   - exit-region junction thinning;
   - decoys toward the exit;
   - forcing away-from-goal stretches;
   - a junction-removal pass for long dead ends.

   For each, give the mechanism, a measured prototype, risks (planarity,
   alternation, physics) and cost.
   - **Binding:** generation changes move every daily and every link.
     Ship behind appended codec params whose defaults reproduce today's
     maze, or a DAILY_V2 from a future day. Relate each option to K46.

## Deliverable

`docs/design/K74_maze_design.md`, with conclusions and the decisions for

## Facts

- The plan works in DOUBLED lattice units (one cell = 2).
- `clearOf` (maze_plan.ts) keeps choice junctions, 4-way merges and
  dead-end cuts outside entranceClearance / exitClearance. The spanning
  backbone only prefers clear sites.
- pickFarthest places splices first, then 4-ways, under junctionSpacing.
  At spacing 2 the floor saturates, which is why branchDensity and
  fourWayFraction do nothing there (K62).
