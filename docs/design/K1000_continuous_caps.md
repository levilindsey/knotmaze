# K1000 stage 1: continuous caps

Ledger item: K1000 (knotmaze `LEDGER.md`); K992 waits on it. K992's
briefs are on this branch:
- stage 2, `docs/design/K992_s2_continuous_stops.md`: read its
  `## Coordinator's calls`, G2, G4 and `## Handoff` first;
- stage 1, `docs/design/K992_corner_rules.md`: read its `## Rules`, its
  findings on the caps and its `## Handoff`.
Read the rest only as needed.

## Why

Stage 2's c17f made the corner rule's own stops continuous: its switch
and fold pops fell to 0, and its pop area in the windows is 0.30 of
master's. It still fails the gate:
- at zoom 1 on muse_drag, 163 pops against master's 77. Its first solve
  tips the cut loop at j100: arm A's cap at j100/2 jumps between 1.186h
  and 3.822h, and the corner between round and sharp, on 12 frames;
- in muse_drag@w2 and muse_flow@w0 on count, 990 and 731 pops against
  879 and 659, mostly small ones.

The caps jump whenever the cut loop adds or removes a cut (j116/2,
j139/2 and j105/1 on master). Master's sharp corners hide much of that.
A round corner shows every jump, because its ball follows its cap: in
c17f's windows the cap class is 121 pops and 111803 px, a third of its
area. No continuous corner rule can pass the gate until the caps are
continuous too.

## Coordinator's calls (technical)

1. **The gate counts every pop, whatever its class.** Stage 1's gate
   (its call 6) stands as written.
   - The classes name the nearest event, not the cause. c17f moved about
     600 pops in muse_drag@w2 between spans, reach and none, and the
     window's total barely moved (990 against c17's 986).
   - Class 1 being out of K992's scope says where the work goes. It does
     not excuse the pops a rule adds through the caps.
2. **The cap is a pure function of the frame,** as the label is.
   - It is continuous and monotone in the geometry by construction, read
     by its test (stage 2's call 2).
   - No hysteresis, no state carried between frames, no smoothing in
     time.
3. **A cut that appears or leaves moves the cap continuously.** Every
   choice the cut loop makes (a cut's side, `fa >= fb`, whether a pass
   counts as a cut, which pass caps an arm) does one of two things:
   - it becomes a measure that moves the cap from one answer to the other
     over a span of geometry;
   - or the geometry is shown to cross it only where both answers give
     the same cap.
4. **Scope: the caps.**
   - V's folds and live/unsolved flips (class 1: V, 22 pops in c17f's
     windows) stay in K1000 for a later stage.
   - The corner rule is not tuned here. Test with c17f as `mk_c17.mjs`
     generates it.
5. **Looks.** A continuous cap should change no shape at rest, except
   near the point where a cut appears. If it does change a corner's look
   somewhere:
   - crop master's caps against the new caps;
   - send the coordinator the path (SendMessage to `main`) with one line
     per crop;
   - go on without waiting.

## Order

1. **Trace the jumps.** One finding per site (H1, H2, ...): which
   decision in the cut loop flips, on what quantity, and why the cap is
   discontinuous across it.
   - **Sites on master:** j116/2; j139/2, muse_drag t21 to t22 (arm A's
     cap moves from crossing 292's pass at 0.607 to the next junction at
     0.903); j105/1.
   - **Site under c17f:** j100/2, muse_drag t4 to t5 (crossing 216's pass
     at 1.186h against crossing 348's pass at 3.822h). The suspect is the
     side choice `fa >= fb` (stage 2 G4, not traced). `cap16.ts muse_drag
     4 j100/2 --k 2 --oracles b16,c17,c17f` is the starting point.
   - **The spans:** why c17f changes a pass's spans on so many more
     frames (stage 2 G4: 1813 spans pops in the windows, against c17's
     451; the largest at spans keys 66, 67, 45 and 84). If it is the cut
     loop, it is in scope. If not, say what it is and leave it.
2. **Continuous caps.**
   - Rewrite the decisions from step 1 so the cap passes calls 2 and 3.
     State the rule in one sentence per decision.
   - Add a cap continuity test, in the style of
     `corner_continuity.test.ts`. It sweeps through each traced site's
     flip, and through a synthetic pass sliding into an arm.
   - Sweep the traced sites and stage 1's 20 sites, under master's rule
     and under c17f.
3. **The census.** Run it under the perf lock, at zoom 1 and in the ten
   windows, twice:
   - master's rule on the new caps;
   - c17f on the new caps, generated by `mk_c17.mjs` from the new caps.
   Attribute both by class (`class17.ts`).
4. **If master's rule on the new caps is no worse than master** (no more
   pops per transition and no more pop area, at zoom 1 and in every
   window):
   - land the caps in `src/render/field/oracle.ts`, with the cap test
     green;
   - report c17f's census on them, but do not land c17f. That needs
     `mk_c17.mjs --src`, which is the next stage.
5. **If c17f on the new caps still fails a window on count,** take ten
   of that window's excess small pops (under 50 px). For each, say which
   structure's ink changed, pixel by pixel, as `crops17.ts` shows them.
   Report; don't fix.
6. **Stop.**
