Branch `worktree-agent-ab4debcc50d9ea9fe`, rebased on master 3043b9a9 (K1283's corner fix). Commits:

**What the strips and wedges were.** Two faults in fill_tri's s (the paint's position along the thread).
Neither involves weights or runs:
- A pieceless strip (no table piece on a side, between two adjacent junctions) is a line between the two
  rings, often inverted and drawn over both fans. Its s was spread linearly between the two junctions' s,
  so it ran against the positions.
- A fan mouth took its strip's end s, which is the piece's end s. Where a border runs into a sharp corner,
  that is the junction's s, even when the mouth vertex sits far along the thread. Example: def j168
  arm 2, whose N is about 0.8 from the hub with s pinned at 6.623.

Together they held the paint at the junction's colour up to the mouth, where it jumped 26-55 levels. The
results were the strips across def_j156, def_j16 and def_j14, and the wedge at muse_default_tw j150.

**The fix** (`fill_tri.ts` after the strip column loop, plus `draw.ts`'s `footNear`).
- On pieceless rows and on strip end columns at junctions, s is now the vertex's foot on its own chain.
  The search runs within 4 hW of the old s, and on a closed chain the result stays on the old s's lap.
- When only one side is pieceless, the centre row takes the mean of its two borders.
- The fans read sP/sM/sN from these end columns, so they inherit the fix.
- Only s4 moves. `k1278s2_ident.ts` shows positions, keys, weights, across and runs identical to master
  on all 12 scenes. On def, 41300 of 969496 slots changed (max |ds| 0.75); on fat, none.
