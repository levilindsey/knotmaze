## Cards

- Keep the card schema, which `build_decisions.mjs` checks:
  - id;
  - headline, a question of 90 characters or fewer;
  - stakes: blocks, open, confirm or none;
  - context, in plain paragraphs;
  - today;
  - parts: q, options, and rec (an option's label);
  - tryIt (https links);
  - images.
- **A "built as recommended" item** is `stakes: confirm`.
  - The rec option is what was built.
  - The other options are the alternatives.
  - Say what changing it would cost.

- **An open decision** gets the options, the recommendation and why, what
  each option changes, and what it costs.

    act.
