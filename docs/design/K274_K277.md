### Every line written (for Levi to review)

K274, the `STATS` table in `src/game/maze_stats.ts` (row label, then line):
- **time**: "From your first move to the end of the run. Time the page spends
  hidden does not count."
- **path / optimal**: "The length of the route you ended on, then of the
  shortest route from the entrance to the exit, in the game's own units. A
  run you backed out of is not part of your route."
- **efficiency**: "The shortest route divided by the route you ended on,
  rounded down. Backing out of a wrong turn erases it from your route, so a
  wander you walked back can still read 100%, while the stars count every
  step. With the memory trail on nothing is erased."
- **backtracked**: "The total length of the runs you walked back out of,
  added up over the attempt. The stars count each of them twice, out and
  back."
- **junctions**: "How many different junctions you have reached. Coming back
  to one does not count it again, and the entrance and exit are not
  junctions."
- **dead ends**: "How many different dead ends you have reached. The
  entrance and the exit are not dead ends."
- **caught**: "How many times the minotaur has caught you. Each catch sends
  you back to the entrance. The walk before it still counts toward the
  stars, and any catch caps them at two."
- **chased**: "How long the minotaur spent hunting you, added up over the
  run. A hunt starts when you come close enough along the threads for it to
  notice you, and ends when you get clear of it, it catches you or you
  finish."
- **fire lead**: "How much further you can walk before the fire reaches the
  junction you stand at. The fire spreads only as you walk, backing up
  included."
- **closest call**: "The smallest fire lead you had on any arrival, the exit
  included. The nearer to 0, the closer the fire came."
- **walked**: "Every run you walked this attempt, forward and back. The
  fire's clock runs on it, not on time."
- `BEST_TIME_LINE` (the stats view's legend, "best time"): "The fastest time
  among your official results. Replays and practice do not count."
