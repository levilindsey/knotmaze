## Decided

### Everywhere

- **Params.** Each mechanism is a param that defaults off, appended after
  the last param on master at your rebase.
  - Pick short keys that no param uses (the design's `lu` may be taken).
  - Every default is off, so the dailies, the golden pins, K87, byte
    identity, `K80_DIGESTS`, `MODEL_DIGESTS`, `TURN_GAMES` and
    `LENGTH_GAMES` must not move. Re-freeze only the key-order pin in
    `test/ui/param_codec.test.ts`.
  - With a mechanism off, its code path is bit-identical to today's.
- **Free play only.** No saved attempts and no store version.
- **A chase must be on** for sleepers, the follower, lures and snares:
  they run on the chase's clock with its catch rules. Their panel rows go
  in the minotaur's group, shown only while a chase is on. Muddy runs work
  with or without a chase.
- **The HUD, the banner and the share.** A row for each mechanism that is
  on, with its help line in `maze_stats.ts`. Share tags from the design,
  shown only when the mechanism is on: "😴 2 woken", "🎵 2 lures",
  "🪢 1". The follower and muddy runs add none.
- **Probe hooks.** Add every monster's state to `__knotChase()`: kind,
  phase (asleep, waiting, awake, held), position and next run. Add the
  lures and snares left, the snared runs and the muddy runs.

### 1. More than one monster

- **Generalize the chase to a list of monsters.** Each one has its own
  state, its own draw stream on the seed (as the minotaur's `TurnRng`
  streams do, so no other draw shifts) and its own marker. The minotaur
  is the first.
  - With no sleepers and no follower the list holds the minotaur alone,
    and every pin stays put.
- **A catch by any monster** is today's catch, plus lives when they are
  on. Rec: a catch puts every monster back at its start, and sleepers
  fall asleep again (the minotaur's CATCH RESETS). List it as a call.
- **Replays.** `replay(taps)` and `replayChase` rebuild every monster from
  the same taps.
- **The telegraph** (K324) shows the minotaur only. Rec: extra monsters
  get no telegraph this stage. List it as a call.
- **Markers.** Each monster is drawn through the K83 marker path
  (`marker_glyphs.ts`, `marker_mesh.ts`), at its own size and glow.

### 2. Sleepers (4.9)

- `sleepers`: 0 (off) to 3.
- **Where they sleep.** Deterministic junctions, 1-3 runs off the optimal
  route, outside the minotaur's lair and territory, never next to the
  start, drawn on their own stream.
- **Waking.** A sleeper wakes when you move along a run that touches its
  junction, or on a lure pluck in hearing range (step 4).
- **Once awake,** a sleeper is a hunter on the chase's clock, with the
  minotaur's pace and catch distance, so the minotaur's guarantees apply.
- **The look.** A dim glyph with a slow breathing glow while it sleeps;
  awake, it takes the hunter's look at a smaller size.
- **The share:** "😴 2 woken".

### 3. Levi's follower

- `sleepKind` (wake | follow | both), shown while `sleepers` is above 0.
  - wake: every sleeper is a 4.9 sleeper.
  - follow: every sleeper is a follower.
  - both: alternate, drawn on their stream.
- **What a follower does.** It does not wake when you pass. It appears
  at its junction 1 or 2 turns after you leave it (drawn per follower),
  then walks your trail behind you.
  - Walking back into it is a catch.
  - On real time it walks your trail at the minotaur's pace, starting 1
    or 2 of your runs behind.
- **The variance.** Rec: besides the delay, at each junction there is a
  25% chance (on its stream) that it takes its shortest route to you for
  one step instead of your trail. That lets it close the gap
  unpredictably. List the rule and the chance as a call.
- **The look.** Rec: it sleeps under the same glyph as a 4.9 sleeper, so
  under `both` you know a junction is risky but not how. The alternative
  is a hidden follower that is not drawn until it appears. List it as a
  call.

### 4. Lure plucks (4.10)

- `lures`: 0 (off) to 5. That is the stock for an attempt, and a level
  restart refills it.
- **The gesture.** A lure button arms the next tap. Build it from the
  scissors component, beside sprint's button, plus a free key
  (`src/app/input_keys.ts`). The tap plucks that run, with its sound and
  the pluck overlay, and uses one lure.
- **Hearing.** Rec: a monster hears a lure within 6 runs of walk distance.
  - A hunter or the roaming minotaur makes the plucked run its next
    destination. It reverts to its model on arrival.
  - A sleeper in range wakes.
  - Rec: a follower ignores lures.
  - A lure nobody hears still uses a lure, and a toast says "Nothing
    heard it."

  List the range and the follower rule as calls.
- **Time.** Rec: a lure is free in time, so no turn passes. The design's
  other option is that it costs a move. List it as a call.
- **The telegraph** shows the lured route, since `plan()` follows the
  destination.
- **The share:** "🎵 2 lures".

### 5. Snares (4.30)

- `snares`: 0 (off) to 3, the stock for an attempt.
- **The gesture.** A snare button (the same component) arms the next tap,
  which places a snare on a run touching the head's junction. That
  includes the run behind you. A knot glyph sits on the run.
- **The hold.** The first monster to enter the run is held for 3 of its
  turns, then the snare is spent.
  - Real time: rec, held until you have walked 3 runs. That matches the
    sword's rule in stage 3a. List it as a call.
- **The share:** "🪢 1".

### 6. Muddy runs (4.33)

- `mud`: 0 (off) to 6 runs.
- **Where.** Runs on the optimal route, drawn on their own stream, never
  the start or exit tail. That way the fewest-moves route and the
  shortest route differ.
- **Cost.** Crossing one counts as two moves for everyone.
  - You: one tap. The dot crosses at half speed, and strokes and par
    count it twice (par's solver in `src/game/par.ts`).
  - The minotaur: two turns on the turn clock, half speed on real time.
  - Stars keep the length metric (K155) unchanged.
- **The look.** The design's look is a fuzzy, wider thread. If that
  needs the paint shader (`paint_glsl.ts` with its CPU mirror and census),
  build a cheap stand-in instead: a brown tint through the per-run colour
  path, or the K83 marker path. Then file the fuzzy look as a ledger item
  for stage 4's paint pair.
