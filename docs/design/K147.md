# K147: a smooth intro on load

Levi, 2026-10-13 (verbatim in `docs/design/levi_notes_2026-10-13.md`):
"Hmm, I notice when loading the page, the animations run at a very low
framerate. I assume it's when things sleep and physics calms down that
things tick way back up. Should we show a loading indicator during the
initial physics simulation, and then only render the maze screen and
initial animaitons after physics calms-down?"

Levi is right about when it recovers. This brief is the coordinator's
answer: fix the cause, and keep Levi's version one setting away so they can
compare.

## Diagnosis

The stepper keeps physics on real time. `ParticleSystem.step(dt)`
(`src/physics/particle_system.ts`, about line 1660) adds
`min(dt, MAX_FRAME_DT)` (0.25 s) to an accumulator and runs
`floor(acc / SUBSTEP)` substeps of 1/120 s, at most
`MAX_SUBSTEPS_PER_FRAME` (30). The live frame (`src/main.ts`, about line
2890) feeds it `(ticker.deltaMS / 1000) * speedMult`.

Call c the wall cost of one substep and r the rest of the frame (render,
paint, fabric). A frame that runs n substeps lasts n·c + r, and the next
frame asks for (n·c + r) / SUBSTEP substeps. The count settles at
n* = r / (SUBSTEP - c): fine while c is small, large as c nears 8.3 ms,
and with no fixed point once c >= SUBSTEP, when every frame runs the cap.
A settling knot is the expensive case (all of it moving, every contact
live), so the intro, which plays over the settle, gets the worst frames;
the K63 sleep gate then stops stepping and the frame rate jumps back.
Check whether Pixi's ticker caps `deltaMS` (its `minFPS`): that sets the
effective cap, so the arithmetic above depends on it.

Recorded (`docs/history.md`, 2026-10-11, gs 18): physics took
150-190 ms a frame, so the intro ran at about 5 fps for about 14 s on a
loaded desktop. The physics-off path, which settles behind a loader,
took about 5 s on the desktop and about 26 s at 4x CPU throttle. Agents
loading the machine make it worse; a phone hits it regardless.

What does not depend on the stepping: the reveal ticks on wall time
(`reveal.tick(ticker.deltaMS / 1000)`, about line 2736), the solve clock
starts when the intro ends (about lines 1738-1757), and the sleep gate
counts simulated time (about line 2915).

## Options

- (a) Levi's: settle behind a loader, then show the maze and the intro.
  Smooth, but a blank wait (the 5 s and 26 s above; a design that never
  sleeps, K136 or K13's attractor, waits for `LOAD_WALL_CAP_MS`, 45 s),
  and the hero relaxation (CLAUDE.md, "Key Design Decisions") is never
  seen. The machinery exists: the physics-off loader (`beginLoading`,
  `loadingTick`, `finishLoading`, about lines 794-850).
- (b) Wait for K7's worker (K28's choice). The right long-term fix, but
  far down the order.
- (c) Chosen, an interim before K7: a per-frame physics budget while the
  load settle lasts, and the hidden pre-sim moved onto the chunked
  loader with a cap. With the cap at its max, (c) is (a).

## The change

1. **A per-frame physics budget during the load settle.** At most B ms
   of physics a frame, and no backlog: cap the dt fed to `ps.step` at
   what the budget affords, from a running estimate of the cost per
   substep, so the accumulator never builds a debt. Progress needs at
   least one substep a frame, or one every k frames when a single
   substep blows the budget (your call, measured: physics moving on
   alternate frames may judder). The settle then plays out in slow
   motion while the intro animates at the render's rate. Keep
   `ps.step`'s semantics unchanged (the byte-identity golden and every
   test that calls it must stay byte-identical) and put the budget
   arithmetic in a pure, unit-tested helper. It applies from the intro's
   start until the first sleep, with a wall cap so a design that never
   sleeps does not stay in slow motion (pick it by measurement); after
   that, today's stepping. It composes with `speedMult`.
2. **The hidden settle on the loader.** Today `startAppearance(true)`
   (about lines 756-790) runs a blocking pre-sim, up to
   `PRE_SIM_SECONDS` (1.2) of sim or `PRE_SIM_WALL_MS` (450) of wall,
   with the page frozen. Run it in the physics-off path's chunks instead,
   capped by a param, with no loader flash: show the trefoil only once
   the settle will run past about 300 ms, and then for at least about
   400 ms. At the param's max, the intro waits for the first sleep (or
   `LOAD_WALL_CAP_MS`): Levi's settle-first. Default about today's,
   unless the measurements argue for more.
3. **Params.** Names and ranges are yours, for example `settleBudget`
   (ms of physics a frame; 0 = off, today's real-time catch-up) and
   `loadSettle` (seconds the loader may settle before the intro; max =
   until the knot sleeps). Neutral values turn each off (K74's rule).
   Append them to codec v4: K74 stage 1 took index 71 and K79 stage B
   has ten appends waiting (72-81 once it rebases), so whichever merges
   second renumbers. Propose them daily-exempt (the exempt set in
   `src/ui/daily_link.ts`, K57) so a viewer's choice never leaves the
   daily; the golden dailies must stay green. Put them in the panel's
   intro group, with tooltips.
4. **Probes.** `scripts/diag_screenshot.mjs` reports sim/wall seconds
   and expects them to match; with the budget they differ until the
   settle ends. Make it report the lag instead of flagging it, and note
   that in `.claude/rules/probes.md`.

Untouched: the physics-off path (its probes check that physics off adds
no `ps.step` calls), K47's resume, the sleep gate.
