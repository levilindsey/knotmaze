# K1179 S1d: a round pinned at V, in closed form, offline (K1184 Run H)

The rule under test is Claude's rethink of Run L's record
(`docs/design/K1179_s1c.md`, `docs/design/K1178_muse_batch16.md` Run L,
`docs/design/K1179_geometry_review.md` section 7): no medial branch at
all. Per corner form B makes (the same cyclically adjacent arm pairs;
style, `cr` and `h` as form B has them):

- V and θ exactly as `solveCorner` computes them (the production path
  here finds V by S1c's pair iteration over the arm windows; over the
  12 scenes and the recordings its V differs from the built corner's
  by at most 5.8e-13h, so the measurement columns, which read the
  built corner's V, measure the same point).
- `L = style·(cr/3)·min(1.5h, 2h·cot(θ/2))` — form B's straight-arm
  touch distance `r0·cot(θ/2)` from V.
- Gates evaluated once at the provisional feet `L` from V along each
  arm's exact edge, not iterated: `g = ss(slack/0.5h)·ss((den/den(V)
  − 0.3)/0.4)`, slack the smaller of the two arms' `L_arm − W −
  d_foot` at the provisional feet, `den = 1 + n_a·n_b` from the edges'
  centre-to-foot normals at the provisional feet, `den(V)` the same
  at V. No root gate, no margin gate (variant ii adds the margin).
- Feet `F_a`, `F_b` at arc length `L·g` from V along each arm's exact
  edge (offset segments and vertex arcs as `edgeOutlinePoints` traces
  them, in `.claude/probes/K1179/s1d/lib_s1d.ts` `walkEdge`).
- The round: the rational quadratic `F_a → F_b` whose control point C
  is where the edges' tangent lines at the feet meet (travel at A is
  inward along arm A, at B outward along arm B), weight
  `w = cos(φ/2)`, φ the turn between those travel tangents. On
  straight arms this is exactly form B's circle. Where the lines do
  not meet on the fill side, or C lies farther than 4h from V, the
  fallback cubic with handles along the tangents of length
  `0.55·|F_a F_b|` is used and counted.
- The fill: the region bounded by the round and the two edges from
  each foot back to V.

Variant (ii) adds the margin gate: for every other centreline piece
within `h + L` of the round's points at t = 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 (other
arms' tubes, and arms a and b beyond their feet; a piece within 0.5h
of a foot along its arm is the foot's own contact and is skipped, as
form B's ω skips it), m is the least centreline distance less h;
`L·g` is scaled by `ss(1 + min(0, m)/0.05h)`, evaluated once on
variant (i)'s round and not iterated.

Offline in Node (`npx.cmd vite-node`), no GPU, browser, server or
phone. Nothing in `src/` or `test/` changed; Run L's probes and
outputs are unchanged. Probe code: `.claude/probes/K1179/s1d/`
(`lib_s1d.ts` the rule and its measurement, `s1d_scenes.ts`,
`s1d_recordings.ts`, `s1d_sweeps.ts`, `s1d_cost.ts`, `s1d_plots.ts`);
outputs in `.claude/probes/K1179/s1d/out/`. Inputs are Run L's: the
12 scenes, every distinct frame of the 4 recordings (41 each), the
four sweeps, master's form B settings.

Most work a corner can take (task 1): no warm start, no tracking, no
Newton, no bisection in the rule. V takes at most 24 pair iterations,
each two window scans of O(nA + nB) in the windows' point counts
(windows are built once per arm per frame). Each foot walk visits the
exact outline's pieces over at most `L + 2h ≤ 3.5h` of edge: the
centreline segments that span touches plus one arc step (0.01 rad) per
arc step of each vertex arc in it. The gates are O(1) beside two
nearest-foot reads in the walked span. Variant (ii) tests, at three
round points, the centreline pieces the segment grid returns within
`h + L`. The round itself is O(1). There is no per-corner cap to
state beyond those window sizes; the observed per-corner cost is in
the cost section.

Measurement error bounds: master's column is Run L's exact outline
(intersections in closed form, ~1e-12h). The variant columns sample:
feet lie on the walked outline polyline by construction (chord-vs-arc
≤ 1e-5 relative at the 0.01 rad arc step); 1v meets are read from 400
round samples (a crossing shallower than ~0.01h of overshoot can be
missed, and a meet's jump is interpolated at its entry sample);
concave turning from 200 samples; agreement is a Hausdorff distance
between 60-sample boundaries (61 on recordings: 21); criterion 3
round points sit at parameter fractions fi/20 (form B's are angle
fractions; both are "fraction from foot A to foot B" as Run L tracked
them, and the skeleton allowance is the larger foot's, unchanged).

## Verdict: fail, both variants

Neither variant passes. Criterion 2 passes exactly, and the new rule
removes most of master's creases (1v raw 63 → 7), but:

| gate | variant (i) | variant (ii) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1v (a) visible | **fail: 2 corners** | **fail: 2 corners** |
| 1v (c) small rounds outside null bands | **fail: 17** (master: 16) | **fail: 17** |
| 2 (feet) | pass: 0 failing feet | pass: 0 |
| 3 (motion) | **fail** (recordings, gate699) | **fail** |
| no concave turning | **fail: 21 corners** | **fail: 19 corners** |
| 4 (cost, p95 ≤ 0.75 ms) | **fail: 9.7–20.9 ms (13–28x)** | **fail: 10.3–21.1 ms** |

Live rounds: of 2,119 scene corners, master has 1,833 live
(r ≥ 0.05h), variant (i) 950, variant (ii) 948. The single-point gates
null the rest outright where form B's integral keeps a partial round —
that one decision drives the agreement (below), the gate699 pop, most
of the motion counts and 46 of the 63 small-or-nulled rounds outside
the slack null band. The margin gate (ii) changes almost nothing:
it fires rarely and moves no failing count except two concave
fallbacks. Per variant, in the brief's terms: (i) fails by 2 visible
creases, 17 small rounds, 21 concave rounds, up to 256 excess round
points in a recording and 20.1 ms a frame over budget; (ii) the same,
with 19 concave rounds and the margin gate's own cost on top.

(`g41_ring_tw64`, 41 corners) to 11.70 ms (`g41_def_tw42`, 385). The

(median 58 µs) and still ~9x over the ~2 µs a corner the budget
allows: the cost is now the exact-edge walks (each builds the

search. Tessellating every round to 0.1 device px chord error
(adaptive subdivision, not gated) would add 0.10–0.60 ms a frame
(180–1,390 segments).

## What comes next

Not S2, and no rebuild against either variant. The rethink removed
the branch, and with it Run L's cost driver and most of the creases —
what it kept is a different trade, and the record isolates it:

1. **Single-point gating is the failure, in four places.** Reading
   cap and steep once at L nulls 883 live master rounds (950 live of
   2,119), pops gate699 in one step, and re-gates in motion (up to
   256 excess round points). Form B's running-minimum integral is
   what makes its gates gentle; any V-pinned rule needs an
   equivalent — a gate read at V and at L with the feet clamped by
   slack directly, say — or hysteresis on the null (route 3 of the
   review), which reintroduces state the closed form was meant to
   drop. That choice is the next design question; it is not a tuning
   of this rule, whose gates the brief fixed.
2. **The fallback cubic inflects.** All 21/19 concave rounds are the
   brief's fallback at 0.55·|F_a F_b| handles. A fallback that cannot
   turn concave (shorter handles, or the quadratic with C clamped
   onto the fill side) is a small, separate fix — worth it only if
   question 1 finds a rule worth keeping.
3. **Cost is walks, not search.** 18.8 µs a corner median is the
   floor of building an exact-edge polyline per corner per frame in
   TypeScript. A production rule at the 0.75 ms gate needs the edges
   it walks to already exist (the renderer's sampled outlines do) —
   that is an integration question for a rebuild, not measurable
   against this offline rule, whose counted cost fairly includes
   building them.

Reproduce: `npx.cmd vite-node .claude/probes/K1179/s1d/s1d_scenes.ts`,
then `s1d_recordings.ts`, `s1d_sweeps.ts`, `s1d_plots.ts`, and
`s1d_cost.ts` under the perf lock (`lock_take_fast.ps1 -Owner

passes with the probes in place.
