# K708: the lives and sword sims

Ledger: K708. One stage, sonnet, 1 h cap. Probes only: no change to
`src/`, `test/` or the ledger.

## Why

K137 s3a step 7 was never run (`docs/design/K137_s3a.md`, step 7 and
"The sim table"). Lives (3 hearts) and the sword (`sword` off | timed |
oneuse, `swordMoves`, default `SWORD_MOVES_DEFAULT`) have merged, so

## Steps

1. Copy the turn sim (`.claude/probes/K80/turns2/`; start with
   `compare_after.ts`) and the real-time sim (`.claude/probes/K324/rt/`:
   `rt_lib.ts`, `rt_sweeps.ts`, `rt_sim.ts`, `vite.node.config.mjs`,
   `results_s3.md`) from the main checkout
   (`C:\Users\lsl\Repositories\knotmaze`, read-only) into
   `.claude/probes/K708/` in your worktree. Read their headers: how they
   run and what they count.
2. **Respawn.** The turn sim ends a game at its first catch. Make a catch
   do what the page does (main.ts's turn-clock catch, `turnCaught`, and
   the controller's `respawn`) and count catches a game, with a move cap
   so every game ends. Check that the real-time sim already respawns
   (`results_s3.md` says it does).
3. **The sword.** Lay it where the page does (`laySword`, `swordSpot`),
   take it when the player reaches it, and run the flee, banish and slay
   through the game modules (`minotaur_turns.ts`: `setSword`,
   `touchSword`; `minotaur.ts`: `swordArrive`, `swordTouched`). Call
   them; don't copy their logic.
4. **Run.** Players: greedy and fleer (and fleerq where the sim has it).
   Clocks: the turn clock at its shipped default, and real time with
   K324's three models (hunter, guard, stalker). Sword: off, timed and
   oneuse at `swordMoves` 8 (and at the default, if it differs). Mazes:
   the shipped defaults as in `results_s3.md` (288 H1 mazes x 3 reps). If
   a full run would take over 20 minutes, cut the mazes and say how many.
5. **Report.** `.claude/probes/K708/results.md`: one table per clock,
   rows player x model x sword mode, columns:
   - mean catches a game;
   - the share of games with 3 or more catches (they run out of 3
     hearts);
   - the share where the sword was taken;
   - the share where it slew or banished.

   Add the commands and the run time. Copy the tables into a "Handoff"
   section at the end of this brief, with a few lines on what they
   suggest for the defaults (lives 3, `swordMoves`).
