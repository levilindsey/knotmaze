# K578: cache the still maze under the confetti

Ledger: K578. One stage, opus, 2 h cap.

## Where it stands

While the finish confetti falls (K140, K568, K337), every frame redraws
the whole stage: the background blit, every ribbon with its crossing-gap
search, the junction fans, the markers, then the confetti. On the Pixel 11
the 8 s fall runs at about 45 fps (frames over 25 ms: about 120) against
60 at the 2026-10-16 base. K572 measured it (`docs/design/K572.md`,
"K568's own cost"): the confetti's own share is about 2 fps; the rest is
the scene redraw, mostly the gap search (57 ms of a 72 ms knot gs24 render
sync on the phone, before K573). K573 step 1 has since cut the search, so
measure today's numbers first; they are the baseline.

The confetti sits ABOVE the world root on the stage (`src/render/confetti.ts`
header: two ParticleContainers, the rain topmost). Nothing under it changes
during a typical fall: physics is asleep or settling, the head is on the
finish. So the scene under the confetti can be drawn once into a texture and
reused until something under it changes.

## Steps

1. **Baseline.** On the phone, the fall's fps and slow frames with
   `k572_confetti.mjs` (`.claude/probes/K572/`; copy what you need into
   `.claude/probes/K578/`). On the desktop, the frame cost during a fall
   (the app's own per-frame timers or `__knotLoop`'s tracker).
2. **Design the cache, in the frame loop's draw step**
   (`src/app/frame_loop.ts`, the K6-3 on-demand draw):
   - **What it holds:** everything under the confetti, background
     included, so a cached frame is the same bytes as a full one (same
     size and resolution as the canvas, a 1:1 nearest blit).
   - **When it is used:** only while the frame's visual need is the
     confetti's alone (`src/app/loop_flags.ts`). Any other visual need
     (geometry, paint, fog, the trail fade, the gold web fade, the
     background's drift, a camera move, a resize, a setting) redraws the
     scene in full and refills the cache, or skips it for that frame.
     Find every source of change under the confetti; a missed one draws a
     stale maze, which is worse than a slow frame.
   - **A lost WebGL context:** the cache's RenderTexture comes back empty
     after a restore (K456 measured the background's). Refill it in a
     `loop.onRestore(fn)` hook (`src/app/frame_loop.ts`): those callbacks
     run after the restore re-renders the background and before the
     restore frame is drawn.
   - **Memory:** one canvas-size RGBA8 texture, made when a fall starts and
     released when it ends, or kept if that measures better.
   - Prefer a pure, unit-tested piece for the validity rule, as the repo
     does elsewhere (`src/ui/head_indicator.ts`, `src/render/web_fade.ts`).
3. **Build it,** with a probe hook that turns the cache off in the same
   page, for A/Bs.
4. **Verify:**
   - Pixels: with the confetti clock pinned, a cached frame equals a full
     frame, 0 px different, on the desktop: knot mode, a daily, fog
     (`xp=fog`), the thread reveal (`xp=threads`), a burn race
     (`fi=fuse`), and a fall over a scene that is still moving (physics
     waking, the trail fading), where the cache must step aside.
   - A context lost and restored mid-fall: the cache refills, 0 px against
     a no-loss shot (`.claude/probes/K456/k456_probe.mjs` has a confetti
     case to start from).
   - The phone: the fall's fps and slow frames, cache on vs off in the
     same build, ABBA. Target: back near 60 fps.
   - The suite and the build pass, and no pixel changes when no confetti
     falls.

## Constraints

- Hold the perf lock (`$env:TEMP\claude-perf.lock`) for timings and
  pixel-exact shots, and the phone lock
  (`.claude/probes/agents/phone_lock_take.ps1`) for the phone. Wait for
  either inside one blocking command. K370 runs at the same time and uses
  both.
- Don't change the confetti's look or the gap search; K573 and K590 own
  the search, K370 the drag latency path.
- Never use `--virtual-time-budget`. Probes use fresh temp Chrome
  profiles.
