"## Plan" section of `docs/design/K138_plan.md`: its entries "4.2 One-way
runs (s3)" and "4.1 Locks and keys (s3)", "The rule kit (stage 1)" and

and the color rule. Stage 2 (`docs/design/K138_s2.md`) gave the kit the
arrival run and a phase, and built turnstiles and hidden runs.

## This stage, in order

What the cap leaves goes in the Handoff as the remainder.

1. **One-way runs (4.2)**, whole:
   - `src/game/one_way.ts` and its test;
   - the orientation: one DFS of the bridgeless core (tree runs point away
     from the root, back runs toward it; Robbins' theorem). Orient a
     seeded 20-40% of the core runs by it, weighted toward the plain
     route. Pockets and tails stay two-way;
   - the rule in the kit (par is the directed search);
   - refusals, with the s1 toast and pluck: a tap against the arrow, and
     a backtrack against it. Undo is always allowed;
   - chevrons as marker glyphs along the run;
   - a HUD row, share, the param `oneWay` (`ow`, [0, 0.6] step 0.05,
     default 0) and a panel row.
2. **Locks and keys (4.1)**, whole:
   - `src/game/locks.ts` and its test;
   - the `exit` form first: the exit tail's run is the one lock, opened by
     holding all its keys (1-3). Then `shortcut`. Then `colors`, if the
     cap allows: the latest key dyes the yarn (`pathColor`) and a gate
     passes only its color;
   - keys are K83 beads at vertices, taken on arrival with no tap and
     never used up. Place them where the start-key-gate detour is longest
     inside a band;
   - the kit searches (vertex, keys held), 2^k states;
   - a refused tap thunks and the lock stays dim;
   - a HUD row (keys held), share, the params `keys` (`ky`, 0-3,
     default 0) and `lockMode` (`lkm`: exit | shortcut | colors,
     default exit), and panel rows.

Each comes with tests on hand-built graphs and real mazes, as s1's Steps
2-5 lay out:
- the rule in the kit (fewest strokes and shortest length, refusals,
  `from` mid-solve);
- placement (one layout per seed, redraws, every draw solvable, no trap:
  every vertex still reaches the exit);
- the controller: a refused tap changes nothing, and Undo, replay and a
  backtrack restore the keys held. Par and strict mode read the kit;
- composition with ice, the color rule, turnstiles and hidden runs.

With every new param off, every pin holds bit for bit and nothing draws
from any RNG.

Then:
- **A census in Node.** Use s1's Step 6 harness (`.claude/probes/K138/s1/`
  and `s2/` in the main checkout). Over 50 seeds at today's defaults,
  give the legal / plain ratio of the fewest taps and of the shortest
  length for:
  - oneWay 0.2, 0.4 and 0.6;
  - keys 1, 2 and 3 in the exit form, then the shortcut form;
  - one-way with ice.

  Add the draws each needed, and how many oriented runs sit on the plain
  route. The table goes in the Handoff.
- **The page** (s1's Step 9). Use real clicks and touch taps on desktop
  and at 411x753 (dsf 2), on seed links such as
  `?7&ow=0.4&am=instant&snd=none`:
  - a tap against a one-way run refused, then the run walked the right
    way;
  - a backtrack against the arrow refused, while Undo still works;
  - a lock refusing, a key taken by arriving, then the lock passing;
  - the HUD rows and the share.

  Stills go in `.claude/probes/K138/s3/`.
- **Lore**: `gameplay-mechanisms.md`, `gameplay-links.md` and
  `gameplay-panel.md`.

**Calls.** Build the recs, and list each in the Handoff with its
alternatives:
1. With a chase on, the rules bind the player only, as s1 did for ice and
   the color rule. Stage 6 makes the minotaur and the fire obey them (the
   plan's question 1 and both entries' calls). Rec: yes.
2. Chevrons are marker glyphs for the prototype. Shader crests from the
   pulse machinery come only if Levi keeps the mechanism. Rec: yes.
3. The lock's default form. Rec: exit, the clearest.
4. Undo past a key pickup drops the key again (Undo restores the state).
   Rec: yes.
5. Par under one-way runs and locks rates against the best legal route
   (the plan's question 4). Rec: yes.
