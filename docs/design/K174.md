## The work

1. **The paces as fractions of the player's (2.1).** Levi thinks of the
   chase pace as "0.7 of your speed", and the K80 sim swept it that way
   (`f0.7` in `results.md`).
   - Make `minoHuntPace` a fraction of `playerPace` (default 0.7), and
     `minoWanderPace` too (default 0.5), so one `playerPace` edit scales
     the whole chase. The slider labels say so ("x your pace").
   - Change the meaning in place: same keys, no codec version bump
     (CLAUDE.md, "Pre-launch: no back-compat").
   - `ChaseParams` in `src/game/minotaur.ts` can stay in u/s, with the
     product taken where the params build it.
   - At the defaults the chase runs exactly as today: 0.7 x 10 and
     0.5 x 10 are exactly 7 and 5 in doubles. Check it.
2. **Re-fog on a catch (2.4).** Add a checkbox, on by default.
   - On: a catch returns the fog to how it stood at the solve's start.
     Before K100 (458e1e5), a catch cleared the fog; see what that
     commit changed.
   - Off: the explored fog stays, as today.
   - Show it only under fog of war, like `minoFog`.
3. **Roam toward the player (2.6).** Add a checkbox, on by default.
   - Today a roam leg picks a junction roamRange/4..roamRange away along
     the threads, or with chance deadEndBias a dead-end tip, at random
     (`minotaur.ts`, the destination pick near line 617).
   - On: bias that pick toward the player, so the minotaur meets the
     player in more than 55% of games. Design the bias yourself, for
     example weight each candidate by how much it closes the thread
     distance to the player, or take the closest with some chance.
   - Keep the module's exact math (K108) and its determinism: a tick
     depends only on the tick count, the params, the seed and the
     player's inputs (`replayChase`).
   - Tune it on the K80 headless sim. No target is recorded. Bring a
     small sweep against "off" at two or three strengths: meet rate,
     catches (mean, median, 3+ share), chase time and solve time.
     Recommend a default, with "0 to 2 catches in most games"
     (`results.md`, point 2) in view. Don't retune the other defaults:
     Levi chose 0.7 and 15.
   - If no single strength serves, add a strength slider next to the
     checkbox and say why.
4. **The new params.**
   - Append them to `CODEC_PARAMS` after its last entry. K111 stage 2,
     running alongside, also appends one after `hoverGlow`: whichever
     branch merges second renumbers its appends.
   - Name the keys in the minotaur's style (`mrf`, `msk`, or the like).
   - They are not daily-exempt (the dailies stay turn-based). Follow the
     other `mino*` params.
5. **K177's open checks, which ride along:**
   - fog at `minoFog=always`, and the other two modes;
   - the minotaur's off-screen indicator at 411x753 (desktop Chrome
     emulation is enough, K193);
   - waiting out a non-instant intro before the first tap;
   - frame time during a chase on desktop, measured under the perf lock
     (claude-config `rules/sessions.md`).
