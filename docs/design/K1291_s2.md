## Why

Stage 1 (c7fa5c61) gave `rp=outline` the app's palette, the gold path, the trail and the fog, through
today's paint code. The reveal is still missing: during the intro today's look draws only the revealed
stretch of each thread (`renderer.revealFx.revealMask[c]`, per chain sample, CPU only:

the whole maze from the first frame with the revealed head in its burn colours (`.claude/probes/K1291/out/s1/intro/`). The thread reveal
(`xp=threads`, K76: the explore view's visible pieces) is unchecked under `rp=outline`.

## Goal

In `rp=outline`, in that order: the intro shows only the revealed stretch, its front closed across the
thread, as today's look does, in every appearance mode; then the thread reveal (`xp=threads`) shows what
today's shows. Today's look is the approved one: judge against it. The technique is yours (stage 1's plan:
pack the masks into a float texture with a per-chain table; the fill already has chain and arc; the band
needs each piece's chain, which `band_gpu` can get from the reply in piece order). A reveal frame shows on
the next frame, with no worker build.
