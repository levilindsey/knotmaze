## This stage

Muddy runs (4.33), whole, as decided in s3b:

- **Param** `mud`: 0 (off) to 6 runs, appended to the codec after
  K795's 214-219 (so 220), with its panel row, tags, lore and the
  usual pins. Gameplay, so not daily-exempt. Works with or without a
  chase; off while a K138 rule layout is in play (the kit rates par
  in taps - a call, listed in the Handoff; the alternative is to
  weight the kit's searches too).
- **Placement** (`src/game/mud.ts`): runs on the fewest-moves route,
  drawn on their own stream (`ruleRng(seed, "muddyRuns")`), never the
  start or exit tail run, so the fewest-moves route and the shortest
  route differ. Count 0 places nothing and draws nothing; with the
  param off every pin stays identical.
- **Cost, two moves for everyone:**
  - You: one tap; the dot crosses at half speed; strokes and par
    count it twice (par's F is the fewest MOVES: a weighted
    `runsToExit`, muddy weight 2; strict's runs left the same).
  - The turn minotaur: a hop into a muddy run ends its turn and its
    next turn is skipped (its routing counts muddy as two moves).
  - Real time: half speed while crossing a muddy run.
  - Stars keep the length metric (K155) unchanged.
- **Look:** s3b's stand-in if the fuzzy look needs the paint shader
  (it is out of scope): the muddy runs read wider and darker at rest.
  The fuzzy look is a call for Levi.
- **HUD/stats:** a "muddy" row while mud is on (the runs laid), with
  its help line; no share tag (s3b Decided: the follower and muddy
  add none). `__knotChase()` lists the muddy runs (s3b probe hooks).
