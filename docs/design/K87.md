# K87 brief: make the maze plan fast without moving a maze

## Why

`buildMazePlan` takes about 250 ms at gs 18; the lattice, surgery and walk
graph together take about 6 ms (K74 doc, section 7). With
`fourWayFraction 1` (no choice splices) the plan takes about 25 ms, so the
choice-splice packing in `pickFarthest` (`src/core/maze_plan.ts`, ~563) is
the suspect: every pick recomputes each candidate's distance to every
placed point with `Math.hypot`. That split is inferred from timings, not
profiled. A faster plan means faster loads, and makes K46's rerolls and
K74's greedy-trap search (option D) affordable.

## Goal

The same plan, bit for bit, for every seed and every param set, in a
fraction of the time.

## Approach

1. Profile first (node, many seeds, gs 14-24, the shapes): where do the
   250 ms go? `pickFarthest`'s distance loops, its `available` callbacks
   (the dead-end pool's callback runs `planTrim`, a strand walk, on every
   pick), or something else?
2. If the distance loop dominates: keep a running minimum per candidate
   and update it only against the points placed since the last pick.
   The minimum over the same set of values is order-independent and
   exact, so with the same distance function the picks are identical by
   construction. Keep `Math.hypot` there: `Math.sqrt` can differ from it
   in the last bit and flip a near-tie. Keep the pool order, the strict
   `>` tie-break and the `d < spacing` skip exactly as they are.
3. `available` has side effects (the dead-end callback writes `c.trim`)
   and its answers change as picks land. Call it exactly as today: same
   candidates, same order, same number of times, unless you prove a
   change cannot alter any result.

## Identity proof

- Freeze today's `pickFarthest` (or the whole plan) as a test fixture,
  as the G2 mirror tests do (`test/core/fixtures/`), and compare plans
  over a few hundred seeds across sizes, shapes and the plan knobs
  (`branchDensity`, `fourWayFraction`, `deadEndDensity`,
  `junctionSpacing`, the clearances). Compare the full plan JSON.
- The existing guards stay green: `test/ui/daily_link.test.ts` (the
  golden dailies and maze identity), `test/core/maze_plan.test.ts`,
  `test/core/shapes.test.ts` (rect identity).

## Also measure (report only, change nothing)

The plan's comparisons use `Math.hypot`, which engines may round
differently. Over the same seeds, record the smallest margin between the
chosen candidate and the runner-up, and between any candidate's distance
and the spacing floor. If a margin falls within about 1e-9 of the value,
a browser on another engine could build a different maze for the same
link or daily: report the seeds. K46 needs the same answer ("validation
must not depend on Math.hypot rounding").

## Deliverables

- The change, with the fixture identity test, timings before and after
  (plan ms per size, under the perf lock), and the margin table.
- WIP commits at every milestone; probes in `.claude/probes/K87/`.

## Scheduling

`src/core/` only, no render or physics files.
