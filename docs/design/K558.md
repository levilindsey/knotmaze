## What pen order means (picked)

- **Every emitted piece of text has a pen time:** when a writer's pen
  reaches it.
  - The word's connected body is written in one stroke: the main
    strand from its start (`mentr_a` for a title), in its traversal
    order.
  - Every other strand is drawn after the pen lifts: t-bars, i and j
    dots, accents, the strokes of `#`, and any other strand that is not
    the main one. They follow the body in left-to-right order of their
    glyph, ties broken by the glyph's own stroke order (Call C1).
  - With today's builder, a spur or retrace branch (from `dropRetraces`
    or `openCusps`) takes the pen time of the moment the pen leaves its
    junction. The redesign removes these.
  - Find the exact emission order in the code, and document the rule in
    a comment above the new function.
- **At each text crossing, the pass with the later pen time is over.**
  With the reverse switch on, the earlier one is.
  - When both passes lie on the main strand (a loop), the second visit
    is the later one: the loop's up-pass goes under, its down-pass
    over.
- **Equal pen times are a builder bug** (two overlapping pieces). Fail
  loudly in tests; never guess.

## Mechanism (picked)

- **Carry pen time from the builder's input.**
  - Each `SegIn` (the segments `buildPlanar` takes, `text_graph.ts:89`)
    carries a pen-time key: a monotone value along the stroke, e.g.
    stroke ordinal plus arc length, or one global float.
  - The key survives `buildPlanar`'s splitting into fEdges: each piece
    inherits its segment's key, interpolated.
  - At a crossing node, compare the keys of its two passes.
  - The redesign reuses exactly this: it replaces the glyph source and
    keeps "emit `SegIn` with pen keys". Say so in the code comment.
- **Replace `assignOverUnder` for TEXT** with the pen-order assignment.
  - It sets `overFirst` from pen times in strand order, and `isOver` is
    derived as today.
  - The emblems keep `assignOverUnder`, byte for byte. Pin each
    emblem's over/under before and after.
- **Pass the reverse switch down.** `attachTitle`, `attachCartouche`
  and the standalone `text` scene (`?txt=`) all pass it to
  `buildTextGraph` through `opts`. Over/under is set on the word's
  nodes before they merge into the maze graph.
- **Alternation.**
  - Text crossings do not alternate in general: a t-bar goes over its
    stem, and a loop's first pass along the same strand goes under.
  - Exempt text crossings from `checkAlternation` with one typed marker
    (`src/core/types.ts`; your choice of form). The warning then stays
    meaningful for the maze and the emblems.
  - Today's digit-6 warning was parity's, not pen order's; it goes.
  - Physics and the renderer need no alternation, because the word is
    pinned (Decision 1, `docs/design/K115_cursive_text.md`).
- **`debug_renderer.ts`:** derive the over/under it shows from
  `overFirst` (or from `ps.crossings`) instead of `isOver`.

### The pen-order rule as built

The full comment is the K558 header above `assignPenOrder` in
`src/core/text_graph.ts`. A pen time is pen travel, in HU.

1. **The body.** The main path runs from the lead-in tail to the
   lead-out tail, starting at `mentr_a`, in traversal order. Its pen
   time is the arc length along it, so a loop's second visit is the
   later one.
2. **Spurs.** Glyph material can hang off the route at a non-crossing
   node: a `dropRetraces` or `openCusps` branch, or a merge letter's own
   start. A spur is drawn as the pen leaves that node on its first
   visit.
   - Spurs at one node are ordered by the glyph's stroke clock, at
     `PEN_EPS` (1e-5) per HU.
   - So a spur never reaches the next body node's time.
3. **Lifted strokes (C1).** Material that meets the route only at proper
   crossings, or not at all, comes after the whole body, from
   `bodyLen + PEN_GAP` on.
   - The order is glyph by glyph, left to right. Within a glyph: its
     lifted strokes on its own stroke clock, then its dots, then its
     kept accents.
   - `PEN_GAP` (1) separates every slot.
   - On a bare line, everything is lifted.
4. **Over and under.** At each text crossing, the later pen time goes
   over. `penReverse` puts the earlier one over.
5. **Ties and malformed crossings.**
   - A tie is a gap of `PEN_TIE` (1e-9) or less. It warns
     `K558: text crossing … equal pen times`, is listed in the result's
     ties, and keeps `overFirst` true. Nothing guesses.
   - A crossing without exactly two passes gets `assignOverUnder`'s
     fallback: `overFirst` true, `isOver` false, no pen times.
6. **Alternation.** Every text crossing carries the `penOrder` marker
   (`types.ts`), and `checkAlternation` skips those nodes. The maze and
   the emblems keep `assignOverUnder`, byte for byte.
7. **Source-agnostic.** `assignPenOrder(graph, edgePen, reverse)` reads
   only the graph and each final edge's pen times at its two ends.
   - The builder emits every `SegIn` with `penA` / `penB`, and
     `buildPlanar` interpolates them onto the pieces it splits a segment
     into.
   - K115's redesign keeps "emit `SegIn` with pen keys", so it inherits
     the rule unchanged.
