# K831: cursive a/i redesign

Ledger: K831. Parent: K115, cursive text. Levi's brief (2026-10-22, on
`.claude/probes/K580/s5/shots_s5/desk_branch/ai_x2_t.png`): the a's entry
joins too high, the i's entry too high and not tangent enough, the a's
exit leaves at a fork angle instead of tangentially, and the a's
top-right has the top and stem as crossing spurs where they should JOIN
at a corner with both cusps sharp; the walk runs up the left side,
around that corner, and down the stem.

Before/after stills: `.claude/probes/K831/before|after/` (`a2`, `ai2`,
`a3`, `ai3`, `i2`; same URLs, `?7&am=instant&snd=manual&fw=0&phw=0&txt=…#glyphs2=…`).

## What was built

The a is one strand again (`src/core/glyphs/glyph_data.ts`, `A_GLYPH`):

- Entry dives from the connector to BL at the oval's bottom-left
  (32, 20), arriving along (1, -1) — the bottom arc's own line. The
  connector's crest stays ~2.6 GU over the entry line (authoring
  searches: `.claude/probes` scratch grids of 2026-10-23, discarded);
  it no longer arches over the shoulder to a junction at the top.
- At BL the body forks up the left side; the bottom arc is a spur out
  of BL that LANDS on the stem at BR (79, 20) (`SpurDef.land`, second
  junction, no END). The connector and the arc are collinear through
  BL, so the join reads tangential.
- The top-right is a declared skeleton corner (`Glyph.corners`, point
  18): one strand turns from the top down the stem, both cusps sharp,
  and the walk runs up the left side, around the corner, down the stem
  on that same strand. No crossing there anymore; the glyph has zero
  crossings.
- The tail leaves BR down the stem's own line and sweeps to the exit:
  the exit is the body itself, tangential by construction (the old
  41-degree J2 fork is gone).
- The i's entry fork dropped to the default 15 degrees and its J sits
  lower (y 44), so the connector meets the stem much more tangentially.

Form support added: `SpurDef.land` (builder forces a junction node at
the landing tip; the lint judges the landing like a junction) and
`Glyph.corners` (declared body corners exempt from the G1 rule and
required to be real corners).

## The BL join is a fork, not a tapered root — and why

The brief said "tangentially", and the codebase's seamless tangential
merge is the K580 tapered root (a body cusp + `tangent: true` spur).
That form was built and measured here at BL: across handle grids and
two structural variants (direct cusp; straight hug leg into BL), the
lint's taper-monotone measure — the root's width growing toward J —
bottomed out at 0.022–0.033 LU per side. The calibrated bound is
0.01 LU (K643 stills: 0.015 first shows on the desk). Both strokes at
BL bend hard in opposite senses (the dive is still turning in, the
rise bends away up the wall), where the old a's J arms bent together;
the medial root cannot stay monotone there. The lint bound was not
weakened.

The fork form keeps the geometry Levi asked for — connector collinear
with the bottom arc, low at the oval's bottom-left — and draws it as a
junction. If a later letter needs the seamless root at a like geometry,
the root machinery is intact: the pre-K831 a is preserved verbatim as
`A_TANGENT` (not in `GLYPHS`), probe switch `#glyphs2=atangent`, and
the K580/K643 tests now run on it.

## Follow-up (Levi's rejection of the first stills, 2026-10-23)

Levi read the first form's stills as: the a's incoming segment entering
downward, the i's entry near-perpendicular rather than collinear, the
a's round parts uneven in curvature, and a bump on the top edge before
the corner. All four were real. The corrections:

- **a entry**: the entry point itself now sits low, south-west of BL
  at (14, 8) with dir (1, 1), so the lead, the connector and the first
  body segment all rise north-east into BL (34, 24) on that tangent
  and the body continues up the left side on the same line. The
  bottom arc is the fork spur out of BL, landing at BR. (The first
  form kept the entry at the join line and let the first segment's
  handles drag the approach down into BL.)
- **a top and tail**: a level top knot (64, 72) runs straight into
  the declared corner (86, 72) - the bump is gone. The tail is ONE
  segment from BR (a second declared corner, point 21, out (1, -1))
  to the exit: every two-segment tail bottomed at R 10.8 GU against
  the 11 GU floor however the handles were split - the climb from
  the valley to the exit is too steep to share between two cubics.
- **i**: the shipped form is now the exact tangent join (fork 0):
  entry arm, departing arm and stem spur collinear on (1, 3) at J.
  J moved up to (22, 50): at (20, 46) the folded arms never parted to
  the thread width inside the entry's arc (tangentRoot returned null;
  the medial separation tops out near 17 of the 18.72 GU needed) and
  the entry's S into J bottomed at R 5.8 GU. The opened variants
  (15-90 degrees) remain for the stage 9 fork measurement.
- **The dot is a dot**: `dotLoop` (glyph_knots.ts) draws the tittle
  as a closed square loop from its centre and two half-diagonals;
  `Glyph.dots` carries it, the builder emits one closed decorative
  strand (no ENDs), and the lint holds it only to closure and
  clearance - no tip, G1 or radius rules. It is not a segment.
- **Builder fix found on the way**: a landing spur's tip was forced
  as both an END and the landing junction, stranding a degree-2 END
  node at the tip (`checkStructure` warned on every "a" build). The
  tip's end forced point is now replaced by the junction.
