# K83 brief: placeholder icons for the start, finish and current position

Levi, 2026-10-12: "Please create placeholder icons for the start, finish,
and current positions. These will be minimal, white strokes with clear
background. Rendered within a black circle with a bit of margin roughly
equal to the stroke width."

## The look

- Each marker is a black disc, and inside it a white STROKED glyph
  (strokes only, no fills) with a clear background. The margin between
  the glyph's outer extent and the disc's edge is about one stroke width.
- Placeholder glyphs (simple, legible at the default zoom on a phone):
  - start: a play triangle;
  - finish: a flag (pole and pennant);
  - current position: a simple figure or a "you are here" ring and dot.

  Propose alternatives on a contact sheet; Levi will replace them later.
- Strokes stay crisp at every zoom: draw explicit geometry (pixi
  tessellates small radii coarsely; see the explicit N-gon marker lore
  in `docs/history.md`, the 2026-10-10 fix round), or textures generated
  at the device resolution. The stroke width scales with the marker.

## Constraints from the current markers

Read `src/render/fabric_maze_renderer.ts` (markers, head dot, halo) and
`.claude/rules/render.md` and `render-gaps.md`.
- The start and exit discs sit in their thread with a capsule erase
  notch under the connecting tail. The per-pixel gap around a dot is a
  ring of radius dot + gap: G1's marker disc slots, in `gap_mask.ts`.
  Keep the gap ring concentric with the new disc, and resize the slot if
  the disc's radius changes.
- The head dot travels along the thread (Stage 12). It has a black ring
  and an under-weave halo (underContainer BELOW the ribbon root), and it
  glows. Decide whether the glow and halo stay (likely yes, behind the
  black disc).
- Fog of war and the reveal: the exit emerges only once explored, and
  markers appear with the reveal; keep that. Flow undulation: markers
  route through flowOffset.
- The off-screen exit indicator (K73) should echo the finish icon.
  Coordinate through the coordinator; don't edit K73's files.
