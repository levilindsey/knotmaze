## The flags (design 4.3, corrected against the code)

A poll is read at the end of a frame and asks for the next one; a push
lands between frames or during one. Sources are a1c4179's file:line.

- **LOAD** (poll): physState `loading` or `settling` (frame_loop 205,
  256, 273), `warmHoldFrom >= 0`, `host.presettling`,
  `!warmup.wave1Done`. Clears at finishLoading, finishPresettle, the
  warm hold's end, and `freeze()` (527) after a physics-off settle.
  Corrected: 4.3 lacked the warm hold, the host's pre-settle and the
  warm-up gate.
- **PHYS** (poll and push): `host.needsFrame()` (new, item 3) AND
  speedMult > 0, not frozen, not `halted`; pushed by `wake()` (160) and
  by each soft-snap frame (465-485). Clears once asleep and the last
  state is drawn (question 2). Corrected: false while paused (the host
  stays awake and nothing steps, 452-458) and while halted.
- **GEOM** (push): `invalidate()` (559; main 1420 and 1467, probe_hooks
  291, 296 and 1022) and `wake()` (162). New record-only notes at
  rebuild, recolorScene, setWidths, resize, the panel's change callback
  and the worker's sleep trip (question 2). Clears after a geometry pass.
  Corrected: 4.3's "panel 1818 and 1865, hooks 4903, 4908 and 5619" are
  those five calls before M1-M3 moved them; no context-restore handler
  exists to push from; a change-detecting handRendererLook is K6-2's.
- **FLOW** (poll, ambient): `look("flowStrength") > 0` (208, 413), half
  rate at rest. Corrected: 4.3's "and Motion not reduced" is already in
  `look()`: reduced motion wears flowStrength 0 (accessibility.ts 242
  and 267, fed by main 1449), so no separate term.
- **REVEAL** (poll): `scene.reveal` running (280-286). Corrected: it
  runs on the ticker's dt, not wall time.
- **FX** (poll): `needsFxRender()` or `appearFxAlive() > 0`. The second
  is new: sceneAnimating ignores the reveal front's particle tails.
- **PAINT** and **PAINT_AMBIENT** (poll; question 7): the renderer's

  fire's FX, webFading, the explore growth, close-in and start fade, the
  halos' breath). The pulses and the fog's wobble (every frame while fog
  shows) set PAINT_AMBIENT, the rest PAINT. Live pulses = any declared
  pulse: a fabric that is unsolved and not mid-move (fabric 2425-2449);
  4.6's "and pulseSpacing > 0" always holds (the panel's range is 0.8-6,
  the renderer floors it at 0.5, 4467), so no link stills them.
  Corrected: "ring laps" do not animate (kRingLap, paint_glsl.ts 167, is
  lap-index math); the explore halo and clock are the fabric renderer's;
  the dot's move is GAME.
- **GAME** (poll): a running chase (tickChase), the turn minotaur's ride

  dot's move), a key hold (keyHold, stepWaiting, holdDue), a pending win

- **CAMERA** (poll and push): `!camera.converged()` (new: the tween, the

6. **`__knotLoop()`** (probe_hooks.ts, through ProbeCtx). It returns:
