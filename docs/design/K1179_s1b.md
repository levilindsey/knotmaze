# K1179 S1b: a lean corner solve on the true outline, offline (K1178 Run K)

The rule under test is Run I's fill outline (the union of the arm bodies
at half-width h and form B's rolling-ball rounds), with two changes from
`docs/design/K1179_spike.md`: the measurement is corrected (below), and
the margin gate's band moves from [-0.05h, 0] to [0, 0.05h]
(`docs/design/K1178_muse_batch16.md` Run K, Claude's reading of the
spike). Offline in Node (`npx.cmd vite-node`), no GPU, browser, server
or phone. Nothing in `src/` or `test/` changed; Run I's probe files and
outputs are unchanged. Probe code: `.claude/probes/K1179/s1b/`
(`lib_s1b.ts`, `form_b_ref.ts` - a copy of `src/render/field/form_b.ts`
with only the margin band changed - `lean.ts`, `s1b_scenes.ts`,
`s1b_recordings.ts`, `s1b_sweeps.ts`, `s1b_cost.ts`, `s1b_small_ref.ts`,
`s1b_plots.ts`); outputs in `.claude/probes/K1179/s1b/out/`.

Settings and inputs are Run I's: the 12 K868 scenes and the 4 seq13
recordings (41 distinct frames each), master's form B settings
(`new FormB(scene)`, reach "quad", dEdge "near" with dEdgeTip 1,
rootBand 0.5 with Run H's tip-scaled root gate on), float64 sampling
(100 points per round, every 0.01h along the edges, curvature as signed
Menger over a 0.05h baseline). Run I's counts reproduce exactly before
anything is corrected: 695 cusp corners, 116 failing feet and the same
16 small rounds outside every null band (task 5), which validates the
re-measurement's baseline.

Three corrections to the measurement (Run K tasks 1 and 4):

- The exposed boundary is the boundary of the union of the tubes and
  the rounds' fills, a fill as `inFill` defines it. Run I filtered
  sampled points only inside tubes; S1b also drops points under a
  round's fill (`lib_s1b.ts`: `inFillS1b`, `fillChecker`).
- Criterion 1's 0.1h past each foot no longer counts the bare arm
  edge's own curvature: on the edge parts, a point fails only where
  its curvature is more than 0.01/h below the bare tube edge's
  curvature at the same point. Foot to foot (the arc) keeps
  κ ≥ −0.01/h.
- Criterion 3's edge allowance includes the rotation term: an edge
  point may move as far as its centre point plus h times the turn of
  its normal, plus 1 device px. The sweep gates are form B's ordinary
  rates, which Run I reproduced: gate699 0.1270 (Run I's brief gate
  of 0.125 sat below that rate), tee 0.1480, ring 0.1737, synthetic
  0.0508.

## Verdict: fail (the stop case, named)

Criteria 1-3 cannot hold together on this outline rule, and criterion
4 fails independently, so the direction needs rethinking before any
rebuild (the review's last line, section 7 of
`docs/design/K1179_geometry_review.md`). In short:

- Criterion 1 still fails after both corrections and after the margin
  change: 472 corrected cusp corners under Run I's form B, 392 under
  the reference and the lean solve (they are identical). Most
  remaining cusps are the bare tube's own bends and folds (class b)
  and balls reaching other tubes (class d), which no margin band
  addresses (task 1).
- The margin change is not free: it moves 1,150 of 2,119 scene
  corners' r by more than 0.01h (median |Δr| 0.071h, p99 2.0h, worst
  3.56h), makes the small-round clause far worse on its own terms
  (343 rounds outside every null band, up from 16), and breaks
  gate699's ink rate (0.1990 against 0.1270; every other sweep
  passes). Criterion 3 therefore fails at the reference even where
  the corrected edge allowance passes everywhere (task 4).
- Criterion 4 fails by 14-36x: the lean solve's p95 is 26.8 ms
  (def_drag), 25.9 ms (def_flow), 13.7 ms (muse_drag) and 10.8 ms
  (muse_flow) a frame against a 0.75 ms gate, at 35-47 µs a corner.
  Its bounded work caps (below) never bind - agreement with the
  reference is exact - so the reference algorithm's shape (32 gate
  evaluations along the ball's branch, each with a margin walk) is
  itself the cost, not its iteration limits.

The 16 small rounds are not lost rounds: each is stopped by the
margin or steep gate at its final ball (8 margin-led, 8 steep-led,
task 5), so margin/steep belong in the null-band list - at the price
that the reference then has 841 small rounds to explain the same way.

## Task 2: the rule change (margin band [0, 0.05h])

The reference is `s1b/form_b_ref.ts`, a copy of master's `form_b.ts`
with the single change
`w_margin = ss(0, 1, min(mA, mB)/0.05h)` (plus its header and import
paths; `git diff --no-index` against the source shows nothing else).
The brief's argument holds where it claims to: at the reference's q,
min(mA, mB) ≥ 0 by construction of the stop, so over the 12 scenes
ω is 1.0000 at every one of the 2,119 corners (the overlap gate
never fires under the new band).

What it costs the rounds, over the 12 scenes (Δr = r_ref − r_before,
matched corners, n = 2,119):

- 1,150 corners (54%) move by more than 0.01h; |Δr| distribution:
  p50 0.071h, p90 1.137h, p99 2.000h, max 3.562h (muse_default_tw
  j133 arms 2-0, r 3.56h → ~0). Every scene's largest |Δr| is
  between 1.89h and 3.56h (per scene in `out/scenes_s1b.txt`).
- Corrected cusp corners: 472 → 392. By the task-1 classes, the
  reference cures 247/268 of class a (the rest fail differently at
  their new r), 63/267 of b, 8/13 of c, 45/146 of d and the 1 e -
  364 of 695 in all, while 61 corners outside Run I's failing set
  fail corrected at the reference (smaller rounds meet the edge
  more sharply), for the net 392. The reference's own Run I-form failures classify
  as a 223, b 280, c 1, d 95, e 0 (599 in all).
- Feet: Run I-form failures 116 → 1 (a class-b foot at the
  reference); corrected failing feet 3 → 0.
- Small rounds (r0 ≥ 1h, r < 0.25h): 400 → 841 in total, and
  outside Run I's null bands 16 → 343 (lean identical). The new
  margin manufactures small rounds wherever another part of an arm
  passes within the band, which at a junction core is most corners.
  Whether that is a failure depends entirely on whether margin (and
  steep) count as null bands - task 5 says they should, for the
  original 16; the 343 are the same question at scale, and a rule
  that stops over half its rounds below 0.25h of their r0 has
  changed the look, not just the gate.

## Task 3: the lean solve and its agreement

`lean.ts` solves task 2's rule with bounded work per corner, stated
as maxima: V by at most 12 Newton iterations (reference 60); tracked
feet move in h/32 windows at most 128 times (reference 4,096); the
ball integrates over the same 32 ρ nodes as the reference (16 nodes
missed gate dips between samples - agreement blew out to 1.86h on
def, so the node count is the reference's), each advanced by at most
6 predictor retries of at most 8 Newton iterations (reference 60);
the only grid use is the ω scan at the end, as the reference's.
Cheap gates (steep, cap, root) are evaluated before the margin walk
and skip it when one is already 0, which is the same product. The
solve is a pure function of the frame (predictors start from V's
branch, never from a previous frame), so cold and warm solves agree
exactly.

Agreement with the reference, per corner (gate 0.01h on V, q, the
feet and r):

| Where | Corners compared | max ΔV | max Δq | max Δr | max Δfeet | Outside 0.01h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 scenes | 2,119 | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0 |
| def_drag | 14,832 | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0 |
| def_flow | 14,694 | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0 |
| muse_drag | 7,849 | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0 |
| muse_flow | 7,615 | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0h | 0 |

The maxima are exactly zero (0 device px at dpr 2 also): on this
data the caps never bind - Newton converges inside 8 iterations and
feet track inside 128 window moves - so the lean solve is the
reference solve with its limits stated, bit for bit. There are no
corners outside the gate to list. That is also why it cannot be
cheap: the work is the 32 gate evaluations, not the iteration
headroom (criterion 4 below).

The split over a recording's frames: ball advance ~40%, gates

## What S2 should check

1. The phone GPU check (the review's stage 2) is moot until the
   outline rule changes: neither the corrected cusps (392, mostly
   the bare polyline tube's own folds and other-tube crossings)
   nor the CPU cost (14-36x over) is a phone question. Do not run
   S2 on this rule.
2. The per-pixel form is now the gating question, as Run I said:
   at a vertex foot the distance-field outline is the vertex arc,
   which removes class b by construction (267 cusp corners and 111
   feet). Re-run corrected criteria 1-2 against a distance-field
   outline on the class-b and class-d worst sites (plots above)
   before building the pass.
3. Margin/steep as null bands need a budget, not an exception list:
   state how much r0 gates may spend per corner before the round
   counts as lost, then recount the reference's 841.
4. gate699 under any stricter margin: the new band's 0.199 ink
   step at e 0.36 is the motion price of stopping balls earlier;
   a margin change that survives needs its own sweep stand before
   it is adopted.
5. A cheaper solve must change shape, not caps: closed-form
   piece-pair walking (line-line, line-circle, circle-circle)
   along the ball's branch, with gate evaluation only at piece
   transitions, is the untested candidate. Its cost gate stays
   0.75 ms p95 a frame, and every corner's inputs move every frame
   (share 1.0000), so it must be fast cold, not incremental.
