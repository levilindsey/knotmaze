### The calls

1. **Sprint by length (s3a call 5).** Built as designed: the pair's
   turn travels half the pair's summed length.
   - Alternative: the minotaur skips the first move's share outright
     and plays the second at full length (simpler, but a long first run
     then buys more than a short one).
2. **What arms in real time.** Built: the NEXT two departures; a press
   mid-run waits for the next departure.
   - Alternative: the run under way counts as the first (sprint now,
     but half a run then counts as a whole one).
3. **The key.** Built: F. The Design named Q, which sits where A is on
   AZERTY, a move key there.
   - Alternative: Q, or Shift.
4. **A catch while armed or mid-sprint.** Built: it spends sprint.
   - Alternative: give it back when nothing was gained (armed, no move
     made yet).
5. **A restart.** Built: every restartChase (a level restart, a Reset
   path, the scene build; the panel row restarts the solve) gives sprint
   back.
   - Alternative: once per attempt, kept spent across restarts.
6. **s3b call 1, what a catch resets.** Not built. Proposal: a catch by
   any monster puts every monster back at its start and puts sleepers
   back to sleep (the minotaur's CATCH RESETS).
   - Alternative: only the catcher goes home; the others keep their
     state (harsher; woken sleepers stay awake).
7. **s3b call 2, extra monsters' telegraph.** Not built. Proposal: none
   this stage; K324's telegraph shows the minotaur only.
   - Alternative: a dim telegraph per awake monster (busier screen).
8. **s3b call 3, the follower's variance rule.** Not built. Proposal: at
   each junction a 25% chance, on its stream, that it takes its shortest
   route to you for one step instead of your trail.
   - Alternatives: a fixed trail with only the 1-2 turn delay drawn; or
     a chance that grows with your lead.
9. **s3b call 4, the follower's look.** Not built. Proposal: it sleeps
   under a sleeper's glyph, so under `both` a junction reads risky but
   not how.
   - Alternative: hidden until it appears.
10. **s3b call 8, the share tags.** Decided for sprint: ` · ⚡ used`
    shows once sprint was used, never when it was not. Sleepers ("😴 2
    woken") and the follower wait for them.
