# K1179 S1e: the pinned round with form B's own gate integral, offline (K1184 Run I)

The rule under test is Claude's read of Run H's record
(`docs/design/K1179_s1d.md`, `docs/design/K1184_muse_batch17.md` Run I):
keep Run H's round pinned at V, but replace its single-point gate with
form B's own gate integral taken along the pinned path. Per corner
form B makes (the same cyclically adjacent arm pairs; style, `cr`,
`h`, θ and `r0` as `solveCorner` in `src/render/field/form_b.ts` has
them, `r0 = style·(cr/3)·min(2h, 1.5h·tan(θ/2))`):

- V and θ exactly as `solveCorner` computes them. The measurement
  columns read the built corner's V and θ; the production (cost) path
  finds V by S1c/S1d's pair iteration (`solveVPair`) over arm windows
  shared across the frame. Its largest |ΔV| against the built corner
  over the cost run is 5.8e-13h, inside the brief's 1e-9h allowance.
- Nodes k = 0..K at `r_k = k·r0/K`. At each node, the edge points
  `E_a`, `E_b` sit at arc length `r_k·cot(θ/2)` from V along each
  arm's exact edge (Run H's `walkEdge`: offset segments and vertex
  arcs). Each arm's exact edge is built once per corner and walked
  cumulatively across the nodes (a cursor advances with the nodes'
  increasing arcs; the build is counted once in the frame cost, and
  each arm's margin window is built once a frame in a shared cache).
  The centreline foot is `E − h·n` (`n` the edge's unit normal away
  from its own centreline): the walk tracks the foot locally in a
  ±0.6h window about the running foot (so no folded part of the arm
  can steal it), and the foot is then read exactly on the centreline
  at the interpolated arm distance. The probe point is
  `q_k = mean(E_a + r_k·n_a, E_b + r_k·n_b)`, which on straight arms
  is form B's ball centre (checked on the straight stands: the sweep
  traces below are form B's to the printed digit, and on the gate699
  stand's corners at e = 0, 0.3, 0.645 the solved `r` matches form
  B's to 0.000000h, at e = 1.0 to 0.0002h).
- `w_k = wOf` at node k, all four factors as form B has them: the
  margin gate with `marginOf`'s own definition (each arm's window
  outside its foot's basin, against `dc = |q_k − that arm's
  centreline foot|`; evaluated by `marginOfWin`, the window form in
  `.claude/probes/K1179/s1c/closed.ts`, checked against the s1b copy
  of form B's `marginOf` to 5.8e-15 world over 536 node margins),
  the steep gate (`den = 1 + n_a·n_b` from the normals at the feet,
  `den(V)` as `denV`), the cap gate (`slackOf`'s slack at the feet),
  and the root gate (each centreline foot's distance from its root
  over `rbEff`, S1g's band with K1163's tip scale).
- `S_0 = w_0`, `S_k = min(S_{k−1}, w_k)`, then
  `r = Σ ½(S_{k−1} + S_k)·r0/K`, form B's trapezoid. A node whose
  feet would pass an arm's window end (a foot at its span end, or an
  arc past the walk's end) ends the sum: its segment is counted with
  S = 0 and S is 0 past it, as the branch's end does in form B.
- The feet at arc length `r·cot(θ/2)` from V, and Run H's round: the
  rational quadratic from `F_a` to `F_b` whose control point C is
  where the edges' tangent lines at the feet meet (travel at A
  inward, at B outward), with weight `w = cos(φ/2)`, φ the turn
  between the two tangents. On straight arms that is exactly form
  B's circle. Where C is not on the fill side, or is farther than
  4h from V, the largest node radius `r_k ≤ r` at which it is is
  used instead (null at k = 0: no round). There is no cubic
  fallback.
- The fill as Run H's: the region bounded by the round and the two
  edges from each foot back to V.

Two variants, both measured: (i) K = 32 (form B's node count);
(ii) K = 8.

Offline in Node (`npx.cmd vite-node`), no GPU, browser, server or
phone. Nothing in `src/` or `test/` changed; Run H's and Run L's
probes and outputs are unchanged. Probe code:
`.claude/probes/K1179/s1e/` (`lib_s1e.ts` the rule and its walks,
`s1e_scenes.ts`, `s1e_recordings.ts`, `s1e_sweeps.ts`, `s1e_cost.ts`,
`s1e_plots.ts`); outputs in `.claude/probes/K1179/s1e/out/`. Inputs
are Run H's: the 12 scenes, every distinct frame of the 4
recordings (41 each), the four sweeps, master's form B settings.

Most work a corner can take (task 1): deterministic and cold every
frame — no warm start, no tracking across frames, no Newton, no
bisection in the rule. Two exact-edge builds, each the outline
pieces over at most `maxArc + 2h` of centreline with
`maxArc = r0·cot(θ/2) ≤ style·(cr/3)·1.5h` (plus one
centreline-foot search per walk point, in the running ±0.6h
window); K+1 node evaluations, each two cumulative walk steps, two
centreline reads, two margin basin walks (each scans its arm's
shared window once) and the three other gate factors in O(1); and
at most K+1 round constructions in the node-fallback search
(normally one). V in the production path takes at most 24 pair
iterations, each two window scans. There is no other per-corner cap
to state beyond those window sizes; the observed per-corner cost
is in the cost section.

Measurement, as Run H's: master's column is re-run here on the
exact outline, not copied — it reproduces Run H's (and Run L's)
63 raw meets, 0 failing feet and 16 small rounds. The variant
columns sample: feet lie on the walked outline polyline by
construction (chord-vs-arc ≤ 1e-5 relative at the 0.01 rad arc
step); 1v meets are read from 400 round samples; concave turning
from 200 samples; agreement is a Hausdorff distance between
60-sample boundaries on the scenes (21 on the recordings: the
round at 21 fractions, as criterion 3 tracks it), in device px at
dpr 2, between the outer of the two solves' feet.

## Verdict: fail, both variants

The gate integral does the job it was brought in for — the nulled
rounds, the gate699 pop and most of Run H's agreement gap are gone
(live rounds 1,952 of 2,119 at K = 32 against master's 1,953, where
Run H's single-point gates kept 950; agreement p50 falls from
1.57 to 0.22 device px; every sweep reads at form B's own rate).
What remains is the pinned feet themselves on curved arms, and
the offline cost of building exact edges corner by corner.

What would have to shrink, and by how much, to meet 0.75 ms:
everything, together, by 26–51x in (i) (19–36x in (ii)). No
single part fits the budget alone except the three O(1) gates
and the round: the edges' build at 8.0–12.6 ms a frame would
have to fall 11–17x even if every other part were free; V+θ
(2.4–3.8) 3–5x; the node feet (2.5–5.2) 3–7x; the margin gate
(1.2–3.4) 2–5x. Cutting K from 32 to 8 barely moves the frame
(compare (ii)) because the two fixed costs — building each
corner's exact edges and finding V over the windows — do not
depend on K. As Run H concluded of its own walks: 54.8 µs a
corner median is the floor of building an exact-edge polyline
per corner per frame in TypeScript, now with 33 gate
evaluations walked along it. A production rule at this gate
needs the edges it walks to already exist (the renderer's
sampled outlines), which is an integration question for a
rebuild, not measurable against this offline rule, whose
counted cost fairly includes building them.

## What comes next

Not S2, and no rebuild against either variant. Run I closes the
question Run H left open, and the answer splits it in two:

1. **The gate integral was Run H's failure, and it is fixed.**
   Read as an integral along the pinned path, form B's four
   gates restore its live rounds (1,952 against 1,953), hold
   every sweep at form B's rate including the gate699 corner
   that popped without the root gate, keep the feet exact, and
   turn no round concave — Run H's 21 inflecting fallback cubics
   are gone with the cubic (the node fallback's 20 corners are
   convex quadratics at smaller feet; 1 null in (i)). The
   remaining 1v raw count is 1 corner under 0.002 device px of
   overshoot, and (i)'s visible count is 0.
2. **The pinned feet are the remaining failure.** Where an arm
   bends at its foot, walking the edge at `r·cot(θ/2)` puts the
   probe point off the medial axis, and the margin/steep reads
   there shrink the round (agreement p95 2.20 px, worst 15.3 at
   κ·h ≈ −12 with a fallback on top) and let it breathe between
   frames (140 excess round points on `muse_drag`). Coarsening
   the integral to K = 8 does not interpolate this away; it
   re-opens a visible crease at j49 and nulls four more fallback
   corners. Any V-pinned successor has to decide what its probe
   point is on a curved arm — a medial correction to `q_k`, or
   feet placed by centreline arc instead of edge arc — and that
   is a new rule, not a tuning of this one: the brief fixed both
   placements, and both are measured here.
3. **Cost is integration, again.** The frame cost is the exact
   edges' build plus V's window scans, before the gates are
   read at all; K and the margin gate are second-order. A rule
   that passes the 0.75 ms gate in a rebuild would walk outlines
   the renderer already samples — unmeasurable offline, as
   Run H said of the same floor.
