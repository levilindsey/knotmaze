### Residuals (known, not fixed)

- A turnstile's third arm is not dimmed (the brief's "third arm dim").
- The bands show the next phase during a move's animation.
- A band's contrast depends on the section colors.
- Excursions longer than 4 taps are not modeled by the kit, so the fewest
  and par can overstate a maze that rewards a longer wait loop.
- A route planned further out walks to its first refused run (as in s1).
- After a backtrack the turnstile's arrival run is the path's previous
  run, so re-entering the run just retraced can be refused.
- At a dead end under turnstiles the kit has no route (it bars the
  arrival run). A hint and the compass point back out there (the
  backtrack, through ruleGoodArm's undo search), checked on 260 seeded
  dead ends in turnstiles.test.ts.
- Auto plucks (music mode) do not reveal (call 3).
- The HUD reads "0 / 0 found" when no hidden run was placed.
- Outside the thread reveal a press on a hidden run grabs its particles:
  by reading (not run), `input_pointer.ts` `grabAt` and `onThread` take
  `ps.nearest`, which knows nothing of gone spans (burnt, cut or
  hidden), so the press tugs the unseen thread instead of panning, a
  tell. Its release reveals nothing (`revealByPluck` skips a hidden
  run). Under the thread reveal `pickThread` decides instead.
