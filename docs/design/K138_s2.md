## This stage, in order

What the cap leaves goes in the Handoff as the remainder.

1. **The kit gains the arrival run and a phase** in its node (the plan's
   "Later stages extend it"). Ice and the color rule keep their results
   bit for bit.
2. **Turnstile junctions (4.12)**, whole: `src/game/turnstiles.ts` and its
   test, the rule in the kit, placement (seeded three-arm junctions on or
   near the plain route, redrawn until solvable and biting), the open
   pair drawn as a K81 route band with the third arm dim, refusals (the
   s1 toast and pluck), HUD row, share, param `turnstiles` (`tst`, 0-8,
   default 0) and panel row.
3. **Hidden runs (4.27)**, whole: `src/game/hidden_runs.ts` and its test,
   placement (seeded runs that shorten the plain route; the kit checks
   the maze solves without them), hidden through the thread reveal's
   pieces so they draw nothing and pick nothing, revealed for the rest of
   the solve by a pluck on a thread of the same note within 3 runs (the
   pluck hook in main.ts; a pluck is not a move), par shown with and
   without them, HUD row, share, param `hiddenRuns` (`hid`, 0-4,
   default 0) and panel row.

Each comes with tests on hand-built graphs and real mazes, as s1's Steps
2-5 lay out: the rule in the kit (fewest strokes and shortest length,
refusals, `from` mid-solve), placement (one layout per seed, redraws,
every draw solvable), the controller (a refused tap changes nothing;
Undo, replay and a backtrack restore the phase and the reveals; par and
strict mode read the kit), and composition with ice and the color rule.
With both params off every pin holds bit for bit and nothing draws from
any RNG.

Then:
- **A census in Node** (s1's Step 6, its harness in
  `.claude/probes/K138/s1/` in the main checkout): over 50 seeds at
  today's defaults, the legal / plain ratio of the fewest taps and of the
  shortest length for turnstiles 2, 4 and 8, hidden runs 2 and 4, and
  turnstiles with ice, plus the draws each needed. The table goes in the
  Handoff.
- **The page** (s1's Step 9), with real clicks and touch taps on desktop
  and at 411x753 (dsf 2), on seed links such as `?7&tst=4&am=instant`:
  a turnstile refusing an arm, then opening it a move later; going back
  through a closed turnstile (always allowed); a hidden run revealed by
  a pluck and then walked; a pluck too far away revealing nothing; a tap
  on a hidden run doing nothing; hidden runs under the thread reveal
  (`xp=threads`) and the fog (`xp=fog`); the share. Stills in
  `.claude/probes/K138/s2/`.
- **Lore**: `gameplay-mechanisms.md`, `gameplay-links.md` and
  `gameplay-panel.md`.

**Calls.** Build the recs, and list each in the Handoff with its
alternatives:
1. The turnstiles' clock: the move count in every mode, real time
   included (the plan's question 6). Rec: yes.
2. A turnstile's starting phase: seeded per junction. Rec: yes.
3. What reveals a hidden run: a pluck within 3 runs on a thread of its
   note; a tap near it does nothing. Rec: yes.
4. Whether a revealed run stays revealed after Undo past its reveal.
   Rec: it hides again (Undo restores the state).
5. Par under hidden runs: rate against the route without them, and show
   the shorter par with them. Rec: yes (the plan's question 4).

**Out of scope:** the minotaur, the fire and the chase files (stage 6
joins the rules to them); a beat-timed turnstile (an idea); switches,
tides and the wait action (stage 4); saved attempts and the store
version; a real phone pass.
