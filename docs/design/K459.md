### Item 2, the stub's pluck to its rounded cap: built (model A, a free end)

- **Why A.** A string fixed at one end and free at the other swings most
  at its free tip, so the cap, the part Levi asked to see, moves most.
  Model B (a fret at the cap's tip) holds the tip still, and the cap is
  only 0.5 tw g long (0.26 world on f5), so under B it would barely move.
- **The code** (b53d5a4, f83ce80):
  - `pluck_model.ts`: `freeEndX` maps a free lobe's shape coordinate onto
    half of a symmetric string; `pinFreeEnds` sends an end whose cap
    holds a node back to the hard fret at the solid end.
  - `pluck_overlay.ts`: `PluckRequest.freeLo/freeHi`. add() pins, then
    finds the tapped lobe; only the tapped lobe, at a node -1 end, gets
    `freeA/freeB` (beta 0.5; sympathetic and still lobes keep both ends
    fixed); layout and capClearance read freeEndX; the extrusion writes
    the cap coordinate to aUV.y and PLUCK_FRAG narrows the thread over
    the cap (`h sqrt(1 - vCap²)`), so the blur is rounded there. A
    fretted lobe under LOBE_MIN_LEN, or a fretted tap with no tapped
    lobe, reports "core" (silent).
  - `ribbon_renderer.ts` (only `pluck()`, the new `stubCaps` and one
    import): no solid stretch reports "core"; otherwise the span grows by
    `stubCaps`, the cap of kExDotsSd at a piece's frontier end with a
    fade, under the dots or beacon look, not under reduced motion.
  - Tests (pluck_model.test.ts): "a free end (K459): the stub's lobe
    swings most at its free tip" and "a free end (K459): a node inside
    the cap pins it to the solid end".
- **pinFreeEnds** came from f12 (chain 85, open): its 0.26 cap held a
