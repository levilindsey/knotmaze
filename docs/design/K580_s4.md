# K580 stage 4: the i's joint, the partial blends and the open-end crescents

Ledger: K580 (stage 4), with K675 and K676. One stage, opus, 2 h cap.
Stage 3 fixed the miter thorn at the a's J and measured what is left: see
the Handoff in `docs/design/K580_s3.md`, its "Step 3: the i's joint", "The
stem" and "Residuals". The design is `docs/design/K580.md`. K620 (the
root's paint) merged before this stage. It added `footC`, `footO` and
`sideC` to `Strand.taper` (`glyph_graph.ts` fills them), and it paints the
root as a blend of the strokes' section colours: see the Handoff in
`docs/design/K620.md`.

## Decided

- The three defects below are this stage. K677 (aland's square step) and
  K678 (the cover strip's fallback) are not.
- tp52 stays and the taper does not change. The fix lives in the glyph
  data or the renderer, not in a new look.
- The target is stage 3's: no bare region of the round-joined ideal deeper
  than 0.01 LU, and no mesh more than 0.01 LU outside it (`taperBulgeLU`).
- Scenes that have no glyph text render 0 px different (knot, maze, reveal
  and burn). Stage 3's count found no sample past 90 deg in them.

## The defects

1. **The i's joint notch in "ai"** (the ledger's K580 next step).
   - The bite is 0.107 LU at xh 2 and 0.227 LU at xh 3, at about (4.585,
     1.595) and (6.740, 2.548). Stage 3's round joins did not change it.
   - It is the round-joined ideal's own concave corner, between the
     junction round piece and chain 3's end.
   - The round piece's centre is the mean, over the arms, of
     `sampling.sampleAtSafe(samples, sampling.paintArcAt(samples, a.slot,
     closedA))` (`src/render/ribbon/joinery.ts`, about lines 1030-1090).
     So it follows the glyph junction slots.
2. **The partial blends (K675).** The round join starts at
   `ROUND_DOT_START` (cos 110 deg) and is full at `ROUND_DOT_FULL` (cos 120
   deg), in `src/render/ribbon_mesh.ts`.
   - The a's J2 at xh 2 turns 113.5 deg, so it is only partly round: the
     outer offset is 1.389 h (1.000 h is fully round).
   - The i's joint sample at xh 3 (i 222) turns 110.8 deg and barely
     blends.
   - A lower threshold rounds both.
3. **The open-end crescents (K676).** Beside spur tips, the fills-only
   raster finds bites of about 0.1 LU: the a's stem at (2.228, 1.703) at xh
   2, by chain 2's start (the J2 spur), and the notaper J bites (0.104 to
   0.117 LU).
   - The raster draws no end discs or junction pieces, so it cannot say
     whether the real render covers them.
   - The Pixel showed a notch at the a's stem by its foot disc (K662). So at
     least that one is real.

## Steps

1. **Reproduce.** Copy what you need from the main checkout's
   `.claude/probes/K580/` into your worktree's: `count.mjs`, `raster.mjs`,
   `thorn.mjs`, `shots.mjs`, `static.mjs`, `serve.mjs`, `joint_zoom.ps1`,
   `s3_sheet.ps1` and `run_s3.ps1`.
   - Re-run the raster on your base (master with K620), to get the
     before numbers.
   - Re-run the count. Add a band from 95 to 110 deg for every scene (knot,
     maze, reveal, burn, kinky, and the "a" and "ai" text), so step 3 knows
     what a lower threshold touches.
2. **The i's joint.** Find which slot puts the round piece's centre where
   it is.
   - **Rec: fix the glyph's junction data** in `src/core/glyphs/`, so the
     piece is centred where the strokes actually meet. Data touches only
     glyph text.
   - The fallback is a change to the centre rule in `joinery.ts`. That rule
     serves every fabric junction, so either confine the change to glyph
     junctions or show with the A/B that the maze scenes stay at 0 px. If
     neither works, stop at that point and report the fix you would make.
   - Hit the target at xh 2 and at xh 3, tapered and notaper.
3. **The partial blends (K675).** Lower `ROUND_DOT_START`, and
   `ROUND_DOT_FULL` with it if the blend's width should hold.
   - **Rec:** start at 100 deg and be full by 110 deg, unless step 1's
     count shows a scene sample in that band.
   - The a's J2 at xh 2 and the i's sample at xh 3 must come out fully
     round (1.000 h).
   - Update the constants pin and the sweep in
     `test/render/ribbon_round.test.ts`. Keep the continuity and the
     no-allocation tests.
4. **The open-end crescents (K676).**
   - First, check the real render. Shoot headed real-GPU stills of the a's
     stem foot and the notaper J at xh 2 and xh 3, at desk and phone scale,
     zoomed with a 0.1 LU grid (`joint_zoom.ps1` does this for the joint).
   - Find where the real render draws an open end: grep `tip` and `cap` in
     `src/render/ribbon/` and `ribbon_renderer.ts`.
   - If the end treatment covers a crescent, say so with the crop, and that
     part of K676 closes. If not, fix it, for example the end disc's centre
     or radius at a spur tip, so the end meets the round-joined ideal.
5. **Tests.** Write unit tests for whatever you change:
   - the glyph slot data, against where its strokes meet;
   - any centre rule;
   - any end geometry.
6. **Verify.**
   - **The A/B.** Run a same-state pixel A/B of master and your branch with
     K550's harness, `.claude/probes/K550/kill/ab_probe.mjs`, on ports
     5322/5323. Builds come from `scripts/preview.ps1 -BuildOnly`. The
     scenes are knot, maze, reveal and burn, at dsf 1 and 2, with the
     same-build control.
     - Expect 0 px everywhere.
     - Attribute any change to a crop, and explain it.
   - **The sheets.** Re-shoot "a" and "ai" at xh 2 and 3 (tapered, notaper,
     aland), desk and phone scale, as stage 3 did. Re-run the raster and
     thorn numbers. Put before and after side by side in
     `sheets/s4_before_after.png`, with a zoom of each fixed spot.
   - **The suite.** `npm test` and `npm run build`.
   - **The cost.** If the code in `RibbonMesh.update()` changes, not only a
     constant, re-run stage 3's `bench/bench.ts`.
7. **Lore.** Update the round-join entry in
   `.claude/rules/render-ribbon.md`. It attaches when you open
   `ribbon_mesh.ts`. Note any glyph slot rule in the glyph lore.

## Stop rule

Stop when the stage is done or at the 2 h wall-clock cap, whichever comes
first. Run `Get-Date` at the start and at each milestone. Commit WIP to
your branch at every milestone, green or not, and never push.

A compaction is not a stop: after one, re-read this brief and `git log`
before going on. Keep reads narrow (Grep -n, then Read with
offset/limit). Any helper runs on sonnet with a 1 h cap under this same
rule.

When you stop:
- commit WIP;
- append a "Handoff" section to this brief: what landed, the numbers
  (raster, A/B, benchmark), the sheets' paths, the residuals and the
  handle fields;
- report.

End your report with the item's handle fields and a `Ledger items` list.
