# K1291 stage 1: the outline in the app gets the app's palette and the base layer's overlays

Ledgers: K1291 (this), K1240 (the redesign), K1278 (the outline's paint; its stage 3 is pulses). One Claude
Opus agent in its own worktree, on its own branch from master. Cap 2 h. Read first: the handoff at the end
of `docs/design/K1240_s4_app.md`, and the headers of `src/render/outline/outline_app.ts` and
`src/render/outline/paint.ts`.

## Why

`?…&rp=outline` (K1240 S4, `src/app/frame_loop.ts` ~line 210) draws the threads from
`src/render/outline/` in place of the ribbon renderer's base and fillet layers. Two things are missing, so
a maze cannot be played in it:
- **The palette.** The outline paints with `sectionPaint(.., "app", baseHue)`'s 12 pastel swatches from
  the prototype, not the app's section colours (`src/core/section_colors.ts`, the panel's colour scheme).
- **The overlays drawn inside the base layer are hidden with it:** the memory trail, the gameplay paint
  (the walked path, the head's run, the finish), the fog of war, the reveal's width masks. Markers, plucks
  and the HUD still draw. In today's look these come from the base layer's paint shader
  (`src/render/paint_glsl.ts`, data textures in `paint_model.ts`, sampled by chain and arc length).

## Goal

In `rp=outline`, in that order: the threads take the app's section colours; then the trail and the
gameplay paint show as they do in today's look; then the fog; then the reveal's width masks. Today's look
is the approved one for every overlay: judge each against it. The technique is yours (one likely route:
the outline's fill and band sample the same `paint_model` textures by chain and arc length, through
`paint_glsl`'s functions). An overlay change (a move, the fog's advance, a reveal frame) shows on the next
frame, without waiting for a worker build. Pulses are K1278 stage 3's: if your route draws them anyway,
keep them on and report what they look like.

## Done

- **Palette.** Every run's colour in `rp=outline` equals today's look's at the run's middle, on def, a
  muse_hash link and a ring, under two colour schemes.
- **Overlays.** For each overlay you land: its footprint (the pixels that change when it is on against off)
  and its colour in `rp=outline` against today's look, over the pixels inside the thread in both looks,
  at three game states (a few moves in, mid-maze, solved). Report the footprint overlap and the colour
  difference (median, p99) per overlay; crop rows today | rp=outline | |difference| at the spots where each
  overlay shows, in `.claude/probes/K1291/out/s1/crops/`, made from your final commit. I view them before
  merging. Real-time headed Chrome only (`scripts/diag_screenshot.mjs`, the S4 tools in
  `.claude/probes/K1240/s4/`); never `--virtual-time-budget`.
- **Nothing else moves.** Today's look (no `rp=outline`) unchanged: its shader text and the paint tests
  (`test/render/paint_*.test.ts`) unchanged, the golden snapshots unchanged. The outline's own checks
  unchanged: `outline_port.test.ts`'s pins and the census tools keep the prototype's palette (the app
  palette is an input only the app passes). The outline worker's frame on def within 3% of before (ABBA
  under the perf lock); main's per-frame cost with every overlay on, mid-drag and at rest, reported.
- **Tests.** `npm test` green; new tests for whatever is pure (the palette mapping, any table you pack).

  read only the lines you need). The Read tool only right before an Edit, and for viewing crops. Junction

  each milestone). Leave the last 25 minutes for the guards. Then commit WIP, append a handoff to this
