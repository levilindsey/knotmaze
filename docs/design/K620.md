## What to build

1. **Each side takes its stroke's paint.** A root pixel lies on the C
   side or the O side of the centerline (the medial axis), by the sign of
   the lateral coordinate and `sideC`.
   - The pixel evaluates the same paint as its stroke would at that
     point: the section color, its blends and the FX, sampled on C at
     `footC(a)` or on O at `footO(a)`.
   - Map the lateral position onto the stroke as well, so any
     across-the-ribbon shading lines up with the stroke underneath. Every
     root pixel lies inside its side's stroke ribbon between R and J
     (K580_s2's inset numbers); check that.
   - If an FX can't be sampled at another chain's point, fall back to the
     section color for that FX and report which.
2. **Both sides blend into the spur's paint at J.** As a goes from aR to
   0, each side's stroke paint gives way to the paint the spur has where
   it leaves J. Use the ease in `section_colors.ts`'s `blendEase` unless
   the stills argue for another, and report the profile.
3. **The seam between the sides** is the centerline. Antialias it, but
   don't widen it into a gradient unless the stills call for it. Under
   `strands`, C and O are both chain 0 in "a", so no seam should show
   there at all.
4. **Every color scheme.** Cover `mono`, `strands`, `depth`, `segments`
   (the default) and `noise`.
5. **Both part points.** tp52 is today's default; `#glyphs2=tp60` must
   work too, including its non-monotone footO near R.

Acceptance:
- On the mirror (`paint_math.ts`), at R each side's paint equals its
  stroke's paint at its foot, and at J both sides equal the spur's paint,
  within 1/255 per channel.
- The stills show no edge at R, along the root's outer edges, or at J, at
  desk and phone scale.
