## What to build

1. **Each side takes its stroke's paint.** A root pixel lies on the C
   side or the O side of the centerline (the medial axis), by the sign of
   the lateral coordinate and `sideC`.
   - The pixel evaluates the same paint as its stroke would at that
     point: the section color, its blends and the FX, sampled on C at
     `footC(a)` or on O at `footO(a)`.
   - Map the lateral position onto the stroke as well, so any
     across-the-ribbon shading lines up with the stroke underneath. Every
     root pixel lies inside its side's stroke ribbon between R and J
     (K580_s2's inset numbers); check that.
   - If an FX can't be sampled at another chain's point, fall back to the
     section color for that FX and report which.
2. **Both sides blend into the spur's paint at J.** As a goes from aR to
   0, each side's stroke paint gives way to the paint the spur has where
   it leaves J. Use the ease in `section_colors.ts`'s `blendEase` unless
   the stills argue for another, and report the profile.
3. **The seam between the sides** is the centerline. Antialias it, but
   don't widen it into a gradient unless the stills call for it. Under
   `strands`, C and O are both chain 0 in "a", so no seam should show
   there at all.
4. **Every color scheme.** Cover `mono`, `strands`, `depth`, `segments`
   (the default) and `noise`.
5. **Both part points.** tp52 is today's default; `#glyphs2=tp60` must
   work too, including its non-monotone footO near R.

Acceptance:
- On the mirror (`paint_math.ts`), at R each side's paint equals its
  stroke's paint at its foot, and at J both sides equal the spur's paint,
  within 1/255 per channel.
- The stills show no edge at R, along the root's outer edges, or at J, at
  desk and phone scale.

Data slots:
- KSTATIC C1.w (`stat[ch*8+7]`, `paintBase`, always 0 today:
  `ribbon_renderer.ts` about line 995 passes `paintBase: 0`; `kChain`
  never reads it) becomes the ROOT POINTER: 1 + the KGEOM texel of the
  chain's root table, 0 = no root. Only root chains get a non-zero value,
  so a scene with no root is byte-identical. The vertex shader already
  holds `kc1`.
- KGEOM after `geomEnd` (PaintScene constructor) holds the root tables,
  one per root chain, allocated by a new `PaintScene.setRootTable(chain,
  texels)`: header H0 = (body chain, sideSign, NR, 0), H1 = (arcJ on the
  root chain, ctrlJ = 2*joint, 0, 0), then NR = 17 entry texels uniform
  in the root's arc from R (alpha 0) to J (alpha arcJ):
  (arcC, arcO, 0, 0) = the body chain's arcs of C's and O's feet.

Plumbing (fields only):
- `types.ts` Strand.taper += `footC: number[]`, `footO: number[]` (the
  BODY chain's chain-local ctrl of each foot, node i = ctrl 2i, 17
  entries R -> J like `sep`) and `sideC` (TangentRoot's, GU y up).
- `glyph_graph.ts` (taper build, about line 932): map the feet's body
  PATH arcs (arcJ - footC(a), arcJ + footO(a); body = chains[0], its
  nodes' `.arc`) to body ctrls.
- `scene.ts` (about line 274): resolve the body chain by the J node id
  (`strand.nodes[taper.joint]` in another strand), so text_graph's merge
  needs no remap; pass `{sep, joint, body, bodyJoint, footC, footO,
  sideC}`.
- `ribbon_renderer.ts`: style `taper` type widened; in update() after
  every chain's writeGeom, for each root chain convert foot ctrls to arcs
  (`kArcOfCtrl` on the body), find the uv side geometrically at mid-root
  (cross-check sideC), write the table + pointer (changed texels only).

Shader (section paint; non-root chains take today's path):
- VERT, root chains only: vKR0 flat = (ptr, sideSign, arcJ, ctrlJ),
  vKR1 = (kSectionAtArc(body, arcC).rgb, arcC), vKR2 = (same for O),
  vKR3 flat = knotSection(root, ctrlJ) = the spur's section at J.
- FRAG, `vKR0.x > 0 && kpt.x < arcJ`: side by sign((vUV.y - .5) *
  sideSign), one-pixel AA across the centerline; each side's paint = its
  section + FX on the body chain at its foot (kFxColor), spur paint =
  vKR3 + FX on the root chain at ctrlJ; w = kEase(alpha / arcJ) (the
  junction ramp's ease, uKSect.y = blendEase); base = mix(side, spur, w).
- Mirror in `paint_math.ts`: `kRootTable` packer, `kRootSection`
  (vertex twin) and `kRootPaint(D, U, root, alpha, s, out)`; tests in
  `test/render/paint_root.test.ts` (synthetic + the real "a" at xh 2/3 x
  tp52/tp60: R = stroke at foot, J = spur, within 1/255).
