# K110 brief: join every piece of the maze to the start

## Why

Levi, 2026-10-13: "we should join pieces as needed to ensure everything
is reachable." A solvable maze can hold walk-graph pieces that the start
never reaches. K109's probe found them only when the entrance and exit
share a strand: 55 of 630 default designs, and 24 of 546 across the
panel's gs 4-24 (heart, star, fish, tree, ring and circle, up to gs 8;
never rect). Dailies 1-400 have none. The player sees thread they can
never walk on.

## Goal

Every walk-graph vertex is reachable from the start, in every design.
Every design that has no orphan piece today keeps its plan bit for bit:
no daily moves and no rect moves. Only the orphan designs move.

## Approach

1. Read K109's `repairTailRoute` (`src/core/maze_plan.ts`, ~869) and
   the probe `.claude/probes/K109/orphans.ts` (b722a12; its header has
   the command). Rerun the probe first and confirm 55/630 and 24/546.
2. Extend the repair to join each orphan piece to the part the start
   reaches. Choose the mechanism (for example, turning a crossing
   between the piece and the reachable part into a junction, the way
   the plan's splices do), but:
   - join each piece at ONE place, so it hangs off the maze as a side
     region. A single join cannot shorten the start-to-finish route,
     so the optimal route's length stays the same; test that;
   - keep the plan's invariants (planarity and the rest);
   - use K108's engine-exact math (`src/util/det_math.ts`; no
     `Math.hypot`, trig, exp, log or `**` in generation).
     `test/core/deterministic_math.test.ts` guards it.
3. The join must render like any other junction (fans, cover strips),
   and must settle and sleep like one.

## Proof

- Over the probe's sweeps (the 630 default designs and gs 4-24), there
  are no orphans after the change, and every other design's full plan
  is bit-identical. Freeze today's plans as a fixture or compare full
  plan JSON before and after, as K87 did (`docs/design/K87.md`).
- Unit tests: reachability for the formerly orphaned designs; the
  optimal route length is unchanged for each of them; planarity.
- `test/ui/daily_link.test.ts` (the golden dailies) stays green with
  no edits. Re-freeze the K87 identity pin
  (`test/core/maze_plan_identity.test.ts`) in a commit whose message
  lists every design that moved (seed, shape, gs).
- A before/after sheet of 4-6 moved designs at rest, with the joins
  marked: real-time `node scripts/diag_screenshot.mjs <url> <out.png>
  [settleMs]`, never `--virtual-time-budget`. Use explicit design links
  (`?<seed>.<blob>`): a bare URL is today's daily.
- `npx tsc --noEmit`, the full `npx vitest run` and `npm run build`.
