# K193 + K195: the phone pass in portrait

The round phone pass (K156, `scripts/phone_pass.ps1`) measures in
whatever orientation the phone happens to sit in. Levi chose to have it
emulate a portrait viewport instead. One stage, one agent.

## What Levi decided

On 2026-10-14 at about 11:10 (email 4.1, "b seems best"): the pass
emulates a 411x753 portrait viewport over CDP and leaves the phone as it
sits. A fresh A/A floor at that viewport then replaces the landscape one
(K195), before the next round's pass (K98, K92, k140-confetti) and
K232's rerun.

Background:
- The docked phone sits in landscape: display 2424x1080, pages 810x331
  to 387 CSS px at dpr 2.625 (K156 3c).
- Round 2026-10-14b's pass ran in portrait, because the phone was off
  its dock and in use. Its A/B against the landscape floor is therefore
  weak (K232).
- Today the pass follows the phone's orientation (`Phone-State`'s
  display check, `scripts/phone_pass.ps1` about line 176).

## The work

1. **Portrait emulation.**
   - Every pass sets its own tab to a 411x753 CSS px portrait viewport at
     the phone's own dpr (2.625), over CDP, before it measures or shoots
     anything (`Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride` with `mobile: true`
     and a portrait `screenOrientation`, or whatever works on Android
     Chrome).
   - It applies to all of the pass's scripts that open or drive the tab:
     the frame bench, the load scenario (a fresh navigation each time),
     the looks and the sheets. Check whether an override survives a
     navigation, and re-apply it if not.
   - The log and `summary.md` record the phone's display as it sits and
     the emulated viewport.
   - Turning it off is a parameter (for example `-Viewport native`), so
     an old landscape pass can be reproduced.
2. **Prove the emulation is real.** Android Chrome might accept the
   override and still render at another size, or scale the page into the
   landscape window. In the pass's tab, while it measures, check:
   - the page's `innerWidth` x `innerHeight`, `devicePixelRatio` and
     `matchMedia('(orientation: portrait)')`;
   - the PixiJS canvas's backing size, which should be about 1079x1977
     device px;
   - a screenshot: 411x753 CSS px, all page and no blank strip (K195
     found every landscape frame carried a white strip at the bottom: the
     screenshot was 387 CSS px high, the page 331).

   If the emulation can't be made real, stop and report what you found
   and the options. Don't fall back to rotating the phone.
3. **The A/A floor at portrait (K195).**
   - First a one-rep smoke pass, so a broken emulation costs minutes
     instead of the full run.
   - Then a full A/A pass with the emulation:
     `-Base master -Head master -Reps 3`, with the default `-Moves 0,7,13`.
   - Make it the floor the summary compares against. Keep the landscape
     floor on disk for reference.
   - Report a short table: the new floor against the landscape one, per
     scenario.

## What must not change

- **Change no phone settings** (Levi, 2026-10-14 about 09:29). That
  means no rotation, no `settings put`, no display or developer option.
  The emulation lives in the pass's own tab and ends with it.
- The pass's safety rules stay:
  - it never wakes or unlocks the phone, and stops at once if the phone
    is locked;
  - a touch on the phone trips the watchdog and stops the pass;
  - the cleanup closes only the pass's own tab and removes only its own
    tunnels.
- The bench's scenarios and what they measure. Only the viewport
  changes.
- Nothing in `src/`. This stage is about the pass, not the game.

## Process

- One stage. Run `Get-Date` at the start and at each milestone. Commit
  WIP on your branch at every milestone, and never push. Don't edit
  `LEDGER.md`.
- Lore: the phone pass's topic file (`docs/lore_index.md` maps it): the
  emulation, how to check it is real, and the new floor.
- Stop when the stage is done or at your time cap, whichever comes
  first. The cap is two hours on Opus and one hour on Sonnet, unless your
  launch prompt sets another. Neither a compaction nor context size is a
  stop. After a compaction, re-read this brief and `git log`, then go
  on.
- At the stop:
  - commit WIP;
  - append a "Stage 1 handoff" section to this brief: what is done,
    what is next, evidence paths;
  - commit it and report.

  Start nothing beyond this stage. The next round's pass and K232's
  rerun come after it.
- The final report covers:
  - what changed and where;
  - your calls;
  - the verification results, with the floor table;
  - a `Ledger items` list: follow-ups, residuals, decisions for Levi;
  - the handle fields: branch, last commit, evidence, next.
