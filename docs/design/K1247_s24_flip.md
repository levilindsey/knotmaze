## Why

Stage 23 moved the band's placement to main (`band_place.ts`, one transform-feedback pass per reply). With
`ob=1` the speed bar holds on def and fat: worker drag p50 about 130-147 → 97-103 ms, band part 44-46 → 11-13
ms, reply 22.7 → 5.9 MB, no buffer resizes mid-drag. The default didn't flip, for three reasons:
1. 4 pieces sit over 1/4 px: def c61 d730 (colRow gives v109's two-point fan no column inside the start clip)
   and muse_hash c8 d121 (duplicated end points, uneven steps, the clip at radius 0.416).
2. Past 4 slots the GPU keeps the passes nearest in s. Today and the CPU records keep `footNear`
   (`band_rec.ts:450`). That changes 4925 quads on muse_hash, and no census measures them.
3. g41_user_tw64's through-bends (j48, j49, joined by K1314 s4) were never run through the twin.

The bench also leaves the TF pass out of the main frame. It was measured at p50 4.8 ms, max 14.5, with a
readPixels sync, under CPU load.

## Goal

`rp=outline` uses the band records unless `ob=0`, with the look held to today's band and the main frame
counted honestly. The technique is yours. Today's look (no `rp=outline`) stays byte-identical.
