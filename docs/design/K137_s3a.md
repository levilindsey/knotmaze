### 1. K137-b: broken runs block

- **The minotaur.** A broken run (`fragile.ts` `brokenRuns`) is gone for
  its pathing on both clocks:
  - the turn clock's `TurnGraph` hops and candidates;
  - real time's `ChaseGraph` routes, roam destinations, the guard's
    territory routes and the stalker's choices;
  - `plan()`, so the telegraph never shows a route through a broken run.
- **A minotaur on a run when it breaks** finishes that run. Rec; list it
  as a call.
- **The fire** (`fire.ts` precomputes arrival times in `buildFire` /
  `fireTime`). The past is fixed:
  - a run that broke before the fire entered it blocks the fire;
  - a vertex the fire had already reached stays burnt, even if its fire
    came through a run that broke later.

  So after each break, keep every arrival time at or before the current
  fire clock, and recompute the rest from those vertices on the graph
  without the broken runs. Breaks only remove runs, so arrival times only
  grow, and the burn race stays winnable (design R.3.3's proof still
  holds: the fire only gets slower).
- **Replays.** `replay(taps)` and `replayChase` must rebuild the same
  blocked state from the same taps.

### 2. Lives (4.32)

- `lives`: 0 (off) or 1-5 hearts; rec default 3 when switched on, from a
  slider whose 0 reads "off".
- **A catch costs a heart** and does today's catch: `respawn()` returns
  you to the entrance and the walk goes on.
- **At zero hearts** the level restarts as `minoRestart` does
  (`restartSolve(true)`, the tallies carried), with the hearts refilled
  and a toast, "Out of hearts! Starting over."
- **Lives replace `minoRestart`.** While lives is on, the panel hides
  `minoRestart` and the game ignores it.
- The design says a catch returns you "to your last pickup". There are no
  checkpoints, so rec: the entrance (today's respawn). List it as a call.

### 3. The sword (4.7)

- `sword` (off | timed | one-use) and `swordMoves` ([4, 20], 8), as the
  design names them.
- **Where it lies.** A deterministic spot 4-8 runs off the optimal route,
  outside the minotaur's lair and territory.
  - Rec: a dead-end tip in that range when one exists (a dead end with a
    reward), else a junction.
  - Draw from its own counter stream on the seed (as the minotaur's
    `TurnRng` streams do), so no other draw shifts.
  - It is render-only: the maze does not change.
- **Picking it up.** Arriving at its spot takes it. For `swordMoves` of
  your moves the minotaur FLEES:
  - each step takes the run that most increases its walk distance from
    you (the design's -1.2 flee map), on both clocks and in every model;
  - its glow turns pale blue and it reverses at once (Pac-Man's mode
    change);
  - the telegraph shows the flee in the same pale blue, or nothing; your
    call.
- **Touching it while it flees.** Timed: it is banished to its lair, then
  comes back out after its lair time with its model. One-use: it is slain
  for the rest of the attempt (the HUD and the share say on which move).
  A catch never happens while it flees.
- **Real time.** Rec: the sword lasts `swordMoves` runs walked by you, so
  standing still does not run it down. The alternative is seconds (N x
  the median run's walk time). List it as a call.
- **The look.** A sword glyph through the K83 marker path
  (`marker_glyphs.ts`, `marker_mesh.ts`). A HUD row with the moves left,
  or "used".

### 4. Sprint (4.29)

- `sprint` (off | on). Once per attempt; a level restart gives it back.
- **The turn clock:** arm it, and your next two moves count as one
  minotaur turn. In K481's by-length mode, the minotaur travels one run's
  worth of length for the pair. Rec; list it as a call.
- **Real time:** rec, your next two runs play at double speed.
- **The button.** K114's scissors button arms the next tap. Build sprint's
  button from the same component and its placement
  (`src/ui/cut_buttons.ts`, merged in 5bb8b6f; see the Handoff in
  `docs/design/K114_cut.md`), plus a free key (`src/app/input_keys.ts`:
  X, Esc and U are the cut's).

## Design

(Appended by the K137 s3a agent before any code.) Where each rule hooks
in, on each clock.

K137-b, BROKEN RUNS BLOCK:
- The minotaurs count their own crossings from the controller's limits
  (`setBreakLimits(fc.breakLimits)`, main.ts `restartChase` when
  `fc.breakable`), one per accepted player run: the turn clock in
  `TurnMinotaur.turn(edge)` (edge >= 0, after the turn it plays and before
  the exit check), real time at the player's `depart` push in
  `movePlayer` (where main.ts calls `fc.tapEdge`). So the minotaur's own
  replay (`inputs`, `replayChase(..., limits)`) rebuilds the blocked set
  with no controller.
- Turn clock: `TurnGraph(walk | chase, blocked)` skips blocked runs in its
  BFS (unreachable = `UNREACH` 32767 while anything is blocked, so a
  `hopd <= sight` test never reads -1 as near); `withBlocked()` copies
  (the ChaseGraph is shared, never mutated). `moves()` skips blocked
  runs; a Spot on a blocked run goes only ahead. The turn that plays over
  the breaking move was committed before it (call 1: it finishes a run it
  is on); the next `commit()` routes around.
- Real time: `ChaseGraph.block(eid)` (restricted searches skip blocked
  runs; the restricted cache is cleared); a Spot on a blocked run reaches
  the graph only through its ahead end. Roam picks, the guard's territory
  (recomputed), random runs, stalker choices, aimed choices and plan()
  skip blocked runs. A player on a blocked run is hunted at the vertex the
  run leads to (the hunter waits there instead of following onto it).
  Contact stays the unrestricted thread distance.
- The fire: after a move that breaks a run, `refire(walk, fire, L,
  broken)` keeps every arrival time at or before L (the move's END clock
  on both clocks: walkedDist on the turn clock, departure + length in
  real time) and recomputes the rest from those vertices over unbroken
  runs. The controller holds the refired fire (`reset()` restores the
  built one), and replay(taps) rebuilds it.

LIVES (4.32): `lives` 0..5 (0 off). main.ts keeps `heartsLeft`, refilled
by `restartSolve` (and so by a Reset path and the out-of-hearts restart).
A catch, where it lands (tickChase's "caught", `turnCaught`), takes one
heart and does today's respawn; the last heart restarts the solve with
tallies carried and toasts "Out of hearts! Starting over.". While on,
`minoRestart` is hidden and ignored.

THE SWORD (4.7) and SPRINT (4.29): hooks as the brief's Decided section;
they land only if the cap leaves time (their remainder goes to the
Handoff).

### The sword (4.7), unbuilt: where it hooks in

As the Decided section says, plus:
- **Params**, appended after `lives`: `sword` (off | timed | one-use) and
  `swordMoves` ([4, 20], 8; PARAM_RANGES). Keys are unused short ones,
  grepped in url_state.ts. LIVE_EFFECTS: none (a change restarts the
  solve, onClockChange).
- **The spot**, in a pure `src/game/sword.ts`:
  - `swordSpot(walk, optimal, lair, territory, seed)` runs a multi-source
    BFS in runs from the optimal route's vertices and keeps the vertices
    4-8 runs off it, outside the lair and the territory;
  - it takes the dead-end tips among them, if any, else the junctions;
  - it sorts them by id and draws one from its own counter stream on the
    seed (a new `TurnRng` stream id);
  - with no candidate there is no sword, and the HUD says so.
- **Pickup:** a move that ends on the spot (`turnAfterMove`; real time:
  the player's arrival in the chase) starts the flee with `swordMoves`
  left.
- **The flee:**
  - turn clock: `TurnMinotaur` gets a flee state. `commit()` re-commits
    at once (it reverses), and each turn takes the move that maximizes
    the hop distance from the player (ties from its stream; by length,
    the same over length distances);
  - real time: `MinotaurChase` turns round on its run and, at each
    junction, `chooseRun` takes the run that maximizes the restricted
    distance from the player;
  - the count falls once per player move (turn) or per run walked (real
    time, call 3).
- **Touch while it flees:**
  - no catch: guard `turnCaught`, tickChase's "caught" and the turn
    clock's decision at the tap;
  - timed: the minotaur goes back to its lair and waits there (the
    lair-after-catch path);
  - one-use: the chase stops for the attempt, the marker hides and the
    HUD reads "slain on move N".
- **Look:**
  - the minotaur's marker and telegraph turn pale blue while it flees
    (fabric_maze_renderer.ts, telegraph_route.ts);
  - a sword glyph lies at the spot until it is taken (a new design in
    marker_glyphs.ts, drawn by marker_mesh.ts).
- **HUD, share and hooks:**
  - the HUD row reads "ready", "N left", "used" or "slain on move N";
  - the share tag is "⚔️ on move 31";
  - `__knotChase().sword` and `__knotTurnMino().sword` give
    `{spot, phase, left, slainAt}`.
- **Replays** rebuild it from the player's path (pickup is a function of
  it).
- **Tests:**
  - the spot is deterministic and in range;
  - the flee per model on both clocks;
  - banish and slay;
  - no catch while it flees;
  - replays;
  - off is bit-identical (the four game pins).

### Sprint (4.29), unbuilt: where it hooks in

- **Param**, appended after the sword's: `sprint` (off | on),
  onClockChange.
- **State:** one per attempt. It is refilled where `refillHearts` is (main.ts
  `restartChase`) and armed by the button or the key.
- **Turn clock:**
  - `turnAfterMove` skips the minotaur's turn after the first sprinted
    move. A walk into the minotaur still catches, so contact is still
    checked;
  - it plays one turn after the second move;
  - by length, that turn travels half the pair's walked length (call 5).
- **Real time:** the next two runs play at twice the player's pace,
  counted by departures in `movePlayer`; main's dot follows the chase's
  pace.
- **UI:**
  - a button from `src/ui/cut_buttons.ts` beside the scissors;
  - a key in `src/app/input_keys.ts`, not X, Esc, U, Z, WASD, the arrows,
    `.` or Space. Grep for a free one first; Q is a candidate;
  - the HUD row reads "ready", "armed" or "used";
  - the share tag is "⚡ used";
  - the hooks gain `sprint`.
- **Tests:**
  - two moves make one minotaur turn, on both counts;
  - once per attempt, and a restart gives it back;
  - real time's pace for two runs;
  - replays;
  - off is bit-identical.
