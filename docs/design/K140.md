# K140 stage 1: finish confetti

Levi's note (2026-10-13 about 15:35, verbatim in
`docs/design/levi_notes_2026-10-13.md`): "I want a gratuitous confetti
effect rainy down from the top of the screen when you finish the maze.
And I want some smaller confetti effect spraying out from aruond the
finish spot."

K155 (a star rating on the win banner) runs beside you in its own
worktree. It owns `src/ui/maze_hud.ts`, `src/ui/daily_stats_view.ts`
and `src/game/share_text.ts`: don't edit them. Hook the finish where
`src/main.ts` already reacts to it.

## What to build

- On every finish (dailies, free play, replays, K80's real-time mode):
  - a rain falling from the top of the screen, in screen space;
  - a smaller spray bursting from around the finish marker, in world
    space, so it stays on the finish as the camera moves.
- Render-only. It never wakes the physics, and the frame loop runs only
  while pieces live: `sceneAnimating()` in `src/main.ts` (K24's ticker
  stop) must report the effect and return to false after the last piece.
- Cheap on phones: the round's phone pass checks the frame rate during
  the effect (Pixel class, threads 4-5 px at fit scale). Draw the pieces
  in one batched mesh or particle container, with capped counts.
  `src/render/appear_fx.ts` (reveal-front particles) is the nearest
  existing pattern.
- Colors: your call (the design's palette or section colors, or a fixed
  festive set); say why in the report.
- With the Motion setting reduced (`src/ui/motion.ts`), a small burst or
  none. Propose which, with a screenshot.
- Params: on/off and an amount, appended to codec v4's table. They are
  viewer preferences: add them to `DAILY_EXEMPT_KEYS`
  (`src/ui/daily_link.ts`) beside `showStats`, update its test, and name
  them in your report for K57.
- It must not block input or hide the win banner (DOM over the canvas).

## What must not change

- The dailies: the golden daily fingerprints and K87's pin stay green, and
  the bare URL still opens today's daily.
- Knot mode stays byte-identical (`test/physics/byte_identity.test.ts`).
- Links: codec v4 appends only. K155 may also append, so the second branch
  to merge renumbers.

## Verification

## Setup

- Link `node_modules` to the main checkout's with a junction
  (`New-Item -ItemType Junction node_modules -Target
  C:\Users\lsl\Repositories\knotmaze\node_modules`).
- Ports 5480-5489 are yours; check that a port is free before binding.
- Serve with a private vite cacheDir. Use the K80 wrapper
  (`C:\Users\lsl\Repositories\knotmaze\.claude\probes\K80\rt\vite.private-cache.config.ts`,
  copied into your probes folder): it loads the real config with
  `loadConfigFromFile`. The older K19-K20 wrapper fails since K122
  (K178).
- `scripts/diag_screenshot.mjs` uses one fixed Chrome profile, so two
  agents collide (K150). Launch Chrome with your own `--user-data-dir`
  under your probes folder (K80's `rt_page.mjs` in the same folder shows
  how). For builds, `scripts/preview.ps1 -Root <your dir>`.
- Probes and results go in your worktree's `.claude/probes/K140/`
  (untracked; the coordinator copies them at the merge).

## Process

- One stage. Commit WIP on your branch at every milestone and never push.
  Don't edit `LEDGER.md`.
- Lore goes in `.claude/rules/render.md` (the effect, its cost, the
  animating flag), a history line in `docs/history.md`, and a structure
  line in CLAUDE.md for each new file.
- Stop at the FIRST of:
  1. a compaction summary in your context ("This session is being
     continued from a previous conversation...");
  2. two hours of wall clock since your start (run `Get-Date` at the start
     and at each milestone);
  3. about 150k tokens of context.

  Then commit WIP, append a "Stage 1 handoff" section to this brief (what
  is done, what is next, evidence paths), commit it and report. Don't
  start anything beyond this stage. Helpers: sonnet for mechanical work,
  each told this same stop rule.
- Final report: what changed and where, the defaults and why, screenshot
  paths, the frame-time table, a playable link for Levi, a `Ledger items`
  list (follow-ups, residuals, decisions for Levi) and the handle fields
  (branch, last commit, evidence, next).
