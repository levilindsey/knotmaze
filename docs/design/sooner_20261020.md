## Conventions

- **Estimates** are agent wall-clock hours at the cap: opus 2 h, sonnet
  1 h.
- **The hotspot** is the param rule. A stage that appends params edits
  `url_state.ts` `PARAM_DEFAULTS`, `param_codec.ts` `CODEC_PARAMS`
  (append-only, `CODEC_VERSION` 8, last row `foilShare`),
  `tweakpane_panel.ts` and the key-order pin in
  `test/ui/param_codec.test.ts`. Whoever merges second:
  - rebases;
  - re-appends its rows after the other's and renumbers the index
    comments;
  - re-freezes the key-order pin and `url_codec.test.ts`'s defaults table.

  Every brief here carries this rule.
- **Keys already claimed** by briefs: K324 stage 1 `mhn mhx mhh mrv mdr mnr
  mtn`; the confetti lane `cfc cfn cfs`; K137 stage 1 `fg pa par`; K525
  `mtl`. Verified free for later stages: `sp2` (spool), `hn` / `hi` /
  `hnt` (hints), `mtk mtt mtv mts`. `pr` is taken (pulseReach).
- **Two warm-up and registry rules:**
  - A feature that puts the fabric on the explore programs extends K307's
    burn rule in `shader_warmup.ts`.
  - A new GL program registers in `program_registry.ts`.

  Otherwise the first use compiles cold and freezes the screen.
