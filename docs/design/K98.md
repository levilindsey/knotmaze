### What is done

- **The param.** `markerSize` is the player's disc diameter in thread
  widths: 0.25-3, default 1.08 (`MARKER_SIZE_DEFAULT` and
  `sanitizeMarkerSize` in `marker_glyphs.ts`). It is codec index 104
  (`num`), in `PARAM_DEFAULTS`, override key `mks`, and in
  `DAILY_EXEMPT_KEYS`. Its slider is in the maze folder after the
  confetti rows, shown in fabric mode only. A change sets
  `fabric.markerSize` and renders one frame without waking physics.
- **One size.** `markerRadiusFor(tw, size, scale)` = size/2 · tw ·
  scale, with no floor. The head, start, finish and minotaur discs all
  wear it. K83's `max(0.55, 1.5 tw)` is gone, and so are the minotaur's
  1.15x and its 10 CSS px floor (`MINO_SCALE`, `MINO_MIN_CSS_R`,
  `minoMinRadius`).
- **Colored dots.** `markerDotRadiusFor` = max(0.95 tw, r + 0.41 tw).
  The start and finish quads draw the dot behind the disc in the same
  SDF shader (`marker_mesh.ts`: `uRing`, `uFrame`, `setRingColor`;
  `place(x, y, r, outer)` sizes the quad to the dot). The dot's color
  is the section color under the marker
  (`RibbonRenderer.sectionColorAt`, so the burn and roots intros color
  it too). The gap slots (occluders), the win ring (1.32 dot radii),
  the finish indicator's on-screen test and the confetti burst read the
  dot radius. The head and minotaur indicators read the disc radius.
- **Glyphs.** `horns` (Taurus) is the minotaur's default, and the bull's
  head stays as a glyph. `spider` is a new role and glyph: a two-dot
  body and eight mirrored legs. Nothing draws it (it has no mesh) until
  K114's call 7.
- **Tests** (`marker_glyphs`, `daily_link`, `param_codec`,
  `url_codec`): the glyphs' fit, the size, the ring at 0.25, 1.08 and 3,
  the codec append, the exemption and the `mks` key.
- **Lore**, in this branch's pre-K173 files: `render.md` "Marker icons
  (K83)" plus a new K98 paragraph; in `gameplay.md`, the finish
