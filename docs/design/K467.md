# K467 + K468: row help on touch, and a full-screen settings sheet on phones

One build stage, Sonnet, a one-hour cap. At launch the coordinator names

(row help) and K468 (the full-screen sheet).

## Levi's words (2026-10-15 ~15:58)

"I want to be able to provide tooltip type information for param
controls on mobile. How should we do that? With info icon buttons? With
long presses? Also, I think the settings menu should be full screen on
mobile."

The coordinator recommended (in reply to him): tap the row's label to
open its help inline, with a small (i) mark as the hint, and no long
press. The reasons: a long press is invisible, and on a slider row it
starts a drag or Chrome's text-selection callout. Separate (i) buttons
are small targets next to the reset glyph, on about 150 rows. An inline
block needs no popover placement and stays open while the slider moves.

## Today (`src/ui/tweakpane_panel.ts`)

- Every adjustable row gets its help text as a native `title` (`row()`,
  about l.474; buttons in `button()`). Phones never show a `title`.
  `markOverridden` prefixes it with OVERRIDDEN_NOTE and keeps the
  original in `dataset.kpTip`.
- The reset glyph (`.kp-reset`) sits inside the label (`.tp-lblv_l`); its
  pointerdown stops propagation.
- Phones: `@media (pointer:coarse)` gives the panel min(320px, 100vw -
  20px) and 30 px rows (about l.410). `--kp-top` places the panel under
  the hamburger row and the mode badge row (K92, K151). The panel starts
  closed under `(max-width: 700px), (pointer: coarse)` unless
  `knotmaze.panelOpen` in localStorage says otherwise (about l.2197).
- Undo and redo show left of the hamburger while the panel is open
  (`kp-open` on the root; `undo_buttons.ts`).
- The app never pushes history: it replaces the address bar on every
  design change (`url_state.ts`, main.ts). Nothing listens for popstate.
- K198's fit-camera pan range reads the panel's rect
  (gameplay-camera.md, FIT CAMERA PAN RANGE).

Lore: `.claude/rules/gameplay-panel.md`, `gameplay-badge.md`,
`gameplay-camera.md`.

## The work, in this order

1. **K468: the full-screen sheet.**
   - When: `(max-width: 700px)`, or a coarse pointer with
     `(max-height: 500px)` (a phone in landscape). Tablets and desktops
     keep today's side panel.
   - The open panel fills the viewport, safe-area insets included,
     opaque, and scrolls inside itself. Rows use the full width, with
     the value column scaled to the width.
   - The hamburger becomes a close button, and undo and redo stay
     beside it in a top bar. The mode badge keeps a visible place.
   - Android back closes the sheet. Push a history entry on open, and
     close on popstate. After the pop, write the current design's link
     again through url_state's writer, so the address bar keeps the
     edits made while the sheet was open. A close by the button pops the
     entry.
   - On a phone layout a load starts with the sheet closed, whatever
     `knotmaze.panelOpen` holds.
   - A rotation or resize switches between the sheet and the side panel
     live.
   - The sheet must not widen K198's pan range. If that needs
     `src/app/camera.ts`, make the smallest change there.
2. **K467: row help.**
   - Tapping or clicking a row's label (not its reset glyph) toggles a
     help block inside that row, under the label and the control,
     showing the row's tooltip text: the same string, OVERRIDDEN_NOTE
     included. There is one source for the text, never a copy.
   - One block is open at a time. It hides with its row (refreshCond)
     and with its folder.
   - On the phone layouts, labels that have help show a small muted
     (i) after the text. Desktop keeps its hover tooltip and shows no
     mark, so its panel stays pixel-identical while no block is open.
   - The label is a button for assistive tech: role, tabindex,
     aria-expanded, aria-controls, Enter and Space.
   - Check that a tap on a checkbox row's label does not toggle the box.
   - Buttons and non-row elements (seed, dice, Daily, stats, links) get
     no help in this stage. List the ones whose tooltip says something a
     player needs.
3. **The peek** (for Levi's review): while a slider is being dragged on
   the sheet, everything but that row turns invisible (`visibility`, so
   the row can stay visible inside hidden parents), and the sheet's
   background clears. The player watches the design change live, and the
   sheet returns on release.
