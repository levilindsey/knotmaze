## The decision (Levi, 2026-10-15)

The coordinator asked: (1) which preset for the real-time fire, Standard
0.2 / 20 (recommended), Brisk 0.35 / 30, Easy 0.15 / 0 or Relaxed
0.1 / 30; (2) should real time get its own defaults, so the turn game
keeps 0.75 / 0 (recommended: yes), with the speed slider and link range
going down to 0.1 either way. Levi: "K440: yes and yes."

So:
- **Real time:** fireSpeed 0.2, fireDelay 20 (stage 2's "Standard": a
  1.7 s head start; on all 30 test dailies the best route survives a 1 s
  pause at every junction).
- **Turn:** unchanged, 0.75 / 0.
- **Ranges:** speed [0.1, 0.95] under both clocks, in the panel and the
  link clamp (FIRE_SPEED_MIN 0.3 -> 0.1); delay stays [0, 40].

## The work

1. **The real-time pair: two new params**, `fireSpeedRt` and
   `fireDelayRt` (override keys `fsr` and `fdr` if free), defaults 0.2 and
   20, clamped like the turn pair. APPEND them to the codec table after
   its current last index; CODEC_VERSION stays. Neither is daily-exempt
   nor a MAZE key, like fsp and fdl. Why two params rather than a default
   that depends on the clock: a link lists only what differs from
   PARAM_DEFAULTS (K132), so `?<seed>&fi=fuse&fck=realtime` must play
   0.2 / 20 with nothing else listed, and a turn link must keep 0.75 / 0.
   A cleaner design that keeps both links right is fine; say why.
2. **`fireSettingsOf`** returns the pair of the design's clock.
3. **FIRE_SPEED_MIN 0.1**, read by both pairs' clamps and rows.
4. **The panel** shows one speed row and one delay row: the turn pair's
   under the turn clock, the real-time pair's under realtime. Same labels;
   each reset glyph returns its own pair's default. A clock change swaps
   the rows without firing their handlers (Tweakpane's refresh fires
   change handlers: `quietRefresh` / the `syncing` guard,
   gameplay-panel.md). Recommended order: fire, clock, speed, delay,
   burnt (the clock before the rows that depend on it). The real-time
   speed's tooltip says what the fraction is of (the dot's fixed pace,
   v_ref) and the delay's what it means under the clock. A seconds
   readout is K448, out of scope.
5. **`undo_stack.ts`:** the new keys under onClockChange, the callback
   their rows run.
6. **Tests:**
   - `test/ui/param_codec.test.ts`'s table pin and `url_codec.test.ts`'s
     defaults table and OVERRIDE_KEYS gain the keys;
   - fireSettingsOf cases: realtime reads the real-time pair, turn the
     turn pair, both clamp at 0.1 and 0.95;
   - `?<seed>&fi=fuse&fck=realtime` decodes to 0.2 / 20 and round-trips to
     a link that lists neither;
   - any panel test that covers the fire rows.

   Then `npm test` and `npm run build`. The golden snapshot, K87 and the
   golden dailies must not move (a daily plays with the fire off); if one
   does, stop and report.
7. **One browser check** at the phone viewport (411x753): on
   `?<seed>&fi=fuse&fck=realtime&snd=none&am=instant` the panel shows
   0.2 / 20 and the fire starts about 1.7 s after the first move; on the
   same link without `fck`, the turn race still plays 0.75 / 0. Stage 2's
   `rt_page.mjs` (the main checkout's `.claude/probes/K114/stage2/`)
   drives a real-time race; adapt a copy. No frame timing.
8. **Lore:**
   - gameplay-links.md: a REAL-TIME FIRE PAIR entry (indices, keys,

   - gameplay-panel.md: FIRE CLOCK ROW (its ranges sentence becomes false;
     the rows by clock, the order);
