Columns:
- **Folder**: the folder path, outermost first.
- **Label · key**: the label the panel shows, then the param or setting key (the label is the key when no `label` is given).
- **Codec**: the index in `CODEC_PARAMS`, then the short key of a key=value link.
- **Type · default · range**: the codec type (else the value's), today's default, the slider range and step, and the options (`label=value` where they differ).
- **Kind**: `design`, a link param a daily fixes; `pref`, a link param in `DAILY_EXEMPT_KEYS` (a daily keeps the viewer's value); `setting`, a `PanelSettings` viewer preference kept per browser, never in a link; `maze` marks a `MAZE_PARAM_KEYS` param (it changes the maze itself).
- **Hidden when**: the row's line in `refreshCond`, with `params.` dropped (blank: it shows whenever its folder shows). Its folders' rules apply too (see Folders).
- **Shows**: whether the row shows at today's defaults (maze mode), and in knot mode (`mf=0`); `in closed X` means a collapsed folder hides it until opened.
- **Effect**: the `PanelCallbacks` its change runs (onRegenerate rebuilds the design; the others apply live or restart the solve).
- **Added**: the commit whose diff first added the key to the panel file, and the ledger item in its subject.
- **Read by**: the source files that read `.key`, outside the ui plumbing (panel, url_state, param_codec, daily_link), with counts; a heuristic.

## Tooltips

- `reducedMotion`: Master switch: stills what the Motion setting covers (backdrop drift, plucks, the camera's ease to an edge marker, the loading knot, mode badge and edge markers) AND the maze's flow undulation (flowStrength and flowSpeed keep your values and come back when it is off). The intro animation and the pulses beside the player keep playing. Shows what is in force: it follows your device's reduce-motion setting until you choose (Motion & intro holds the detailed choice, including follow system). Remembered per browser.
- `highContrast`: Master switch. While on it overrides, and dims, these rows (your values come back when it is off): background = none and a black page (no texture behind or through the crossings), palette = pastel (the lightest threads), pathColor = yellow, fadeLength = 0 (crossings cut crisp), gap at least 0.12 (a wider dark line between over and under threads), markerSize at least 1.5. Follows your device's high-contrast setting (prefers-contrast: more, forced colors) until you choose. Remembered per browser.
- `gridSize`: Lattice size. Bigger = a larger, denser knot with more threads. Regenerates the design.
- `shape`: Lattice silhouette: the knotwork grows only inside this shape (ring keeps a real hole in the middle). Regenerates the design.
- `startAnchor`: Which side of the shape hosts the maze ENTRANCE (compass point). Regenerates the maze.
- `exitAnchor`: Which side of the shape hosts the maze EXIT (compass point). Regenerates the maze.
- `weaveDensity`: How much of the lattice fills with woven thread. Low = sparse open tangle, high = tight fabric. Regenerates the design.
- `turnBias`: How often growing threads turn instead of running straight. Low = long runs, high = curly meanders. Regenerates the design.
- `seed`: The design's seed - the first part of the link. Type a seed (or any word) and press Enter to load that design; the dice rolls a random one.
- `mazeFabric`: Branching maze: walk the thread from the green entrance to the orange exit. Tap a highlighted run at your branch point to advance (tap the gold one to backtrack); tug anywhere else.
- `clock`: Turn-based: the maze waits for each move. Real time: you run the threads at your own pace while a minotaur roams them from the finish; it hunts you once you come close, and a catch sends you back to the entrance (counted in the stats). No undo. Restarts the solve.
- `playerPace`: Real time: your pace along the threads, in lattice units per second. Applies live.
- `deadEnds`: Dead ends: some loops become long threads that end, somewhere far away, in a loose tip. Off = a pure braided maze where every wrong turn loops back. Regenerates the maze.
- `deadEndDensity`: Fraction of eligible loops cut into dead-end threads. 1 = maximally tree-like. Regenerates the maze.
- `deadEndLength`: How far each dead end runs before it ends (half lattice cells of thread with no junction on it): longer tails take longer to find out. 0 = the older placement, spread out and mostly short. Regenerates the maze.
- `hCutShare`: Fused arm cuts (H1): the share of bridges between two neighbouring threads replaced by one smooth junction, so the bridge's two junctions become one. 0 = off, every bridge kept. Regenerates the maze.
- `branchDensity`: How many extra choice junctions the maze adds beyond the minimum needed to connect everything. Regenerates the maze.
- `fourWayFraction`: Share of junctions realized as 4-way crossings instead of 3-way splits. Regenerates the maze.
- `junctionSpacing`: Minimum distance between junctions (half lattice cells): higher spreads the choices out, so fewer fit. Regenerates the maze.
- `entranceClearance`: Junction-free radius around the entrance (half lattice cells): the first stretch is a single path. Regenerates the maze.
- `exitClearance`: Junction-free radius around the exit (half lattice cells), so the endgame isn't cluttered with choices. Regenerates the maze.
- `pathViz`: How your route is shown: fullPath = your whole traveled route stays recolored; recentPath = memory mode: only the last stretch of your route stays lit (trailLength sets how much) and there is no backtracking; string = unroll a thin white yarn behind you - it U-turns at junctions when you backtrack and is never picked up. Applies live.
- `cameraMode`: Camera: fit = frame the whole maze (under fog: just what you can see); follow = chase your position. Pinch, scroll or drag to zoom and pan in either - in follow mode a gentle pull brings the view back after a moment. Double-click / double-tap empty space to reset the view. Applies live.
- `trailLength`: How much of your recent path stays lit in recentPath mode, in lattice units. Applies live.
- `tension`: Ariadne string tension: 1 lays the yarn taut along the thread, 0 leaves it loose and wavy like slack cotton. Applies live.
- `fogOfWar`: Fog of war: the maze hides until you have explored close by; explored areas stay visible. Applies live.
- `fowRadius`: How far around your explored path the fog lifts, in lattice units. Applies live and reshapes everything already explored.
- `fowIntro`: Play the intro appearance animation in the open, then close the fog in around you (it fades in while its reveal radius contracts). Applies to the next intro.
- `moveSpeed`: Maze travel speed: how fast the dot and the gold front move along the thread. Applies live.
- `pulseSpacing`: Distance between traveling pulses on the current junction's arms. Applies live.
- `pulseReach`: How far the current junction's pulses, and the hover highlight on a choice, reach along each arm, in lattice units, before fading out. Applies live.
- `hoverGlow`: Hover glow: the run under the pointer (or under a touch press) glows softly along both edges near it, fading toward its junctions; on one of your choices it adds to the choice's own highlight. 0 turns it off. Applies live.
- `hoverGlowLength`: Hover glow length: how far the hover glow reaches along the thread from the pointer, in lattice units, before it has faded out. Applies live.
- `hoverGlowFade`: Hover glow end fade: how long the hover glow takes to fade in past each end of the run, where the junctions cover it, in lattice units (0 = a hard edge). Applies live.
- `hoverGlowWidth`: Hover glow thickness: how far in from each edge of the thread the hover glow reaches, as a share of the half-width (1 = the two edges' glows meet in the middle). Applies live.
- `showMazeStats`: Show the maze readout in the top-left corner: solve time (from your first move to arriving at the exit), your path vs the optimal route, efficiency once solved, the distance you backtracked, junctions visited and (with dead ends on) dead ends entered. Remembered per browser.
- `finishIndicator`: While the finish is off screen, a marker at the edge of the screen points the way to it (under fog of war too, as a direction only). Remembered per browser.
- `headIndicator`: While your own position is off screen (you panned away, or a long run outpaced the camera), a marker at the edge of the screen points back to it. Remembered per browser.
- `minotaurIndicator`: In a real-time chase, while the minotaur is off screen, a marker at the edge of the screen points to it. Remembered per browser.
- `indicatorSize`: The size of the edge markers (the finish's, your position's, the minotaur's): 1 = the default. The markers in the maze have their own size (markerSize). Remembered per browser.
- `tapToPan`: Tapping an edge marker (the finish's, your own position's, the minotaur's) brings what it points at into view. Off: the markers only point, and taps go through them to the maze. Remembered per browser.
- `movePad`: A four-way pad in the bottom-right corner that moves like the arrow keys: press a direction to take the run that best matches it, and keep it pressed to keep going at each junction until nothing matches. On by default on touch screens. Remembered per browser.
- `confetti`: Celebrate the finish: confetti rains from the top of the screen and bursts from around the exit. With reduced motion (the Motion setting), only a small, slow burst at the exit.
- `confettiAmount`: How much confetti a finish throws: 1 = the default, 0 = none, up to 3x.
- `markerSize`: Marker size: the diameter of your position marker, in thread widths. The start, the finish and the minotaur match it; the start and the finish keep a ring of their thread's color around the icon. Applies live.
- `fire`: Turn-based: a fuse lit at the entrance burns along every thread behind you, slower than you walk, and burnt threads are gone. A run whose far end the fire reaches first smolders and refuses your tap. Trapped with no way left, you burn and the race starts over. Backtracking feeds the fire. Not with the minotaur: turning one on turns the other off. Restarts the solve.
- `fireSpeed`: The fire's speed as a fraction of yours: at 0.75 it burns 3 units of thread while you walk 4. Restarts the solve.
- `fireDelay`: How far you walk before the fire starts to spread, in lattice units (a run is about 4). Restarts the solve.
- `minotaur`: Turn-based: a minotaur that moves when you move, from its lair at the finish. Hunter: roams, hunts you once you come within sight, then checks where it last saw you. Guard: the hunter, keeping to the ground around the finish. Stalker: always hunts you. Running into it, or it into you, sends you back to the entrance (counted); the entrance tail is a sanctuary. Tap your own position to wait a turn. No undo while it is on. Restarts the solve.
- `minoSpeed`: Runs it takes each turn: half = one run every other turn; one; charge = one, two while it hunts you; two. Charge and two always show its next move. Applies live.
- `minoTelegraph`: Show its next move in red: the run it will take and where it will stand, decided when your move ends (forced on at charge and two). Applies live.
- `minoSight`: Sight, in runs: it hunts you once you are this few runs away, and gives up beyond 3 more. It never sees you on the entrance tail. Applies live.
- `minoRoamRuns`: Hunter: the farthest it roams in one go, in runs (its nearest destination is 3 runs away, or this if less). Applies live.
- `minoTerritory`: Guard: it roams only to junctions within this many runs of the finish. Applies live.
- `minoAccuracy`: While it hunts (the stalker: always), the chance each step takes a shortest way to you; otherwise it steps at random. Applies live.
- `minoPause`: The chance it stands still for a turn. Applies live.
- `minoSleep`: Turns it waits in its lair before it first moves, at the start and after each catch. Applies live.
- `minoWanderPace`: The minotaur's pace while it roams, as a multiple of your pace (playerPace): 0.5 = half as fast as you. Applies live.
- `minoHuntPace`: Its pace while it hunts you, along the shortest way to you, as a multiple of your pace: below 1 you outrun it by moving away. Applies live.
- `minoPaceJitter`: Pace variety: each roaming leg runs at the wander pace times 1 ± up to this, at random. Applies live.
- `minoPauseChance`: Chance it stops for a while at a junction it roams through. Applies live.
- `minoPauseMin`: Its shortest pause, in seconds. Applies live.
- `minoPauseMax`: Its longest pause, in seconds (at least the shortest). Applies live.
- `minoDetect`: It starts hunting you once you are this close, measured along the threads (lattice units). Applies live.
- `minoLose`: It loses you once you are farther than this along the threads (never less than the distance above). Applies live.
- `minoCatch`: Catch distance: closer than this along the threads and it catches you, sending you back to the entrance. Applies live.
- `minoDeadEnds`: How often it roams to a dead-end tip instead of a junction: dead ends are its hunting ground. Applies live.
- `minoRoam`: The farthest it roams in one go, along the threads (lattice units). Applies live.
- `minoToward`: It roams toward you: some of its roaming legs head only for places nearer you along the threads, so it finds you more often. Off: it roams at random. A player on the entrance or exit tail pulls nothing. Applies live.
- `minoWake`: Seconds it waits in its lair at the finish, at the start and after each catch. Applies live.
- `minoFog`: With fog of war: show the minotaur always, only while it hunts you, or never (only where the fog has lifted). Applies live.
- `minoRestart`: A catch restarts the level: your path, the fog and the clock start over, and the minotaur goes back to its lair. Your catches still count. Off: a catch sends you back to the entrance and the solve goes on (the rows below say what else resets). Applies from the next catch.
- `minoLair`: A catch sends the minotaur back to its lair at the finish. Off: it stays where it caught you, and roams on from there (in real time after its wake pause). Applies from the next catch.
- `minoRefog`: With fog of war: a catch returns the fog to how it stood at the start of the solve. Off: what you explored stays uncovered. Applies from the next catch.
- `colorScheme`: Section coloring: mono = one color; strands = per-thread hues; depth = gradient by distance from the start; segments = a random gamut color per run between intersections; noise = a spatial dye field. Sections blend smoothly through intersections. Applies live.
- `junctionBlend`: How far (in lattice units) each intersection's blended color bleeds into the sections meeting there, beyond the short built-in blend. 0 = the blend stays inside the intersection; a section shorter than twice this becomes one continuous gradient between its two intersections. Segments and strands schemes. Applies live.
- `hueShift`: Rotates the whole color gamut around the hue wheel (added to the seed's base hue). Applies live.
- `gamutArc`: Width of the hue arc colors are drawn from: narrow = one tight family, 360 = the full rainbow. Applies live.
- `palette`: Saturation/lightness character of the gamut. Applies live.
- `pathColor`: Color of the traveled-path highlight (the classic gold). Applies live.
- `threadWidth`: Ribbon width of every thread, in lattice units.
- `gap`: Visible break in the under thread at each crossing - the classic over/under gap.
- `fadeLength`: How far the under thread fades out approaching a crossing gap. 0 = sharp cut. Applies live.
- `cuspRadius`: How far the rounded corners reach where threads join at an intersection. 0 = sharp joints. Applies live.
- `background`: Backdrop behind the knot, in the design's own colors (it shows through the crossing gaps too): none = the plain page; vellum = dark parchment grain (still unless given a speed); mist = slow marbled smoke; wash = drifting watercolor blooms; silk = long flowing streaks; aurora = soft curtains of light; contours = the contour lines of a slowly shifting map. Everything but vellum drifts very slowly (backgroundSpeed sets how fast; reduced motion keeps every backdrop still). Applies live.
- `backgroundIntensity`: Backdrop contrast: 0 = the plain page color, 1 = the strongest look. Applies live.
- `backgroundSpeed`: How fast the backdrop moves: 0 = still, 1 = the effect's own pace, up to 8x. Vellum is still until you give it a speed (then its mottling drifts and its light wanders; the grain stays put). The reset glyph returns to each effect's own pace. Reduced motion (Motion & intro) keeps every backdrop still. Applies live.
- `backgroundScale`: Backdrop pattern size: 1 = as designed, 0.5 = half as large (finer), up to 2x larger. Applies live.
- `backgroundDetail`: Backdrop roughness (mist, wash, silk and contours): 0 = smooth, soft shapes; 1 = as designed; 2 = rough, the fine detail loud. The contrast stays the same. Applies live.
- `backgroundFibers`: Vellum's paper fibers: 0 = none, 1 = as designed, up to 3x stronger. Applies live.
- `backgroundGrain`: Vellum's fine grain: 0 = none, 1 = as designed, up to 3x stronger. Applies live.
- `backgroundAngle`: Silk's streak direction, in degrees counterclockwise from pointing right (0 = left to right, 90 = upward). Each design picks its own until you set one; the reset glyph returns to it. Applies live.
- `backgroundColorMode`: Backdrop colors: design = the page color plus the design's own colors (they follow hue, gamut and palette); custom = choose the page color and the accent colors yourself (they start from the design's). Applies live.
- `backgroundBase`: The page color: the backdrop's darkest tone, and the plain page behind the knot when there is no backdrop (and the fog of war over it). Applies live.
- `backgroundAccent1`: Accent 1, the backdrop's main color: vellum's tint, mist's glow, aurora's bright crests, one of wash's blooms. Applies live.
- `backgroundAccent2`: Accent 2: one end of the backdrop's color range (mist's smoke, silk's streaks, the contour lines, an aurora curtain, a wash bloom). Applies live.
- `backgroundAccent3`: Accent 3: the other end of the backdrop's color range. Applies live.
- `motion`: How much the page moves. follow system = your device's reduce-motion setting (it switches live); full = all motion; reduced = backdrops hold still, a plucked thread brightens briefly instead of vibrating (the sound is the same), the camera jumps to an edge marker's target instead of easing there, the loading knot, the mode badge and the edge markers stop breathing and fading, and the maze's flow undulation holds still. The Accessibility group's Reduced motion switch sets this same answer. Remembered per browser.
- `flowStrength`: Flow undulation: how far the whole fabric sways, as if drifting in water. 0 = perfectly still. Render-only - applies live, even while the knot sleeps. Reduced motion (Accessibility, or the Motion row above) holds it still without changing this value.
- `flowSpeed`: How fast the flow undulation drifts. Applies live.
- `appearMode`: Appearance preset: sets the sequence, effect and speed below together. random = new random ones every time the page loads; custom = keep them as set. Editing any of the three switches this to custom (or to the preset they match). Rebuilds the scene.
- `appearSequence`: How the design animates in: instant = appear fully drawn; trace = grow outward from the maze entrance, splitting at every junction; bloom = the same flood from several random spots at once; draw = pen-draw each strand end to end; ink = a dye wave spreading from the entrance. Rebuilds the scene.
- `appearSpeed`: How fast the appearance animation reveals the threads. Applies live.
- `appearFx`: Particle effect at the growing thread ends while the design draws itself in: rainbow = six saturated stripes ride each fresh thread behind a white-hot tip, fading to its true color, with a full-spectrum spark spray; comet = a blue-green streaking head, the thread materializing from transparent behind it; dust = dense twinkling glitter over threads that fade in; burn = the thread smolders in, orange tip charring to black with rising smoke; roots = brown rootlets probing in with a soft dirt spray. Applies live.
- `settleBudget`: Settle budget: at most this many milliseconds of physics per frame while the knot first settles after loading, so the intro stays smooth on slow devices - the settle plays in slow motion instead of the frame rate dropping. Ends when the knot first comes to rest (or at the latest after five minutes on screen). 0 = off: the physics keeps up with real time and the intro stutters while it settles. Applies at the next load or Replay settle.
- `loadSettle`: Load settle: seconds of physics to run before the knot appears, so it starts its intro already partly relaxed. A loading knot shows if this takes a noticeable moment. 0 = appear at once; the max = wait until the knot is fully at rest (settle first, then the intro). Applies at the next load or Replay settle.
- `pluckSize`: How wide a plucked thread swings. A tap (or an auto pluck) sets the thread between its crossings vibrating - visual only, nothing else moves. 0 = sound only. Applies live.
- `pluckGhost`: The speed cue on a vibrating thread: a faint crisp copy of the string flickering inside the blur. 0 = a pure time-exposure blur. Off under reduced motion (Motion & intro). Applies live.
- `pluckReach`: What a pluck sets vibrating: run = the whole maze segment you hear, each stretch between crossings humming (in knot mode, the plucked stretch and two either side); lobe = only the stretch between the two nearest crossings. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pluckWhole`: How far the whole plucked segment swings as one string, on top of the stretch you tapped (scales with pluckSize). 0 = only the tapped stretch and its neighbors move; 0.75 = the default; up to 1.5. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pluckCrossings`: How the whole segment's swing meets the crossings inside it: through = it carries across them, so the segment's blur stays one piece; ride = it stays within each crossing's soft limit (short stretches keep it near a thread width); pinch = it narrows to a waist at every crossing. Applies live.
- `pluckGlow`: A glow on the plucked segment, brightening toward white as it rings: off = movement only; instead = the glow replaces the whole segment's swing (the tapped stretch still moves); with = the swing and the glow; fallback = the glow only on plucks whose movement is too small to see (zoomed out, or hemmed in by other threads). Reduced motion always glows instead of moving. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pluckRipple`: A ripple spreading from where a thread is plucked, drawn over the threads (render only: nothing physically moves). wave = the threads it passes bend for a moment; glint = a bright band sweeps outward along them; ring = a soft ring expands from the pluck; off = none. Auto plucks ripple more faintly (rippleAuto). Off under reduced motion. Applies to the next pluck.
- `rippleStrength`: How strong your own plucks' ripple is (taps and drag releases). 0 = none; 1 = the default; up to 2. Applies to the next pluck.
- `rippleAuto`: How strong auto plucks' ripple is (the random and music sound modes), kept below your own so the knot's own music stays subtle. 0 = none. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pluckSheen`: A bright sheen at the edges of a vibrating thread's blur, where a real string spends most of its swing (like a long-exposure photo of one). 0 = none. Off under reduced motion. Applies live.
- `pluckTug`: A small real tug on the plucked stretch on top of the drawn vibration: the thread twitches toward the side you plucked and the knot settles back. 0 = drawn vibration only. None with physics off or under reduced motion. Applies to the next pluck.
- `tugAuto`: Auto plucks tug too (the random and music sound modes). Off = only your own plucks tug, so the knot can rest while the music plays. Applies to the next pluck.
- `autoPluckGain`: How loud auto plucks play (the random and music sound modes) next to your own: 1 = as loud as a tap; 0.5 = the default, half the level (-6 dB); 0 = silent. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pickMargin`: Mouse pick margin: how far past a thread's edge, in screen pixels, the mouse still hovers, clicks or grabs it (a pen too; touch keeps its own wider reach). 0 = only on the thread itself. Applies live.
- `pickChoiceRatio`: Choice pick ratio: the mouse pick margin of your next moves (the runs out of your current junction), as a multiple of the margin above, so a choice is easier to hit than other threads. 1 = the same margin. Applies live.
- `tugLimit`: Dragging a thread tugs it (taps pluck instead). How far a thread can be tugged, in thread widths; the stretch resists more as it nears the limit (drag further, move less). Applies live.
- `snapBack`: After a drag: how quickly the released thread eases back to where it was grabbed (soft release style). 0 = release in place. Applies live.
- `pluckStyle`: After a drag: string = let go at once - the knot snaps back and the released stretch vibrates like a plucked string; soft = ease home gently (speed = the snapBack slider), no vibration.
- `temperature`: Agitation. Shake the knot with noise; slide back to 0 and it settles into a new pose.
- `soundMode`: Pluck behavior: none = silent; manual = your plucks only; random = the knot plucks a random thread now and then; music = a scripted song plays itself on matching threads. Auto plucks make the thread ring (visual only - nothing else moves).
- `pitchSpace`: Scale the thread pitches live in: every maze segment gets one pitch by length (longer rings lower). chromatic = all 12 half steps - enough to play anything.
- `octaves`: How many octaves the segment pitches spread across.
- `rootNote`: Root note of the pluck scale. 'seed' derives it from the design seed.
- `tonicEmphasis`: How strongly the segment pitches gravitate to the key: more threads ring the tonic (up to 3x), the dominant (2x) and the third (1.5x). 0 = every scale note equally often.
- `soundSpeed`: Tempo of music mode and cadence of random plucks. 1 = normal.
- `volume`: Master output level.
- `reverb`: Hall reverb amount. 0 = dry strings, 1 = a wash of room.
- `brightness`: String brightness: low = felted and dark, high = wiry and bright. Applies from the next pluck.
- `sustain`: How long strings ring after a pluck.
- `shimmer`: Unison-course detune: the slow beating shimmer of paired strings. 0 = a single pure string.
- `pickPosition`: Where along the string the finger plucks: low = near the end (nasal, thin), high = near the middle (round, hollow).
- `muted`: Silence everything (also pauses the random/music auto-pluck modes).
- `physics`: Physics simulation. Off: each design settles behind a loading spinner, then its layout freezes - nothing simulates afterwards (lighter on battery and slow devices). Threads can still be plucked (and flow, pulses and the intro still play), but not tugged: a drag pans the view. Applies live - turning it off first lets a moving knot come to rest; the sliders below still shape the frozen layout.
- `simSpeed`: Simulation speed. 0 pauses, 5 is real time, 10 is 5x.
- `slack`: How loose the threads rest. 0 pulls the knot rope-taut, 1 leaves it soft and open.
- `drag`: Motion damping. Low = springy, waves ring after a pluck; high = movement dies quickly.
- `repulsion`: Breathing room between parallel threads. Higher spreads the composition apart.
- `bend`: Bending stiffness. Higher resists sharp curves - smoother, rounder thread paths.
- `stiffness`: Structural spring strength along each thread. Higher = firmer, less stretchy material.
- `smooth`: Curvature smoothing. Evens out wiggles along threads so they read as continuous curves.
- `gridAlign`: How strongly every thread is pulled back toward its lattice position, on an exponential scale (each tenth of the slider doubles the pull): 0 = free-flowing curves, 1 = a formal, grid-like composition. Applies live.
- `showDebug`: Overlay the physics debug view (particles, bindings, contacts).
- `showStats`: Show the build + performance readout (version, sim/wall time, frame cost) in the bottom-left corner. Remembered per browser.
