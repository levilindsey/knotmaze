- `appearSpeed`: How fast the appearance animation reveals the threads. Applies live.
- `appearFx`: Particle effect at the growing thread ends while the design draws itself in: rainbow = six saturated stripes ride each fresh thread behind a white-hot tip, fading to its true color, with a full-spectrum spark spray; comet = a blue-green streaking head, the thread materializing from transparent behind it; dust = dense twinkling glitter over threads that fade in; burn = the thread smolders in, orange tip charring to black with rising smoke; roots = brown rootlets probing in with a soft dirt spray. Applies live.
- `settleBudget`: Settle budget: at most this many milliseconds of physics per frame while the knot first settles after loading, so the intro stays smooth on slow devices - the settle plays in slow motion instead of the frame rate dropping. Ends when the knot first comes to rest (or at the latest after five minutes on screen). 0 = off: the physics keeps up with real time and the intro stutters while it settles. Applies at the next load or Replay settle.
- `loadSettle`: Load settle: seconds of physics to run before the knot appears, so it starts its intro already partly relaxed. A loading knot shows if this takes a noticeable moment. 0 = appear at once; the max = wait until the knot is fully at rest (settle first, then the intro). Applies at the next load or Replay settle.
- `pluckSize`: How wide a plucked thread swings. A tap (or an auto pluck) sets the thread between its crossings vibrating - visual only, nothing else moves. 0 = sound only. Applies live.
- `pluckGhost`: The speed cue on a vibrating thread: a faint crisp copy of the string flickering inside the blur. 0 = a pure time-exposure blur. Off under reduced motion (Motion & intro). Applies live.
- `pluckReach`: What a pluck sets vibrating: run = the whole maze segment you hear, each stretch between crossings humming (in knot mode, the plucked stretch and two either side); lobe = only the stretch between the two nearest crossings. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pluckWhole`: How far the whole plucked segment swings as one string, on top of the stretch you tapped (scales with pluckSize). 0 = only the tapped stretch and its neighbors move; 0.75 = the default; up to 1.5. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pluckCrossings`: How the whole segment's swing meets the crossings inside it: through = it carries across them, so the segment's blur stays one piece; ride = it stays within each crossing's soft limit (short stretches keep it near a thread width); pinch = it narrows to a waist at every crossing. Applies live.
- `pluckGlow`: A glow on the plucked segment, brightening toward white as it rings: off = movement only; instead = the glow replaces the whole segment's swing (the tapped stretch still moves); with = the swing and the glow; fallback = the glow only on plucks whose movement is too small to see (zoomed out, or hemmed in by other threads). Reduced motion always glows instead of moving. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pluckRipple`: A ripple spreading from where a thread is plucked, drawn over the threads (render only: nothing physically moves). wave = the threads it passes bend for a moment; glint = a bright band sweeps outward along them; ring = a soft ring expands from the pluck; off = none. Auto plucks ripple more faintly (rippleAuto). Off under reduced motion. Applies to the next pluck.
- `rippleStrength`: How strong your own plucks' ripple is (taps and drag releases). 0 = none; 1 = the default; up to 2. Applies to the next pluck.
- `rippleAuto`: How strong auto plucks' ripple is (the random and music sound modes), kept below your own so the knot's own music stays subtle. 0 = none. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pluckSheen`: A bright sheen at the edges of a vibrating thread's blur, where a real string spends most of its swing (like a long-exposure photo of one). 0 = none. Off under reduced motion. Applies live.
- `pluckTug`: A small real tug on the plucked stretch on top of the drawn vibration: the thread twitches toward the side you plucked and the knot settles back. 0 = drawn vibration only. None with physics off or under reduced motion. Applies to the next pluck.
- `tugAuto`: Auto plucks tug too (the random and music sound modes). Off = only your own plucks tug, so the knot can rest while the music plays. Applies to the next pluck.
- `pickMargin`: Mouse pick margin: how far past a thread's edge, in screen pixels, the mouse still hovers, clicks or grabs it (a pen too; touch keeps its own wider reach). 0 = only on the thread itself. Applies live.
- `pickChoiceRatio`: Choice pick ratio: the mouse pick margin of your next moves (the runs out of your current junction), as a multiple of the margin above, so a choice is easier to hit than other threads. 1 = the same margin. Applies live.
- `tugLimit`: Dragging a thread tugs it (taps pluck instead). How far a thread can be tugged, in thread widths; the stretch resists more as it nears the limit (drag further, move less). Applies live.
- `snapBack`: After a drag: how quickly the released thread eases back to where it was grabbed (soft release style). 0 = release in place. Applies live.
- `pluckStyle`: After a drag: string = let go at once - the knot snaps back and the released stretch vibrates like a plucked string; soft = ease home gently (speed = the snapBack slider), no vibration.
- `temperature`: Agitation. Shake the knot with noise; slide back to 0 and it settles into a new pose.
- `muted`: Silence everything (also pauses the random/music auto-pluck modes).
- `volume`: Master output level.
- `autoPluckGain`: How loud auto plucks play (the random and music sound modes) next to your own: 1 = as loud as a tap; 0.5 = the default, half the level (-6 dB); 0 = silent. Applies to the next pluck.
- `soundMode`: Pluck behavior: none = silent; manual = your plucks only; random = the knot plucks a random thread now and then; music = a scripted song plays itself on matching threads. Auto plucks make the thread ring (visual only - nothing else moves).
- `soundSpeed`: Tempo of music mode and cadence of random plucks. 1 = normal.
- `pitchSpace`: Scale the thread pitches live in: every maze segment gets one pitch by length (longer rings lower). chromatic = all 12 half steps - enough to play anything.
- `octaves`: How many octaves the segment pitches spread across.
- `rootNote`: Root note of the pluck scale. 'seed' derives it from the design seed.
- `tonicEmphasis`: How strongly the segment pitches gravitate to the key: more threads ring the tonic (up to 3x), the dominant (2x) and the third (1.5x). 0 = every scale note equally often.
- `reverb`: Hall reverb amount. 0 = dry strings, 1 = a wash of room.
- `brightness`: String brightness: low = felted and dark, high = wiry and bright. Applies from the next pluck.
- `sustain`: How long strings ring after a pluck.
- `shimmer`: Unison-course detune: the slow beating shimmer of paired strings. 0 = a single pure string.
- `pickPosition`: Where along the string the finger plucks: low = near the end (nasal, thin), high = near the middle (round, hollow).
- `physics`: Physics simulation. Off: each design settles behind a loading spinner, then its layout freezes - nothing simulates afterwards (lighter on battery and slow devices). Threads can still be plucked (and flow, pulses and the intro still play), but not tugged: a drag pans the view. Applies live - turning it off first lets a moving knot come to rest; the sliders below still shape the frozen layout.
- `simSpeed`: Simulation speed. 0 pauses, 5 is real time, 10 is 5x.
- `slack`: How loose the threads rest. 0 pulls the knot rope-taut, 1 leaves it soft and open.
- `drag`: Motion damping. Low = springy, waves ring after a pluck; high = movement dies quickly.
- `repulsion`: Breathing room between parallel threads. Higher spreads the composition apart.
- `bend`: Bending stiffness. Higher resists sharp curves - smoother, rounder thread paths.
- `stiffness`: Structural spring strength along each thread. Higher = firmer, less stretchy material.
- `smooth`: Curvature smoothing. Evens out wiggles along threads so they read as continuous curves.
- `gridAlign`: How strongly every thread is pulled back toward its lattice position, on an exponential scale (each tenth of the slider doubles the pull): 0 = free-flowing curves, 1 = a formal, grid-like composition. Applies live.
- `showDebug`: Overlay the physics debug view (particles, bindings, contacts).
- `showStats`: Show the build + performance readout (version, sim/wall time, frame cost) in the bottom-left corner. Remembered per browser.
