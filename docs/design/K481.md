## Decisions (approved 2026-10-15)

1. **A toggle, off by default,** for the turn minotaur only (the turn
   clock; the real-time chase already moves by time). Suggested name
   `minoByLength` (`mbl`), appended to `CODEC_PARAMS`
   (gameplay-links.md), with a row among the turn minotaur's rows
   (gameplay-panel.md). Not daily-exempt, like the other minotaur rows.
2. **Travel:** each move, the minotaur travels its speed factor times
   your move's thread length: half 0.5x, one 1x, two 2x, charge 2x while
   it hunts or has you in sight (1x otherwise, as today's charge). A wait
   moves it the maze's median run length times the factor. Sleep and
   pause still count turns.
3. **Mid-run:** a turn can end mid-run, and it can turn back on a run. A
   position is a run plus an offset, as the real-time chase's `Spot`.
4. **The route** is picked at the move's start, from where you stand
   (today's `commit`), and long enough for the longest choice's travel.
   Its decisions keep today's units (sight, roam, give-up and territory
   in runs; the RNG counter on `turns`), so only the travel changes.
   Where the travel outruns today's destination, the route extends past
   it or stops there; decide which with the sim and record why.
5. **Contact** is checked continuously through the move: you at t x len
   along your run, it at t x travel along its route, caught when their
   thread distance falls to a catch distance (start from the real-time
   chase's `catchDist`, 1 lattice unit; tune it on the sim). The start
   still hides you, it never steps onto the start or the exit, and an
   exit END run still wins before contact.
6. **Difficulty:** tune on the turn sim so the mode's catch rates match
   today's turn mode, by stage 3a's criterion (the nine cells: daily,
   treelike and dead-end sets x the explorer, 80% and fog-4 players).
7. **The telegraph:** one red route with a tick per choice along it,
   where the minotaur would stop if you took that choice. Hovering or
   pressing a choice lights its tick and moves the ring there; with
   nothing hovered (and always on the phone) the route and its ticks show
   alone. A wait has its own tick, lit when you hover your own junction.
   For a multi-run tap (K480), the tick of its first run. Fallback, if
   the ticks read badly: tint the choices that would get you caught.
8. **K480's stop rule keeps working:** a multi-run tap or a hold stops
   before its next run when the minotaur has just started hunting you, or
   when its telegraph touches the rest of the route.
9. **Stats:** close calls ("a move that ends one run from it") and seen
   need a reading in this mode; pick one and record it.
