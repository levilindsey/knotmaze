## Question
When you tap the "Search settings" box in the panel on your phone and type, does the screen behave: the maze behind stays still, the keyboard leaves the box visible, and Enter finishes the search?

## Context
The settings panel got a search box (K793, done in 9b2a4ea7). Its page check passed 30 of 30 on a desktop and 33 of 33 in an emulated phone window (411x753): names, typos, tags, option labels, clearing, and the results scrolling. An emulator has no soft keyboard, so one risk is untested: the keyboard re-fitting the screen. That risk is inferred, not seen.

## How to check
1. Open the link on your phone, then open the settings panel (it opens as a sheet).
2. Tap the "Search settings" box and type a word such as "touch". The maze behind the sheet must not jump or zoom.
3. With the keyboard up, it must not cover the box, and the results must scroll under the sticky search bar.
4. Press the keyboard's Enter (Go or Search): the keyboard should close and the best row should be outlined briefly.
5. Say which step, if any, went wrong.

## After you answer
