## Question

On the phone, a tap sends a glint ripple out along the thread. It used to be too small to see past a
fingertip, so it got a size floor: on small screens its front grows until it ends at least this many
CSS px from the tap. Which size reads right?

## Context

- **56 (master now):** the front ends about 10 mm from the tap on the Pixel. It clears a fingertip
  (5 mm) at about 0.19 s. 56 matches the desktop ripple's apparent size at a normal viewing distance.
- **68:** the front ends about 12 mm from the tap: the same ripple, about a fifth larger.
- **Cost doesn't decide it.** The phone ran both four times with no dropped frames: every window
  after a tap ran at 58-60 fps, with nothing over 40 ms, at both sizes.
- **Desktop is unaffected.** The floor only acts on small screens, so default desktop views don't change.
- **What I saw in the picture:** both rings read clearly. 68's glint reaches a little further along
  the threads at each moment, and neither looks crowded.

## Options

- **56 (rec, keep as is):** nothing changes.
- **68:** a little larger ring on the phone.
- **Other:** a size between them or beyond them, as a number.
