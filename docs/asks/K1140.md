## Question
At your request, memory modes were brainstormed on 2026-11-03: the game shows you the way, hides it, and you walk it from memory. Six came out of it. Which do you want, and which first?

## Context
- Nothing is built for any of them. Today's hint lights only the next good run, until your next move.
- Glimpse would show the route as one light running from you along the way out, through several junctions, then fading; paths off the route stay dark. The pulse fix's plan (K1139) builds that light as its last stage. What the camera does when the route leaves the screen is still to be decided.
- The myth and the lantern come from the earlier list of gameplay ideas (K80); the other modes exist only as the brainstorm's one-line notes, given below.

## Options
- **Glimpse (rec, first)**: the base mode. The route is shown once, then hidden, and you walk it; a wrong move is one that does not bring you closer to the exit.
- **Shuffle (rec, as Glimpse's hard setting)**: after the glimpse, a shake re-settles the knot, so the paths no longer look as they did. If the shake moves them too little, the view rotates or mirrors instead.
- **The myth, with a Retrace encore**: reach the lair at the centre, take the sword or slay the minotaur, then find the way out, with Ariadne's thread as the way back; the twist is the minotaur cutting the thread. What the Retrace encore does is not written anywhere yet.
- **Echo**: "Simon by pitch" (all the record says): the memory game Simon, played with the threads' notes. It needs the route light from the pulse fix.
- **Beacons**: darkness and a lantern (the fog of war closes again after some moves, and lantern oil is a resource), plus beacons along the route.
- **Memory hints**: the hint shows the next 3-4 runs, then fades.

## After you answer
- Your picks set the order: the first gets planned, and the rest stay ideas until their turn.
- Echo or Glimpse first also waits on the pulse fix's route light (K1139).
