# K1040 · Could you unlock the phone on its charger and leave it on the home screen, untouched, for about 25 minutes, so the junction drawing's phone timing can run?

## Question
The new way of drawing junctions needs one timing run on your phone, and every try so far found its screen off and the phone locked. Could you unlock it, leave it on the home screen with the screen on, and not touch it for about 25 minutes?

## Context
- The junction redesign draws each junction with a pass on the graphics chip that works out, pixel by pixel, which thread is on top (the label pass). Its budget on your phone is 2 ms a frame. At the last phone run (the stage before) it took 4.1-9.4 ms (median 5.8), and an empty pass, drawing nothing, already 2.0-3.6 ms.
- The latest stage (2026-11-03) trimmed the pass's fixed cost. On the desktop the trimmed pass, drawing nothing, now costs 0.2-0.3 ms more than the least any such pass can cost. Whether that holds on the phone is the open check.
- Its run found the screen off and the phone locked at every try: eight between 12:27 and 13:23, then 23 more about 77 s apart until 13:52. It never wakes or unlocks the phone, and it starts only from the home screen after 2 minutes without a touch.
- The run measures three things on the phone: whether the trimmed pass is within 0.3 ms of that least cost; the least cost itself (if it is over 1 ms on most scenes, less than 1 ms is left for drawing, and today's drawing, 1.2-4.4 ms, misses on every scene); and the pass's cost per scene against the 2 ms.
- What waits on those numbers: the next stage's plan, and which saving comes first: sending fewer bytes to the graphics chip each frame (212,992-901,120 bytes now; one lever saves a quarter of them), packing its tables into one texture, or restoring the graphics state without the 17 queries the pass makes now (the desktop's whole 0.2-0.3 ms).

## How to check
1. Put the phone on its charger, as for the earlier phone sittings (it then kept its screen on), unlock it and go to the home screen, with no app open in front.
2. Tell Claude it is ready.
3. Leave the phone alone, buttons included, for about 25 minutes: the run waits 2 minutes without a touch, then takes about 15 minutes, then about 6 more.

## After you answer
- The numbers go into the next stage's brief and settle which saving comes first; then you can use the phone again.
- A touch or a button press during the run stops it (a guard watches for one), and the run has to start over.
