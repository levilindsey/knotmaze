## Question

setting that turns spiders off and swaps its icon. What is left is how much
to build first: the simple spider that only hunts (S), or one that also
spins new webs (M)?

## Context
- The design (R.3.2): the spider waits at a junction near the exit and
  cannot see; it feels. Each move you make rings the runs you walk, each
  pluck rings its run, and the shake fades along the threads. It darts to
  the strongest one in range, then waits again. Standing still is silent,
  so thinking stays free in real time.
- S runs on the minotaur's chase engine, with its own icon and the "feel"
  rule above. No new threads.
- M adds spinning: new web strands between nearby junctions (a drawn strand
  plus a walkable run). That touches the maze graph and the drawing, so it
  is a stage of its own.
- The spider glyph (a two-dot body with eight legs) exists and already marks
  waiting followers; as an enemy it has no marker mesh yet (K226).
- It was queued behind the real-time minotaur work (K324 stage 3), which is
  built.

## Options
- **S (rec)**: one stage builds the enemy, its icon, the no-spiders setting
  and the glyph's mesh. M stays possible as a later stage.
- **M**: the same plus spinning webs; two stages, and the maze graph changes
  while the enemy is still unproven.
