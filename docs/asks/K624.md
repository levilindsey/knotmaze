## Question
In a burn race the fire waits at the entrance as a lit fuse: a glow on both sides of its cut and no dark band. Every burning front after it has the dark band ahead of it, and under Gone, the default look, its whole glow lies ahead of the cut. Should the fuse be drawn like those fronts, so the fire looks the same from its first frame?

## Context
- Your K409 calls (2026-10-20) fixed the fire's widths in world units, put the whole glow on the unburnt side under Gone, and kept the dark band under both looks. They cover burning fronts. The fuse was not in them, so it kept the earlier build's shape under both looks: a glow on both sides and no band.
- The old reasoning for that shape, in the code: nothing behind the fuse has burnt, so it keeps both sides.
- The fuse shows while the fire's clock is within its delay; at delay 0, the turn clock's default, that is until your first move.
- Under Char a front is the band plus a glow on both sides, so there the fuse differs from a front only by the missing band. Under Gone it also differs in the glow, which sits on both sides of the cut.
- The shape is chosen in `frontShape` (`src/render/explore_glsl.ts`), where the fuse is the one front that ignores the look. K623 is a separate open ask about the glow hiding the band under Gone; the fuse would follow whatever Gone ends up as.

## Options
- **Yes, same shape as the fronts (rec)**: under Gone a dark band ahead of the fuse and the whole glow ahead of the cut; under Char the Char front. One fire look from the start.
- **No, keep the fuse as it is**: glow on both sides and no band, under both looks.

## How to check
1. Open the first link and look at the entrance before you move: the lit fuse next to the start disc.
2. Make one move, then look at the fire front that follows you: a dark band ahead of it, and under Gone the glow only on the unburnt side.
3. Open the second link for the Char look and compare the fuse with a front the same way.

## After you answer
- Yes: the fuse takes the look's front shape, in the same stage as the K623 call; K624 closes.
- No: K624 closes and the fuse stays as it is.
