## Question
When the minotaur shows where it will walk next, is the red glow along the edges of each run enough, or should small red arrows also flow along the run in the direction it is heading?

## Context
- Both clocks already draw the warning: the runs it will take light up red along both edges (faint while it roams, bright while it hunts, the later runs dimmer), with a ring where the route ends. Real time got it on 2026-10-20 and it is on by default (`mtg=0` turns it off).
- The arrows come from the original minotaur design (K80 section 2.7): red chevrons that flow along the run in its direction, like the pulse crests but slower and bold enough for a phone. They were held until the real-time warning existed and you had seen it on the phone.
- The glow lights a run but does not point. The moving end of the route (a tongue in front of the minotaur that shortens as it walks) and the ring at the far end are what show the direction today.
- Nothing for the arrows is built. They would be a fourth look beside the three that exist (edges, line, both), which K626 also asks about; answer the two together.

## Options
- **Drop the arrows (rec)**: the glow, the line and the ring stay as they are; K80's last look piece closes.
- **Build the arrows for both clocks**: a stage adds a flowing red band to the route in both turn and real time, as a new look value (K80 and K324 both planned them for both clocks).

## How to check
1. Open the picture: compare the roam and hunt rows. Can you tell which way it will go from the lit runs and the ring alone?
2. Or open the real-time link on your phone and watch the minotaur leave its lair; keep out of its sight to see it roam, let it spot you to see it hunt.
3. Open the turn link, make a move, and look at the route it shows for its next move.
