## Question

On K1236 you asked:
- b, v and w should exit a little more downward;
- f's word-end tail was too long, too upward and too bumpy;
- p's word-end tail was too long and too upward;
- o, q and a were fine.

J3n does each of these, and the variant goes a step further. Which reads right?

## Context

- **b, v and w at a word's end:** the stroke leaves the point lower. J3M left it at 22.5° below
  level; J3n leaves it at 34°, the variant at 40°. It still dips and swoops up, with no hook,
  well short of v6.8's curl.
- **b, v and w into a letter that starts high:** the join dips a little deeper. Into a, c, d, g, o
  and q, J3M's check mark becomes a gentle hammock. Into m, n, v and x, the small wave stays,
  slightly deeper.
- **b, v and w into a letter that starts low (e, h, k, r, s):** unchanged.
- **f at a word's end:** the tail is about a fifth shorter, ends at 30° instead of 40° (the variant at
  25°), and has no bump. It still runs down beside the stroke to the baseline as a spur. Its
  bottom turn is the tightest of the tails; the variant's is rounder and flatter.
- **p at a word's end:** the tail is about 30% shorter and ends at 35° instead of 55°. The variant's
  tail is a little longer and flatter, at 32°.
- **Nothing else moved.** Across the 232 test words only these joins and tails changed, with no
  spikes or hooks.

## Options

- **J3n (rec):** "a little" lower out of b, v and w, and the shorter p.
- **The variant:** lower still out of b, v and w; f's and p's tails flatter, with f's turn rounder.
- **Mix:** say which letter takes which (for example, J3n's b, v, w and p with the variant's f).
- **Change a spot:** say what (for example, keep J3M's check mark into round letters).

## How to check

Each row is one word. Compare the J3n and variant columns with J3M to their left. Orange marks a
lead-in or a word-end tail, blue or red a join, and grey is the ink at the pen's width.
