## Options
- **A**: as merged. The glow covers the band; a browner tail and the ash core show.
- **B**: the glow only down the middle of the thread (about 60% of its width), so the dark band shows along both edges.
- **C**: the glow fades out faster, so the band shows in the far half of the glow.
- **D (rec)**: the dark band is longer (1.0 ctrl, up from 0.7; a ctrl is the spacing of a thread's control points, about one thread width), so a dark rim shows past the glow.
