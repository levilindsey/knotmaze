## Question
When you take the sword in real time, the minotaur flees for a number of runs you walk, and its warning route turns pale blue while it flees. Do you want seconds instead of runs, or no warning at all while it flees?

## Context
- Both were built as recommended on 2026-10-20 (K137 stage 3a, kept in stage 3b) and stand unless you object. They are also calls 3 and 4 of K705; answer them in one place.
- The sword sits 4-8 runs off the best route. Taking it makes the minotaur turn round and flee. Contact while it flees is not a catch: the timed sword sends it back to its lair, the one-use sword slays it. `swm` (4 to 20, default 8) is how long it flees.
- The flee loses one as each run you walk ends, so standing still does not run it down.
- The pale blue (a light sky blue) colours its glow, the route strokes, the ring, the ticks and the rims.

## Options
- **1 Runs walked (rec, built)**: a flee of N runs. Alternative: seconds, a flee of N s. Real time counts everything else in seconds, but the design note says seconds do not reward walking slowly.
- **2 Pale blue warning (rec, built)**: the route stays but changes colour, so you see where the fleeing minotaur goes. Alternative: no warning route while it flees; the blue glow alone shows the flight.

## How to check
1. Open the link and walk to the sword icon (a stand-in sword, see K722). The HUD row "sword" then reads "N left".
2. Walk a run: the count falls by one as it ends. Stand still: it does not fall. The minotaur's glow and route are pale blue until the flee ends.
