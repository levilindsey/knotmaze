## Question
The real-time Guard and Stalker ship with numbers of their own, tuned on the
chase simulation. Do you keep them, or use the turn clock's numbers instead?

## Context
- K324 stage 3 (2026-10-20) added both to the real-time clock. The Guard
  roams the junctions near the exit, spots you within a distance and
  loses you beyond a larger one; hunting, it takes the best run at each
  junction with some chance. The Stalker wanders at random until it sees
  you, then follows you with that same kind of chance.
- Shipped: Guard spots you within 20 u and loses you beyond 30 u, accuracy
  0.4; Stalker accuracy 0.45. The turn clock's: Guard sight 4 runs (30 / 40 u
  converted), accuracy 0.3; Stalker accuracy 0.3.
- A first guess near those (sight 30 / 40 u, accuracy 0.7) caught the
  greedy simulated player 6.4 times a game, so the shipped numbers aim at
  the hunter's catches a game.
- Greedy player, catches a game / hunts escaped: hunter 2.61 / 5%, Guard
  2.49 / 56%, Stalker 2.72 / 48%. The fleeing simulated players escape the
  Guard and Stalker about 9 hunts in 10 (the hunter: 55% and 79%).
- At the turn clock's accuracy of 0.3 both get easier: Stalker 1.75
  catches a game, Guard about 2.0 to 2.2.

## How to check
1. Open each link and play a minute: how often does it find you, and does
   it feel like its own character?
2. Add `&mgx=0.3` (Guard) or `&msx=0.3` (Stalker) for the turn clock's accuracy.
