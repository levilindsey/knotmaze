# K144 · Which backend should hold sign-in and shared stats: Cloudflare Workers with D1, or hopnbop's Nakama?

kind: decide
asked: 2026-11-03
blocks: every account stage (sign-in, result sync, sharing, bands)
rec: Workers + D1; Google sign-in first; results synced first

## Question
You asked (2026-10-13, a stretch goal after everything else) for sign-in so your stats follow you across devices, an opt-in to share them, and percentile bands for those who share. Five calls wait on you; the first, the backend, gates every stage. Do you accept the design doc's recommendation on each?

## Context
The options doc (design only, no code) recommends building beside the site you already host: a Cloudflare Worker and a D1 database, in TypeScript, with no cost while idle. The other choice is hopnbop's Nakama server, which has accounts and storage built in but ties the game to its host, releases and incidents.
The account holds results first: the daily results the game already keeps locally, synced per account, with the local store still the offline source of truth. Sharing is off unless you turn it on, and only while signed in. A signed-out game does not change at all.
Five small stages follow: 1 sign-in and result sync; 2 the sharing switch and percentile bands; 3 polish (a second provider, download your records, the final wording); 4 submitting taps for silent replay checks; 5 cross-device resume of a saved attempt (optional).

## Options
- **Q1 Backend: Workers + D1 (rec)**: a same-origin API with a session cookie, and stage 4's replay check runs the game's own TypeScript. Nakama instead: less to write, but a launch spike lands on hopnbop's machine and the replay check is an awkward fit.
- **Q2 Providers: Google first (rec)**: and then Apple now or later, or any other at the first stage's launch?
- **Q3 Sharing wording (rec as drafted)**: "Share my daily results so my solves count toward everyone's percentile bands. Off by default; no name is ever shown." Confirm it or put it in your words.
- **Q4 Same-day bands: only once the day has closed everywhere (the doc's assumption)**: or show a day's band while the day is current, recomputed through the day.
- **Q5 Cross-device resume: decide after stages 1 to 4 (the doc's order)**: stage 5 matters only if seeing your history on another device is not enough; dropping it loses nothing the earlier stages promise.

## After you answer
Q1 and Q2 let stage 1 be briefed; stages 2 and 3 need Q3 and Q4; only stage 5 needs Q5.
