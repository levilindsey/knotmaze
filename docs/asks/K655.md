# K655 · Should par's default allowance be four times the fewest runs instead of two?

link: https://levi.dev/knotmaze/?7&am=instant&pa=strict | seed 7 with strict par at the shipped allowance

## Question
Par is the fewest runs from the entrance to the exit plus an allowance, set
by the "Par slack" slider as a multiple of the fewest. It ships at 1 (par is
twice the fewest, slider 0 to 1). Should it default to 3 (par is four times
the fewest, slider 0 to 4)?

## Context
- Par landed 2026-10-20 (K137), off by default. Strict loses the moment the
  exit can't be reached in the moves left; strokes never loses and shows the
  finish against par (+2, E, -1).
- The aim: a hurrying player finishes strict about half the time, a careful
  one nearly always. Measured with simulated players on 50 mazes (30
  dailies, 20 free designs), not on people.
- Inside 0 to 1, slack 1 comes nearest: the hurrier finishes 30%, the
  careful player 89%. The hurrier re-walks dead ends (a median 4.5 times the
  fewest), so half needs slack 3.4.
- Slack 3 gives 48% and 100%, but the slider has to reach 4 (one constant
  in `par.ts`, the link codec's clamp and the panel row).
- A typical maze's fewest is 14 runs (10 to 20): par 28 as shipped, 56 at 3.

## Options
- **3 in 0 to 4 (rec)**: a looser par that matches the aim; the hurrier
  finishes 48%, the careful player 100%.
- **1 in 0 to 1 (as built)**: a tighter par; 30% and 89%.

## How to check
1. Open the link. The HUD shows the par and the moves left; try to finish
   inside it the way you normally play.
2. Add `&par=0.5` for a tighter one. The slider stops at 1 for now, so the
   looser one can't be tried until it is built.

## After you answer
3: a small commit moves the default and widens the slider. 1: the item
closes as built.
