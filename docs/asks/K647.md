# K647 · If you press Reset path as the winning move arrives, should the result card still show?

kind: decide
asked: 2026-10-20
blocks: nothing
rec: show the card (the result already counts)
link: https://levi.dev/knotmaze/?7&am=instant&ms=0.25 | a free-play maze with the slowest travel speed, so the press is easy to time

## Question
Pressing Reset path while the dot is still travelling on the move that wins
the maze counts the win, records the result and throws the confetti, then
restarts the maze in the same instant. The result card is built and hidden
again at once, so you never see it. Should the card show?

## Context
- Found 2026-10-20 while checking every way to reach the win card (K186,
  K336). The other eight (live finish, reload during the dot's travel,
  replay, practice, free play, real time, both fire-race clocks) show the
  card and throw one burst.
- Seen in the page in four runs: the press throws the confetti, the card
  never paints, and 101 to 139 pieces are still falling over the restarted
  maze five seconds later.
- On a daily, the same press writes the official result (time, moves),
  and the badge then reads "replay, not scored".
- Cause: Reset path finishes a pending win ("a winning move whose dot is
  still on its way counts"), then restarts; the restart hides the card and
  leaves the confetti. This was already so before the confetti work.

## Options
- **Show the card (rec)**: the finished win gets its card, matching the result.
- **Leave it**: a celebration and a recorded result but no card (as now).
- **Skip the confetti on this press**: recorded, nothing celebrates.
- **Clear the confetti on the restart**: recorded silently, no leftover pieces.

## How to check
1. Open the link and tap your way to the finish. Hard by hand: press Reset
   path in the panel just as the last move's dot sets off.
2. Today: confetti falls over a fresh maze and no card appears.

## After you answer
Show: a small change to the Reset path press so the finished win keeps its
card. Leave, skip or clear: the matching one-line change, or nothing for
"leave". Either way the item closes.
