## Question
Two puzzles where a walker walks the maze by itself and you only cut runs (Lead it out with few cuts; Delay it as long as you can) were built with six rule choices, each as recommended. Does any one need the alternative?

## Context
- Built 2026-10-21 (K114 walker) and standing unless you object. Lead the walker follows its strand straight through junctions and turns by a fixed rule where the strand ends; Delay it takes the shortest route again after every cut.
- The first-time-player question from the design (can someone new read the walker's next run before tapping?) can only be answered on a phone; the picture is that test.

## Options
- **1 At a strand's end the walker keeps right (rec, built)**: it takes the first live arm clockwise from straight on, and turns back only if nothing else is live (an end always turns it back). Alternatives: keep left; the straightest arm.
- **2 Lead is a puzzle (rec, built)**: you cut while it plans, Go starts the walk and locks the cuts; a walk that ends short says why and returns to planning with the cuts kept. Alternative: cutting while it walks (an action game, not built).
- **3 The whole route shows (rec, built)**: to the exit or its first repeat, redrawn after every cut, the next run full and the rest faint. Alternative: only the next few runs (harder to read and to solve).
- **4 Lead's stars (rec, built)**: 3 at par or under, 2 at par + 1, 1 beyond; Delay has none. Alternatives: no stars in walker modes; stars for Delay from its walk against the shortest.
- **5 In Delay each cut or pass is a turn (rec, built)**: the walker takes one run after each; Undo takes back the last turn. Alternative: the walker walks on a clock and you cut at will.
- **6 Par 0 gets nothing special (rec, built)**: one daily (18) has par 0 and reads `par 0`; Go wins with no cuts. Alternatives: skip or reroll par-0 seeds for Lead; say "no cuts needed".

## How to check
1. Open the picture: in each a/b pair, could you have said which run the walker takes before it moved?
2. Open the Lead link on a phone, tap runs to cut them, press Go, and see where it keeps right.
