## Question
When the real-time minotaur hunts you it now runs at 0.9 of your speed (it was
0.7), and five of its new tuning labels are shorter than the brief's. Do you
keep both?

## Context
- K324 stage 1 (2026-10-20) made the real-time minotaur stop at every
  junction (0.2-0.6 s roaming, half that hunting) and added seven tuning
  sliders. The stops made it easier to escape, so its hunt speed was raised
  to keep today's catch rates, as you had taken in the recs.
- The brief said to leave the speed alone if nothing from 0.7 to 0.9 restored
  the rates. 0.9 does for the greedy player (catches 2.61 against 2.50 before).
  The fleers escape 55% against 50% before, and the queued fleer 79% against
  75%. Nothing up to 1.0 restores the queued fleer.
- Five labels were over the panel's 23-character limit, so they shipped
  shorter: "Junction stop, min (s)", "Junction stop, max (s)", "Stop while
  hunting (x)", "Dead-end reach (x roam)" and "Nearest roam (x roam)".

## How to check
1. Open the link and let the hunter find you; it closes in at 0.9 of your
   speed.
2. Add `&mhp=0.7` to feel the old speed.
3. The five labels are in the panel's Minotaur tuning rows.
