## Question
With fog of war on, walking back over a path you already took used to clear the fog around it a little more each time, so its edges grew crisper. Now the fog stays as your first walk left it: softer edges, and a slightly narrower clearing. Keep the new look?

## Context
- The fog remembers where you have been as a list of small clearings. Before, every pass over the same ground added another set, so the list grew without end, and the repeats cut deeper into the fog.
- The fix (16b1c56f, 2026-11-03) adds a clearing only where none sits nearby already, so the list stops growing: on their test walk (four runs out and back, three times) it stopped at 29 clearings, where before it reached 181.
- The difference is on paths you walk again: after the repeats, the cleared area on that walk is about 12% smaller than it used to be.
- They stopped there rather than widen the clearings, so how a walked-back path looks is yours to call.

## Options
- **Keep the new look**: nothing changes; the item closes.
- **Clear more**: the fog's drawing or its spacing changes so a walked path clears as much as the old repeated passes did. That stage's size is unknown.

## How to check
1. Open the link.
2. Walk four or five runs out, then back the same way, two or three times.
3. Watch the fog along your path: it should stay as your first walk left it.

## After you answer
- Keep: the item closes.
- Clear more: a stage changes the fog's drawing or spacing, and you get the same walk's pictures again.
