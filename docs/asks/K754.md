## Question
In the beat game the dot rides on a steady beat and plucks each run's own note. Is 100 beats a minute (0.6 s a run) the right speed, or would 80 or 132 feel better?

## Context
The design doc asked for a tempo slower than the song's 132 and to start near 100, then try it on the page and report; 100 is that starting guess and nobody has judged it by feel. One beat now covers one run, so a run takes about 0.75 s at 80, 0.6 s at 100 and 0.45 s at 132 (the panel allows 40 to 180).
The other beat calls from that doc were built as recommended and stand unless you object: the run's own note (no song underneath); the first tap starts the dot on the next beat; the fire steps once per move; the cut, walker and Undo are off; a late tap queues for the next junction; the share tag shows a note glyph and the tempo; a pause button.
The run length a beat covers (2) and the 4-beat cap matter only when the Beat timing row is set to "by run length": at 2, about a quarter of runs hit the cap.

## How to check
1. Open the 100 link with sound on. Tap one arm: the dot leaves on the next beat and keeps going, straight on unless you tap an arm of the junction ahead. Ride about 10 runs.
2. Do the same on the 80 and the 132 links.
3. Optional: add `&btm=length&btl=2`, then `&btl=3`, to the 100 link to feel the by-length timing.
4. Say which tempo you want as the default.

## After you answer
Your pick becomes the default tempo, a one-number change.
