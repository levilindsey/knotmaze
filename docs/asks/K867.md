## Question

## Context

- An agent rewrote the fallback as an in-tune voice (two slightly detuned oscillators shaped like a plucked string). Measured offline, its pitch is within 1 cent of the target at 110 to 880 Hz. The fix trades timbre for pitch: it sounds less like the harp, and that part only you can judge.
- It is one parked commit (69e079eb, on the desktop-only branch `park/muse-2026-10-26`: `knot_synth.ts` and its test). On 2026-11-03 it still applied cleanly to master.

## Options
- **Keep (rec)**: cherry-pick 69e079eb onto master. Plain-HTTP test links then play in tune, with a timbre that is not the harp's.
- **Drop**: leave the old fallback as it is. Plain-HTTP links stay out of tune; the live site is unchanged.

## How to check

3. Pluck a few threads and drag. The pitch should sound right. Compare the timbre with the harp at https://levi.dev/knotmaze/?7.
