## Question
On the turn clock the minotaur shows the run it will take next, and the spot where it will stand. It can light both edges of that run (the default), draw a line over it, or do both, and today it is all red. Do you want to keep these three looks in red, make the color white like your hover highlight, or add a fourth look?

## Context
- You asked on 2026-10-20 for a look setting whose new default lights the run's edges the way the hover highlight does, with the old line kept as an option. Built that way (K525): `rims` (default), `line`, `both`.
- The ledger's reasoning for red: it cannot pass for your hover, but it reads worse on a section that is itself red. White would match the hover and could be mistaken for it.
- The ledger says K525's sheet shows white too. It does not: the sheet is red only. The K525 notes list white edges, a run on a red section, the gold trail under the route, a hovered run on the route (red first, the hover's white over it) and High contrast as not yet shot.
- A later change (K813) made the color and the width settings, so you can try white now without a build. The panel has "Telegraph look", "Telegraph color" and "Telegraph width" rows, and the links take `tgc` (six hex digits, no `#`; default `ff2418`) and `tgw`.
- On the emulated phone, the edges were faint while the minotaur roams and short while it hunts, because the junction gaps and fades eat short runs; the line read more clearly. Tuning is not done.
- No fourth look is designed.

## Options
- **Keep the three, in red (rec)**: as built; red stays apart from your hover.
- **White like the hover**: the default color becomes white, a one-value default change. It should read better on a red section (unchecked) but can look like the hover.
- **A fourth look**: say what you picture; it becomes its own item.

## How to check
1. Open the first link and make a move. The run the minotaur will take next lights red along both edges, with a ring where it will stand; fainter while it roams, stronger once it hunts you.
2. Open the second link and make the same kind of moves. Is the line easier to read than the edges? Add `&mtl=both` to either link for both together.
3. Open the third link for white edges. On a desktop, hold the pointer over a run to see the hover highlight: can you tell the two apart?
4. If a red section lies on the route, look at how the red edges read on it. On a phone, check that the route reads at fit zoom.

## After you answer
- Keep the three in red: K626 closes; the unshot cases stay on K525's list.
- White: the default color changes to white and K626 closes.
- A fourth look, or a different width: say what you want; it becomes its own item.
