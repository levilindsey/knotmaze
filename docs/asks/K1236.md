## Question

You asked (K1225) for b, v and w to keep a cusp but turn out of it about east-south-east, then
swoop up, at a word's end and into letters that enter high; into letters that enter low, to keep
the sharp downward cusp and start the swoop later. You asked for o's tail to follow the o further
round before it leaves, and for f's tail to reach the baseline and leave along the stroke into its
cusp, so the two fuse into a spur. J3m does each of these. Do they read right, and do the spots
below read right?

## Context

- Letters that enter low (they get the sharp cusp): e, h, k, r, s. Letters that enter high (they
  get the east-south-east exit): all the others. Only b, v and w have this cusp. o's top exit is a
  different shape and is unchanged.
- b, v and w into a high letter: they leave the point 22.5° below level, then swoop up into the
  next letter. They dip much less than v6.8's did before climbing into a tall letter.
- b, v and w into a low letter: v6.8's sharp cusp, then the stroke runs a little way back along the
  letter before it swoops out. No V into r and no small spikes in be or we (v6.9's flaws).
- b, v and w at a word's end: they leave the point east-south-east and swoop up to 30°.
- o at a word's end: the tail follows the o a little further down its curve, then rises at 25°.
- f at a word's end: the tail runs back down beside the stroke into the cusp, reaches the
  baseline, then turns up at 40°.
- Nothing else moved. Across the 232 test words, only the joins and tails out of b, v, w, o and f
  changed.
- Spots to look at. Each comes from the letter's rule, not from data for one pair:
  - into a, c, d, g, o and q (ba, bo, bq, wa, vq): a straight run out of the point, then a turn up
    into the letter, like a check mark;
  - into m, n, v and x (bm, wn, vv, wx): a shallow dip, a slight wave;
  - into p (bp, vp, wp): a flat dip, then a steep climb;
  - o's word-end tail has a small notch where it leaves the o;
  - f's spur was viewed only at the sheets' pen width, where its two sides fuse.

## Options

- **Keep J3m (rec)**
- **Change the exit into high letters**: say how (for example, a rounder turn into a, c, d, g,
  o and q, or a different angle out of the point).
- **Change o's tail**: say how (for example, no notch, or leave sooner).
- **Keep v6.8 or J3l at a spot**: name it.

## How to check

Each row is one word or one pair. Compare the right column (J3m) with approved v6.8 on the left.
Orange marks a lead-in or a word-end tail, and blue or red a join. Grey is the ink at the pen's
width.
