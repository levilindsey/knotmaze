# K630 · Should a drag that starts inside a junction be silent on release even when the point you grabbed would pluck?

kind: veto
asked: 2026-10-20
blocks: nothing
rec: no; keep it as built (what sounds always draws, at the grabbed point)

## Question
You said players should not be able to pluck a junction core. Pressing on a
thread right at a junction and dragging can grab a point just outside the core.
On release, that grabbed point plucks (a note and the ripple). Do you want that
release silenced too, because you pressed in the core?

## Context
- Built 2026-10-20 (K244): auto plucks pick only sites that draw, and a drag
  released with its grabbed point inside a core plucks nothing. Taps in a core
  were already silent.
- The release is judged at the grabbed point, not at where your finger
  pressed. In the run, 8 of the 12 presses at a core grabbed a point outside
  it, so those released with a note and a ripple on a drawn neighbour.
- Judging at the press point instead would make those releases silent while
  the thread still moved and a pluck could have drawn.
- Pixel check (K631): auto plucks with nothing drawn fell from 8 of 186 to 0
  of 372, and releases on drawn sites sounded, 47 of 47.

## After you answer
Keep (no answer needed): the item closes and the rule stays "what sounds
always draws". Silence them: a one-line change gates the release on the press
point as well; say so.
