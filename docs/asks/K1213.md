## Question

At the end of a word, the approved cursive (v6.8) runs its s and its p on in a flat tongue along the
baseline. The font stops the s on its curl and closes the p's bowl into its stem. v6.8's i and j dots
are slanted dashes, where the font has an oval dot. Should these spots follow the font, or stay as
v6.8 draws them?

## Context

- You kept v6.8's joins and letters. This stage fixed the trace kit's checks and drew a candidate
  for each spot where v6.8 still differs from the font.
- Each candidate changes only its own spot: across 232 test words, no other piece moved.
- In the picture, grey is the ink at the pen's width and the thin black or coloured line is the
  pen's path.
- **s** (14 word-final s): v6.8 runs about 77 font units past the font's end. S2 stops at the
  font's end; S1 stops about 3 units further round the curl.
- **p** (13 word-final p): v6.8 runs on about 82 units past the stem. P1 closes the bowl into the
  stem and stops.
- **Dots** (41 dots in 38 words): v6.8's dash pokes out of the font's oval at both ends. D2 fills
  the font's oval; D1 is a smaller round dot inside it.
- **q** (6 word-final q): at 3x the tails look the same. At 5x v6.8 has a tiny step where the tail
  starts, and Q1 carries straight on.

## Options

- **All four (rec)**: S2, P1, D2 and Q1. The next cursive stage puts them in the template.
- **s: S1 instead of S2**: the s stops a little further round its curl.
- **Dots: D1 instead of D2**: a small round dot instead of the font's oval.
- **Keep v6.8 at a spot**: name it (s, p, dots or q), and it stays as you approved it.

2. Compare each candidate's end or dot with the font's.
