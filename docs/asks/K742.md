## Question
Ice, the color rule and the later rule mechanisms (turnstiles, hidden runs, one-way runs, keys) do not work together with the spool (the limited length of thread you can unspool): turning the spool on turns them all off. Is that fine, or should they work with it, starting with a slide paying its whole length to the spool?

## Context
- Built 2026-10-21 (K138 stage 1, `docs/design/K138_s1.md` Handoff). The brief recommended that a slide pay its whole length to the spool and be refused whole past the limit; the stage instead kept the spool among the things that switch the rules off (fragile runs, collapse, the cut, the fire). The later mechanisms share that switch (`setRules` in `fabric_controller.ts`).
- Hints and the compass follow the best legal route under a rule, as recommended.
- A tap on a refused run goes through the existing tap-to-route handling and comes back refused; the planned check of refused runs within reach was not built. A route planned further out ignores the rule, so it walks until its first refused run and stops with the toast.
- Cycle's glyphs on the head's runs (call 4, recommended yes) were deferred.
- The rule colors have had no grayscale or colorblind review: alternate is lightness 0.8 and 0.3, cycle 0.8, 0.5 and 0.22, picked so lightness alone orders them.

## Options
- **Rules off while the spool is on (rec, built)**: nothing to build; the two features stay separate.
- **Rules work with the spool**: the brief's recommendation for ice: a slide pays its whole length to the spool and is refused when longer than what is left. A stage in the spool and rules code; the other mechanisms would each need a decision.

## How to check
1. Open the first link, then the second (the spool on): the frost and the two shades disappear.
2. In the pictures, check that the shades and the three colors tell apart for you; a phone's grayscale or colorblind filter does that.

## After you answer
- Keep: the item closes (the glyphs and the color review stay as separate follow-ups), this card is deleted.
- Rules work with the spool: a stage changes the spool and the rules to work together.
