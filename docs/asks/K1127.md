## Question

On a narrow screen that line now wraps only between whole phrases. Does it read well?

- On a 360x740 screen with stats on, the rows were "0 / 2 found ·", "fewest 14, 13 with", "them". Now they are "0 / 2 found", "· fewest 14,", "13 with them".
- The words are the same; only where the line may break changed (2026-11-03, a0546b30). At the three sizes they checked (411x753, 360x740, 320x640), stats on and off, no row is a single word and none ends with the dot.
- One size effect: on a 320x640 screen with stats off the line now takes a third row, so the panel grows from 64.6 to 84.8 px tall; nothing spills past its edge.
- They held back evening out the rows' lengths, since it was not needed.
- The panel spilling past its edge on a 320x640 screen with stats on, in some states, is a separate question (K1095), unchanged here.

## Options
