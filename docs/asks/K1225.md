## Question

You asked for a tail on every word-end letter, following the curve it passes and swooping up
(K1213). In J3l, s hugs the bottom of its bowl, then peels off and swoops up, and p does the same
off the bottom of its bowl. In J3lc, s and p run a little under the bowl before they rise. b, v
and w now end with a short flick off their top stroke, o with a short tail off its top, and f with
a swoop under its crossing. Which s and p do you want, and do the new tails read right?

## Context

- The candidates are built on v6.8. Only the word-end forms differ, plus the dots (D2, which you
  picked) and q's tail (Q1, with its slight S smoothed). Across 232 test words, nothing else
  moved.
- s: v6.8 doubled back along its curl and ran on flat. J3l's tail is about 85 font units long
  and is rising at 58° where it ends. p: J3l's ends rising at 55°.
- The other 18 letters keep v6.8's endings, which already rise.
- In the pictures, orange marks a word-end tail. Grey is the ink at the pen's width.

## Options

- **J3l for s and p, and keep the new tails (rec)**
- **J3lc for s and p**: the tail runs under the bowl before it rises.
- **Change a tail**: say which, and how (longer, higher, flatter).
- **Keep v6.8 at a spot**: name it.
