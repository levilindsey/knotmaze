## Question
In the new letter shapes, a letter such as the "a" has a short tapered root
where a stroke branches off. The root now wears the colours of the strokes on
either side of it. Do you also want it to copy their effects (the reveal wipe,
the rainbow, the burn), or is matching the colours enough?

## Context
- You approved the colour blend on 2026-10-20 ("K580 rec sgtm"), and it
  landed that day (K620): each side of the root takes its stroke's colours
  and both fade into the spur's colour where they meet. Before, the root was
  a flat wedge with a hard edge across both strokes.
- The effects run once over the blend, from the root's own thread, so
  they are not matched to each neighbouring stroke.
- An earlier build (commit 3fa7aab) did copy each stroke's effects per side.
  It made the shaders compile slowly: in the test browser the reveal and burn
  pages took 45 to 54 s to load, against 27 to 33 s for the colour blend, and
  two loads ran out of the shader warm-up's time limit. So it was dropped.
- Other looks on the root are separate items: the scalloped seam between the
  two sides, the light band at the join, the pink tab (K691, K692).

## How to check
1. Open the link and look at the "a": the root should grade from blue on the
   stem's side and pink on the bowl's into the spur, with no flat wedge.
2. Watch the letters appear (reload): does the root ever look out of step
   with the stroke beside it?

## After you answer
Keep (no answer needed): the item closes. If the effects must match, it
becomes a stage of its own, which first needs a cheaper way to read them.
