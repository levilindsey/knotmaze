## Question
You chose healing cuts for monster games: a cut closes again after a few moves, so you can't strand yourself. Five details wait on you, each with the design doc's recommendation. Do you accept them, or change any?

## Context
Today the scissors are classic-game only and each cut is permanent for the rest of the solve. Allowing cuts with the minotaur or the fire changes the verb: a cut run is closed to the monster too, and cutting the run the minotaur has committed to, while it is on it, is the trapdoor.
The doc (182cfc8d) builds it in stages: the controller's heal first, then the turn clock, then real time, then the fire, then saved attempts, with the drawing and the panel in each stage's tail. A balance simulation tunes the numbers before anything ships.

## Options
- **1 Heal count: 3 of your moves (rec, to be measured)**: fewer and a cut is a blink; more and it is a wall you rebuild. It counts your accepted moves (the same count under real time), not seconds, beats or fire steps. The alternative is letting standing still heal a cut too, with a timer.
- **2 Charges: at most 3 cuts closed at once, handed back as they heal (rec)**: or a stock for the whole hunt, spent for good. The classic scissors stay unlimited (rec).
- **3 Trapdoor: stun the minotaur where it stands for one turn (rec)**: or send it back to its lair at the exit. If it stuns: one turn, or until the cut heals.
- **4 Par beside cuts: cuts stay off while strict par is on (rec)**: or both play together, judging the loss against the maze as it stands at each move.
- **5 Fire: build a firebreak that only delays the front, and measure it (rec)**: or wait for Firebreak as its own mode. With the fire there is no trapdoor, and a burnt run cannot unburn.
