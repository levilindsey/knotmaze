## Question
The maze generator now fuses away most short bridges between two threads (the H shapes), so a solve has fewer awkward extra short steps. Do you want a smaller share fused, or a different choice of which ones, instead?

## Context
- An H is a short bridge (1.2 to 2.0 units; a cell is 2) between two neighbouring threads. Before, a maze had about 93, and about 5 of the 19 taps in a solve crossed one.
- Fusing cuts one arm of the H back to its first crossing, which becomes a 3-way junction. The numbers of junctions, loops and decisions stay the same.
- Call 1, built as recommended: the share of H's fused is 1: every H is tried and 69% of the bridges end up cut. It is the largest share that keeps your bar on dailies 1-80: a greedy walker who heads for the far end takes 2.65x the optimal route in the median, and 12 of 80 dailies (15.0%, exactly the bar) let that walker finish within 1.5x. Bridge taps per solve fell from 4.76 to 1.31.
- Call 2, built as recommended: a share below 1 would fuse an even stride through the H's in strand order. The alternatives (smoothest corners first, nearest the exit first) matter only below 1.
- On dailies 81-240 the bar fails at every share, even 0, so holding it is the job of the greedy-trap search (stage 4), which is off. With it off, dailies 1-240 sit at 20.0% within 1.5x (bar 15%).

## After you answer
Keep: nothing changes. Smaller share: the default `hcs` (share, 0 to 1) is edited in `PARAM_DEFAULTS` and the pins that follow it are re-frozen. Another order: it is built, then measured on dailies 1-80.
