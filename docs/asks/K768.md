## Question
Sleepers (extra monsters that wake when you step on their junction) and followers (ones that trail you) were built with five calls. Is the follower too harsh, or do all five stand?

## Context
Built as the design doc recommended; page-checked on a desktop window and a phone-sized window. Each call with the alternatives the doc named:
1. A catch resets everything (monsters go home asleep) but keeps the woken count. Alternatives: only the catcher goes home; the extras stay put; woken sleepers stay awake.
2. No warning before the extra monsters move. Alternative: a dim line per awake extra.
3. A follower waits 1-2 moves after you leave its junction, and at each junction has a 0.25 chance to step straight toward you. This is the harshest piece: in the simulation one follower takes a greedy player from 2.22 catches a game to 9.40, and 15% of games stay unfinished at 600 moves. Alternatives: a pure trail with no shortcut; a lower chance; a chance that grows with your lead; half pace; giving up after N moves or out of sight.
4. A follower looks like a sleeper while asleep, shows amber while waiting, red while awake. Alternative: hidden until it appears.
5. The share tag shows "N woken" whenever a sleeper plays, 0 included; nothing for followers. Alternatives: count the followers' appearances; hide the tag at 0.

## After you answer
Silence leaves all five as built. To change one, name its number and the alternative. For call 3 you can already try other values: the panel has rows for the follower's delay and shortcut chance (links `&fdm=`, `&fdx=`, `&fsc=`).
