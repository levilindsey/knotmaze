# K251 · When the panel opens, should an edge disc slide across the screen or fade out and back in?

## Question
When the panel opens, an edge indicator that sat under it moves out of the way in a 0.25 s slide, and on the way it passes over the open panel. Is that fine, or should the disc fade out where it was and fade back in at its new spot?

## Context
- The edge indicators are the discs on the frame's edge that point at the finish, at your head, or at the minotaur when it is off screen.
- The panel itself snaps open and shut. The view eases in 0.25 s, and a displaced disc slides along the frame's edge, the shorter way round.
- The contact sheet shows the disc crossing over the open panel during that slide. It was seen on the phone. The phone's panel is a full-screen sheet now (K468), so the question applies to the desktop and tablet side panel only.
- With Reduced motion on, the view and the slide both jump (no animation), so a fade would only matter with motion on.
- Measured: the displaced finish disc is in place within 224 ms at 1400x900; closing slides it back in about 0.2 s.

## Options
- **Keep the slide (rec)**: as built. The disc crosses the panel for a fraction of a second.
- **Fade out and back in**: no crossing over the panel. A small change to how the slide is drawn (`blendPlacement` in `src/ui/offscreen_indicators.ts`), with its tests.

## How to check
1. Open the contact sheet and find a strip where the disc is mid-slide over the panel.
2. Say whether that crossing bothers you.

## After you answer
Keep: K251 closes. Fade: a small change as above, re-tested with `test/ui/offscreen_indicators.test.ts`.
