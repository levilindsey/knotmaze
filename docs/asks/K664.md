## Question
Since 2026-10-16 every default maze, and so every daily, ends in a trap-zone
finish. It works: a simulated hurried player takes much longer to get out.
Do you want it that way in the defaults?

## Context
- Built as recommended in K74 stage 3 (commit 83d4405): the last 24 half
  lattice cells of thread before the exit carry no choice junction, so the
  threads near the finish are near misses that do not lead in, and the
  exit's tail that wanders farthest from the exit is the one kept.
- It made the minotaur chase simulation's numbers rise (K637 traced the rise
  to this commit alone and found it intended). For the greedy simulated
  player: games with 3 or more catches 23% to 33%, solve time median 58 to
  81 s and p90 181 to 310 s, mean game 90 to 136 s.
- Catches per 100 s of play did not move (1.85 to 1.84): the chase is no
  harder, the walk is longer.
- With no minotaur, 6% of the greedy simulated walks never finish within 900
  s. They come from the away flank; that is the trap working.
- The alternative is a shorter or no corridor (the Maze folder's "Exit
  corridor", default 24) or the older stub flank as the default. Every daily
  and its pins would move again.

## How to check
1. Open the first link and head for the exit: the threads beside it pass
   close without leading in.
2. Open the second link for the older finish on the same seed.

## After you answer
Keep (no answer needed): the item closes. Soften: say how far (a shorter
