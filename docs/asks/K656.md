## Question
In Snap a run breaks as soon as you have crossed it. Simulated hurrying and
exploring players strand (no unbroken way to the exit left) on 40 of 50
mazes. Do you keep the rule and label the mode hard, or add a softer answer?

## Context
- Fragile runs landed 2026-10-20 (K137): Crack (a run cracks on the first
  crossing, breaks on the second) strands none of the simulated players;
  Snap strands 40 of 50 (25 of 30 dailies, where the design expected about
  20). The careful simulated player never strands.
- A strand plays a falling run of notes and shows the Stranded banner:
  Undo and Restart, or Restart only under the minotaur chase or the fire,
  whose state Undo can't rebuild.
- K840 (2026-10-22, your call) then made Snap harsher: dead ends and the
  entrance and exit tails break too, so a dead end strands you at its tip.
  The 40 of 50 predates that.

## Options
- **Hard label (rec)**: keep the rule; describe Snap as the hard mode in
  the panel so a strand is expected. Undo covers the rest.
- **Mending runs**: a broken run grows back after a while, so a strand is
  rarely final; the rule and its timing would be designed first.
- **A way out under the chase or fire**: some escape where only Restart is
  offered now; new work.

## How to check
1. Open the first link and walk into any dead end: you are stranded at its
   tip and the banner offers Undo. The second link adds the real-time
   minotaur, where a strand offers Restart only.
2. Swap `fg=snap` for `fg=crack` to compare: a stub can be walked in and out.

## After you answer
Hard label: a small change to the panel's copy for the mode. Mending or a
way out: briefed as a stage of its own.
