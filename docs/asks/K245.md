## Question
When the real-time minotaur is not hunting, how strongly should its roaming lean toward where you are? It is set to 0.5 on a 0-to-1 slider. Is that right?

## Context
- The pull is the chance that a roam leg picks a destination nearer to you than the minotaur is now. 0 is the old blind roam. The Game folder has the slider (link key `mpl`).
- A simulated greedy player on 288 mazes, three runs each, was spotted at least once in 56% of games with no pull, 60% at 0.25, 63% at 0.5, 64% at 0.75 and 66% at 1.
- Catches per game went 1.32, 1.62, 1.68, 1.84 and 1.94 in the same order. So a stronger pull mostly adds catches, not meetings.
- Without homing (heading straight at you), meetings top out near two thirds, because a fifth of solves end inside 30 seconds. More meetings would need homing or the "closest" pick, which was tried and dropped.
