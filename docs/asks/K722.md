## Question
The sword icon is a quick stand-in: a blade pointing up, a crossguard, a grip and a pommel dot. Do you want to draw the real one?

## Context
- Like every marker icon (start, exit, head, minotaur, spider, chalk tick, key, lock), it is white strokes on a black disc and was made as a placeholder for you to replace (K83: "Levi will replace them later").
- The icons are drawn on a 24 x 24 grid (y down) the way line-icon sets are: stroked lines with round ends, stroked circles and filled dots. Every icon gets the same stroke width and a margin of one stroke inside the disc; the code scales a drawing to fit.
- The sword is the one placeholder with its own ledger item. The chalk tick (K736), the key and padlock (K789) and the bull (K175) are placeholders too.

## Options
- **Draw it**: hand over any drawing; an SVG of the strokes on a 24 x 24 grid converts most directly.
- **Keep the stand-in (rec until you want a real one)**: nothing changes, and the item closes.
