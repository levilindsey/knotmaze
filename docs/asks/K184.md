## Question
Every finished maze gets one to three stars from how far you walked against the shortest route. Three calls in that scoring were made without asking you. Is any of them wrong?

## Context
- The measure is the distance you walked divided by the shortest route, with backtracks counted both ways and, under a minotaur, the walk again from the entrance after each catch. Three stars at 1.5 times or less (under a minotaur, never caught), two at 4 times or less, one for any finish.
- The lines come from simulated players on 288 mazes and 288 dailies: a careful one (it sees the whole maze) earns three stars 93 to 94% of the time; a greedy one (K74's depth-first walker) has a median of 2.6 times.

## Options
- **1. A greedy solve earns exactly two stars (rec)**: the two-star line sits at 4 times, so 52% of simulated greedy solves earn two (53% on dailies). The aim was "about half"; if that meant two or three stars about half the time, the line moves to about 2.5 times.
- **2. A catch caps the finish at two stars (rec)**: the walk before the catch already counts in the ratio. The alternative takes one star off per catch, which charges a catch twice: a greedy player's two-star share in real time would fall from 47% to 33%.
- **3. The banner says "2.2× the shortest route" (rec)**: the opened banner reads, for example, "You walked 2.2× the shortest route: 412 against 196, backtracks counted both ways." The alternative is a percent; the HUD already shows an efficiency percent that scores only the route you ended on, a different number.

## How to check
1. Open the first link, walk to the exit (arrow keys or WASD) and tap the stars on the win banner: the three rules and your numbers show. Wander into a few side routes first to push the ratio up.
2. Open the second link, let the minotaur catch you once, then finish: the stars stop at two, and the explanation says the walk before the catch counts too.
