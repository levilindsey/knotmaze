## Question
A turnstile junction dims its closed arm. When you arrive by that closed arm, going back is your only legal move, yet the dim stays on that arm and the yarn (the path you walked) covers it. Keep it that way?

## Context
The dim and the band show the turnstile's own state, not what you can do from where you stand. This is live on master (91c8d32b). The page check passed on desktop and phone-sized windows: the dim sits on the closed arm at every position; it reads clearly at the fog camera's zoom and is a small smudge at the phone's default zoom. The doc calls this a question for you, not a bug, and names no other design. Whether the dim reads on a real phone is a separate open check (K760).

## After you answer
Silence keeps the dim as built. If you want it to follow what you can do instead, say so and that becomes its own small task.
