## Question
With the turn minotaur moving by length, its red route has a small tick per choice, marking where it would stop. On the phone a choice that would get you caught is also tinted red at your head. Do you want that tint on desktop too, or are the ticks enough there?

## Context
Built as recommended (31b2238, 2026-10-16, K481 stage 3):
- Hovering or pressing a choice lights its tick and moves the minotaur's destination ring there; with nothing hovered the route and ticks show alone.
- The phone gets bigger ticks (about 0.8 of the ring radius against 0.55) because at fit zoom they sit a few pixels across near the minotaur. That is why the tint ships on the phone only; desktop keeps ticks alone and the tint was left to your call.
- The real-phone sheet (round of 2026-10-20) shows a press lighting choice 0, with the lit tick subtle on a still.
- The mode is off by default (`mbl`, "minotaur by length"), so a default link never shows any of this.

## Options
- **Ticks alone on desktop (rec)**: nothing changes; K545 closes.
- **Tint on desktop too**: the same red tint of a would-be-caught choice at the head on every screen. A follow-up; the tint code already exists for the phone.

## How to check
1. Open the link on desktop: the minotaur's red route and its ticks show.
2. Hover a choice: its tick lights and the ring moves there. On the phone, press and hold a choice.
3. Judge: could you read a would-be-caught choice from the ticks alone?

## After you answer
Keep: K545 closes. Tint on desktop: a follow-up item is filed.
