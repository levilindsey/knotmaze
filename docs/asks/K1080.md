## Question
Zoom the maze in and out on your phone and tap threads and junction choices. Does your finger pick what you meant at every zoom?

## Context
A touch used to pick within a distance measured in the maze's own units, so the tap area grew with the zoom: at the phone's default zoom it reached 3.9 px past a thread's edge (5.7 px for a junction choice), and it doubled and redoubled under a pinch zoom.
Since K331 (bc5f0043) it is measured in screen pixels: 4 px past the thread's drawn edge, and 1.5 times that (6 px) for a junction choice, at any zoom. At the phone's default zoom that is within 2 px of the old sizes. A touch press also lights the choice's glow at once, before you lift.
It was checked by unit tests (the same pixel reach at 1x, 2x and 4x zoom) and a desktop-browser touch probe, not on a phone. The setting is "Touch pick margin" in Touch & feel (0 to 20 px, default 4).

## Options
- **Keep 4 px (rec)**: the item closes.
- **A different margin**: say which zoom missed or picked the wrong arm and which number felt right (the `tpm` link key tries one); that number becomes the default, a one-number change.

## How to check
1. Open the first link on your phone. At the default zoom, tap a few threads and junction arms. The glow should light as your finger lands.
2. Pinch to zoom in, then out, and repeat. The reach should stay one finger size: no misses on thin threads, no neighbouring arm picked.
3. If a zoom feels off, open the second link (or the setting) and say what felt right.
