# K727 · Keep 3 hearts and an 8-run flee as the hearts and sword settings?

kind: veto
asked: 2026-10-21
blocks: nothing (both numbers are the ones in use)
rec: keep both
link: https://levi.dev/knotmaze/?7&am=instant&mt=hunter&mlv=3&swd=timed | seed 7 with 3 hearts and the timed sword on (the sword makes the minotaur flee 8 runs)

## Question
Hearts and the sword are optional and off in a normal game; the hearts slider advises 3, and the sword's flee lasts 8 runs. Do you want other numbers, or a sword strong enough to kill the minotaur?

## Context
- Measured 2026-10-21 by simulated players on 288 mazes, 3 plays each, against the shipped hunter minotaur (`docs/design/K708.md`).
- Hearts, share of games that would use up 3 hearts: in real time a blind player 32.9-36.1%, one who flees 4.3-8.4% (queued taps 3.5-11.0%); on the turn clock a blind player 8.4%, one who flees 1.3%, one who reads the warning route 0.0%. Two hearts is the first setting that bites on the turn clock (16.6% blind) and ends 47-48% of blind real-time games, so 3 is a middle for both clocks.
- Sword at 8 runs: a player who walks straight to it touches the minotaur (banish or slay) in 0.5% of turn-clock games, 4.2% at 12 runs, 19.3% at 20. A blind player takes the sword in 13-23% of games and it changes their catches by 0.13 a game at most.
- Real time, with a player who goes for the sword: they take it in about every game and touch the fleeing minotaur in 4.4-8.9%, yet are caught more often than the blind player. The flee runs at the player's speed on the turn clock and at half in real time. That this is why it rarely lands is a reading of the code, not a test.
- Both settings are off by default (`mlv` 0, `swd` off). The 3 is the tooltip's advice; the slider has no jump to 3.

## Options
- **Keep both (rec)**: 3 hearts advised, flee 8 runs. The sword stays a breather (the minotaur turns round and flees for a while), not a weapon.
- **Other hearts**: 2 bites on both clocks. 5 hearts still run out in 18.6-20.9% of blind real-time games, and in 0.3-3.1% of games by players who flee.
- **A longer flee**: `swm` goes up to 20, where a straight-to-the-sword player touches the minotaur in 19.3% of turn-clock games. The count alone does not make it a weapon.
- **A sword that can slay**: change the flee rule or its pace so the minotaur does not keep ahead of you. That is a design stage with a new measurement.

## How to check
1. Open the link, walk to the sword icon and take it: the minotaur turns round and flees for 8 runs.
2. Try to catch it, then add `&swm=20` to the link and try again with the longest flee.

## After you answer
- Keep: the item closes, this card is deleted.
- Other numbers: a change to the advice or `swm`'s default; a slaying sword is a design stage of its own.
