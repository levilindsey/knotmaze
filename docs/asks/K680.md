## Question
When you finish a maze, two cannons at the sides fire paper and long curling
streamers. On a desktop screen, 2 to 3 seconds in, the streamers hang over a
fair part of the knot. Is that fine, or should there be fewer?

## Context
- Landed 2026-10-20 (you asked for them on 2026-10-14). Each cannon fires 309
  pieces on a 1400x900 window (150 on a phone); a fifth are streamers (62, or
  30 on a phone), the rest plain paper. The finish spray at the marker throws
  none.
- The desktop sheet shows the streamers fall in two clouds about a third of
  the way in from each side, and by 3 s they hang as vertical strips over the
  lower half of the maze. They are gone 5 to 6 s after the finish.
- Your Pixel 11 (2026-10-20): the confetti adds roughly 12 to 20 points of
  missed frames, and 2.5 times the pieces added only 2 to 3 more. So fewer
  streamers would not buy frames; this is purely a look call. They would turn
  into plain paper, since a cannon fires the same number of pieces.

## Options
- **Keep (rec)**: share stays 0.2; the finish stays as busy as shown.
- **Thin**: lower the "Streamers" row (for example 0.1); half the ribbons,
  a little more plain paper.
- **No streamers**: share 0; the cannons fire paper only.

## How to check
1. Open the first link, play to the finish, and watch at 2 to 3 s: can you
   still read the paths through the strips? Short of time, press F12, open
   the Console and run `__knotConfettiBurst()`: it throws one burst at once.
2. Add `&cfs=0.1` (or `&cfs=0`) for fewer streamers or none.

## After you answer
Keep: no answer needed, the item closes. Thin or none: a small commit moves
the default share (the Streamers row) and its test pin; no maze changes.
