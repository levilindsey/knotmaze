## Question
In the beat game the dot keeps moving whether you steer or not, and the fire steps once per move. Should the beat game start with a gentler fire than the normal turn-based one?

## Context
- Under the beat, every forced move (the dot going straight on because you did not tap) steps the fire. The normal fire is speed 0.6, delay 0.
- The page check of the beat build burned a player who hesitated at 0:14 ("The fire reaches the end of every way on before you"). The doc calls that a legitimate loss.
- With `fsp=0.1&fdl=40` the same check passed 12 of 12 (solved at 0:34 with the fire burning). Those two numbers are the lowest speed and the longest delay the panel allows, so they show the check can pass, not a tuned pace.
- That 0:14 was measured when each run took several beats. The default is now one beat per run, and the fire was not re-measured since.

## Options
- **Yes, the beat gets its own fire defaults (rec)**: a slower speed and some delay whenever the clock is beat; you pick the numbers by playing.
- **No, keep the normal fire**: a hesitant player burns quickly; anyone can still lower the fire speed in the panel.

## How to check
1. Open the first link, tap an arm to start the dot, then stop tapping. Note how soon the fire ends the game.
2. Open the second link and do the same.
3. Say whether the first feels fair for a player who steers a little.

## After you answer
If yes: a beat-only default for the fire speed and delay, with the numbers you liked. If no: this closes.
