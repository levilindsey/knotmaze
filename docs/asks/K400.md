## Context
The burn race has two clocks: "turn" (the fire steps when you move) and "realtime" (the fire runs on a clock, even while you stand still). Stage 2 of K114 built realtime (2026-10-15); `fck=realtime` selects it, the default is still turn. Five calls went in as recommended, none asked:
- **Dot pace.** Under realtime your dot runs at one fixed pace (12 units a second, the turn animation's mean pace: 11.80 measured over dailies 1-6); the move-speed setting is unused. Instead: each run takes length / pace, or today's animation plus a mid-run loss.
- **One fire speed and delay for both clocks.** Superseded: K440 (done 395756b, 2026-10-15) gave realtime its own pair, 0.2 and 20 (`fsr`, `fdr`).
- **The loss.** You lose when the fire reaches you, where you stand. Instead: lose once no safe arm is left.
- **Taps queue.** Under realtime a tap during a run always queues, so the dot never gets ahead of the clock.
- **Where it lives.** `fireClock` is a row of the Burn race settings, not a separate mode.

## How to check
1. Open the link and make one move: the clock starts at your first move.
2. Stand still: once the fire reaches you, the banner reads "The fire reaches you where you stand."
3. Try again and keep moving: taps during a run queue and the dot keeps its pace.
