## Question
Muted and Sound mode "none" used to leave the minotaur's sounds playing (its growl, heartbeat, footsteps and catch notes). Since the sound-off fix they are silent too, like every other sound. Is that right, or do you want a separate setting for them?

## Context
- Found by the sound-off fix (K720, 2026-10-21; its Handoff, Residual 1). These cues are plain notes played from `main.ts` that never checked Muted or none, so they sounded under both. That is from the code; no chase was probed.
- The fix makes sure no audio opens while sound is off: an open audio context keeps a phone's audio awake (battery), and on Android may take audio focus from an audiobook. The minotaur's notes were the one thing left that could open it.
- The cues, from the code: a growl when it first sees you, a heartbeat while it ends within two runs of you, a quiet footstep for each run it walks (gone at six runs away), and low notes at a catch.
- With sound off you lose these audio warnings. The warning route is drawn either way.

## Options
- **Silent with sound off (rec, built)**: one switch for all sound. Nothing to build.
- **Their own setting**: a switch for the minotaur's cues that sounds even with the rest of sound off. A panel row and a link key, and the cues would open audio themselves when they play, which the fix avoided.

## How to check
1. Open the first link, tap the maze once so audio opens, and walk toward the minotaur: listen for the growl when it starts hunting you, then footsteps and a heartbeat as it closes in.
2. Open the second link and do the same: nothing sounds.
