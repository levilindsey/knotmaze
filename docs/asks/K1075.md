## Question
With fragile runs set to snap, a run breaks behind you with a plucked note, and when every way to the exit has broken you hear a falling run of notes. Do those two sounds sound right: pitch, loudness and timing?

## Context
Both sounds came with the fragile runs (K137). They are unchecked and need an ear (left by K658).
- The snap pluck: the broken run's own note, an octave up, at strength 0.85, when the dot arrives.
- The strand glissando: once each time you become stranded, 6 plucks 0.12 s apart, each 2 semitones lower and fading, from the pitch at the dot's head, starting 0.12 s after the snap's pluck.
Both play only with sound on (not muted, a sound mode chosen, volume above 0; the defaults qualify). The runs at the start, at the exit and at dead ends never break.

## How to check
1. Open the link with sound on.
2. Tap an arm and let the dot walk a run past the first junction. When it arrives, listen for one pluck an octave above that run's note.
3. Keep walking, not looking for the best route. Snap strands most walks (397 of 400 random ones), so the "Stranded" banner should appear within a few runs; as it does, listen for the falling six notes.
4. Say whether the pitch, loudness, spacing and overlap with the pluck are right, or what is off.

## After you answer
Right: the item closes. Off: the pitch step, spacing and strengths are named constants in src/app/rule_loss.ts, each a one-line change.
