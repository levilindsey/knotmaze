# K707 · How should "Keep it in" be made a fair fight?

kind: decide
asked: 2026-10-21
blocks: building "Keep it in", the third walker puzzle
rec: the walker takes two runs a turn and the no-cut zone around the exit is 5 or 6 runs
link: https://levi.dev/knotmaze/?7&am=instant&ct=delay | Delay it, the built walker puzzle with the same loop (you cut or pass, the walker takes a run); Keep it in would use it with a different goal

## Question
In "Keep it in" you cut one run a turn to shut the walker away from the exit before it gets there. A simulated cutter wins nearly every time, so the puzzle would be trivial. What should tip the balance toward the walker: a bigger no-cut zone around the exit, a faster walker, or both?

## Context
- The design guessed (from counts, not a simulation) that a no-cut zone of 3 would tip it to the walker. The probe (naive fencing, daily mazes 1-30, the exit tail always protected) did not reproduce that.
- "Radius r" below counts runs from the exit junction (r = 1 is the runs at that junction). The design's own counts put its "zone 2 and 3" at the probe's 3 and 4, so what the zone means is also open.
- Cutter wins out of 30 mazes, one run a turn: 30 at every radius 0-5, 29 at 6, 28 at 7. Two runs a turn: 29 at radius 2, 27 at 3, 26 at 4, 18 at 5, 14 at 6, 12 at 7.
- A cut every other turn is the same as two runs a turn. A bigger zone means more cutting: the median fence is 11 runs at radius 3 and 32 at radius 7.
- The probe's cutter is naive: it keeps cutting after the walker is inside the fence, and five mazes have a one-run fence at radius 1, trivial there (K714). Smarter play on either side could move the numbers (K716).

## Options
- **Faster walker and a zone of 5 or 6 (rec)**: two runs a turn; naive fencing then wins 18 or 14 of 30 and the walker takes the rest.
- **Only a bigger zone**: at one run a turn even radius 7 gives the cutter 28 of 30, with a fence of about 32 runs.
- **Only a faster walker**: two runs a turn with a small zone (2-3) still gives the cutter 27-29 of 30.
- **Drop "Keep it in"**: Lead the walker and Delay it already exist; this would have been the third.

## After you answer
- A choice: a stage builds Keep it in with that walker speed and zone, and the zone is defined in the rules text.
- Drop: the item closes and the design note is marked unbuilt.
