## Question

- Under reduced motion no ripple shows at all, with or without High contrast.
- It waits on you because only you can say whether the ring reads well there.

## Options

- **It needs work**: say what (stronger, longer, another look), and it gets another round.

## How to check
1. Open the link. Pull a thread a little, away from any junction, and let it go: the glint runs out from where you let go.
2. In the panel, open Accessibility and turn on High contrast. Pull a thread and let it go again: now the ring runs out.
3. In the panel's Touch & feel folder, look at the Pluck ripple row: it is dimmed and still shows glint. Turn High contrast off to have the glint back.
