## Question
After you press an arrow key (or W, A, S, D) once, small arrows appear at the start of each run leaving your junction, showing which key takes which run. At the default marker size they sit mostly under your own marker, and only their tips show. Move them out past the marker, or keep them as they are?

## Context
- Today every hint overlaps your marker (163 of 163 in their count, 92 of them centred inside it). Moved out, none does.
- "Out" puts each hint whole on its thread, just clear of the marker's edge. Both placements show the same key for the same run.
- In both, a hint can still touch the start marker's coloured dot at the entrance, which is wider than your marker and sits under you there.
- The out placement is built (f2c79236) and is live behind a console switch only: no link setting, no panel row, nothing stored. Today's placement stays the default.

## Options
- **Move them out**: the out placement becomes the default.
- **Keep them as they are**: nothing a player sees changes.

## How to check
1. Open the link and press an arrow key once: the hints appear at your junction. A key with no run that way shows them without moving you.
2. To see them moved out, open the browser console (F12) and type `__knotKeyHintPlace("out")`; the next frame draws them there. `__knotKeyHintPlace("reach")` puts them back.
3. Move along a few runs and watch the hints at each junction, in both placements.

## After you answer
- Out: a small change makes it the default, and the item closes.
- Keep: the item closes.
