## Context
- **Reset path with the fire on** restarts the race with the fuse relit. The alternative, walking back to the entrance, means walking into the fire, because the entrance burns first.
- **The fire and the turn-based minotaur never run together.** Since the game-mode setting (`gm=burn`, `minotaur` or `classic`) only holds one at a time, this is now built into the setting. They combine only if you ask: a catch's respawn at a burnt entrance, and the fire's clock during a wait move, are both undefined.
- **Fog stays its own setting** (the exploration setting, `xp=fog`), not part of the burn mode. The simulation backs this: on dailies 1-30 the fire bites without fog (a player who knows the best route wins with the fire within one thread stretch behind in 80-100% of wins, at fire speeds 0.75 to 0.9). A fogged explorer is trapped in 90-97% of games until a later stage models the fire's own light.
- Reset path inside a daily is not settled; stage 4 of the burn race takes it.
