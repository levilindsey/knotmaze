## Question
When you drag a thread, the grabbed point chases your pointer at a set rate. It was 0.6 and is 0.8 now. Does the drag still feel right, or would you rather have 0.6 back or try 1.0?

## Context
- You said on 2026-10-22 that the recommendations stand: raise the rate and measure. K827 (da1d64cc, same day) raised the cap from 0.6 to 0.8 and added the `gcr` try key. Your feel call on the drag itself stayed open.
- A higher rate makes the grab stiffer: the grabbed point reaches your pointer in fewer physics steps. At 0.6 it took 4 steps to cover 90% of a pointer step (K370).
- The agent's measure, on the desktop only, dragging the daily: the median frame was 72.3 ms at 0.6 and 55.1 ms at 0.8 (a repeat gave 55.2). At 1.0 it was 55.1 ms, no cheaper than 0.8.
- So 0.8 is the cheaper drag; 1.0 differs from it only in feel, if at all. The phone was not re-measured for this.

## Options
- **Keep 0.8 (rec)**: as built; the daily's median drag frame is about 24% shorter than at 0.6.
- **Back to 0.6**: the old feel, at the old cost.
- **1.0**: stiffer still, no cheaper than 0.8.

## How to check
1. Open the first link, press on a thread and drag it around, slowly and then fast, and let go. On a phone, do it with a finger.
2. Open the second link (0.6) and make the same drags. Is there a difference you can feel?
3. Open the third link (1.0) and do it once more. Better, the same, or too stiff?
4. Tell me which rate feels best, or that you cannot tell.

## After you answer
Keep 0.8, or "cannot tell": K610 closes. Another rate: the cap becomes that value (one constant, `MAX_SLIDE_GRABBED`) and K610 closes.
