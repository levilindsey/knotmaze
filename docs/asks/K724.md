- **4 Par and stars rate the best legal route (rec, built)**: under a rule the stars measure against the best route the rule allows. A no means they measure against the plain route.
- **5 Only more exits changes generation (rec)**: locks, one-way runs, pickups, switches and tides use the maze as built. A no means they may reshape it. (The K138 ledger line lists six mechanisms as changing generation; this narrows that.)
- **6 Turnstiles and tides step on the move clock in every mode, real time included (rec, turnstiles built)**: Alternatives: a wall-clock or beat-timed turn in real time; turning on fresh moves only, not backtracks.
