## Question
Sprint is an optional, once-per-attempt burst of speed against the minotaur, off by default. It was built with five small choices, each as recommended (listed below). Is any of them a no?

## Context
- Built 2026-10-21 (K137 stage 3c, `docs/design/K137_s3c.md` Handoff "The calls" 1 to 5). With sprint off nothing changes; with it on, a lightning button (bottom left, key F) arms it.
- Turn clock: your next two moves count as one minotaur turn. It stands still for the first and plays its turn after the second. Real time: your next two runs play at double speed (20 against a pace of 10 in the page check).
- Page-checked 2026-10-21 on both clocks, at desktop size and in an emulated 411x753 window: a click arms it, a second click disarms it on the turn clock, F re-arms it, and a spent sprint ignores both. The real-phone sitting (K749, 4a1dd4d5) reported that sprint passes on both clocks.
- Call 1 is also call 5 of K705, whose list of alternatives differs; answering it in either card answers both.

## Options
- **1 By length, the pair's turn travels half their summed length (rec, built)**: it matters only in the by-length mode (`mbl=1`, off by default). Alternative: the minotaur skips the first move's share outright and plays the second at full length (simpler, but a long first run then buys more than a short one).
- **2 Real time, a press arms the next two runs you start (rec, built)**: a press mid-run waits for the next run. Alternative: the run under way counts as the first (sprint now, but half a run then counts as a whole one).
- **3 The key is F (rec, built)**: the design named Q, but moves are read by key position, so on an AZERTY keyboard Q sits where A does on a US one, a move key there. Alternatives: Q, or Shift.
- **4 A catch while armed or mid-sprint spends it (rec, built)**: Alternative: give it back when nothing was gained (armed, no move made yet).
- **5 A restart gives sprint back (rec, built)**: every restart does (a level restart, a Reset path, the scene build; the panel row restarts the solve). Alternative: once per attempt, kept spent across restarts.

## How to check
1. Open the first link, click the lightning button or press F: it lights and the HUD reads "sprint | armed". Walk one run: the minotaur stands still and the HUD reads "1 more move". Walk another: it plays one turn and the HUD reads "used".
2. Open the second link, arm it and walk: your next two runs go at twice the pace. Press it mid-run: that run stays normal and the next two are fast (call 2).
3. Open the third link for call 1: the minotaur travels by thread length, and a sprinted pair makes one turn sized by the average of your two runs.
