## Reading the rows

- The `arm N L a -> b, Lcut c -> d` text in "what changed" is F1's near-d-edge cut of that corner's arm (L = the arm's length, Lcut = its
  cut length from a pass behind its root). F2 rows carry such lines too, because the same arm moved under F1, not because F2 moved it:
  F2 changes r only (the `tip move (F1 .., F2 ..)` split says which of the two moved the tip). Many arm moves leave the ink unchanged,
  because the thread covers the arm's extent.
- Sites 03 and 04 are the two ends of one small eye (the same 112 px differ in both zoom 4 windows). Sites 13 and 14 are one corner
  (j49 151-152, the same numbers) in two scene files. The 18 pairs hold 16 distinct changes.
- A play-size window (+-8h) can hold other corners' F1 changes beside the site's own; the table marks it with `(play window: ..)`
  (site 08: 230 px in the play window, 36 in the zoom 4 window).
- j0 0-1 and j156 475-473: the defaults build no corner there (F1's near d-edge removes it); j36 109-110 and j155 471-472 keep a corner
  with r = 0. The old fills at j0, j155 and j156 are r 0.04-0.13h (0.7 to 2 device px), so the visible change at those three is at the
  thread ends and the eye, not in a fill.
- Sites 16-18 are not in the brief's list: they are the three largest tip moves among the corners the list does not already hold. All three are
  F1's (0.34 to 0.75h, F2 0.000h), against 0.10h for the largest F2 tip move among the ten F2 sites.
- Check: `FormB({dEdge: "skip", rootBand: 0})` reproduces the frozen S1f ink exactly at all 18 sites (0 px differ; `pairs.txt`), so "before"
  is S1f's final and the only difference to "after" is F1 and F2.
