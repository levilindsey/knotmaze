## The rule and what it leaves open

`cover_lib.mjs`'s header (19D's copy, quoted in `gk147.mjs`): the
last covering primitive wins per pixel and sample (no blending at
rest: `aM = 1`, `gk = 1`, `lookA = 1`); a fan triangle whose aLook at
the pixel centre exceeds 1.001 discards the whole pixel; a cover
strip is never discarded by its look; kinds are 1 this record's fan,
2 its cover strip, 3 any other record's primitive, 0 nothing (the
webs-off frame shows); and "Not modelled: gk (a foreign over
thread's silhouette discards a fan fragment)".

So the rule is: a foreign over thread's silhouette discards a fan
fragment. The header leaves four things underdetermined, named here
and supplied openly by the readings:

- Which threads are foreign and where their centrelines run. The
  dump holds no gap-slot table, no SAMP centreline, no slot strength
  and no per-triangle aMaskId (`diag_s4.mjs`'s `r<ji>.tri.bin`
  carries mesh, triangle index, vertices, aLook and aRec only), so
  no distance to an over centreline can be computed from it. The
  readings take the silhouette from the dump's frames instead.
- Whole pixel or per sample. The header's aLook precedent discards
  the whole pixel; per sample is tried too and collapses (below).
- Binary discard or partial keep. The header says "discards", so
  the dump readings are binary. A partial keep's fraction is in
  neither the header nor the dump; it is only fitted from the GPU's
  own lumas as a diagnostic bound (`fit`), never as evidence.
- What shows after a discard: the composite's kind-0 colour, the
  webs-off frame at that pixel (kinds 0 and 3 already read it).

Readings: `foot` (silhouette = any ribbon footprint in the
webs-off frame, webs-off luma over 40, all 276 crossed - the widest
reading; the dump cannot separate arm 1's own ribbon from a foreign
one in that frame), `debug` (crossed only where the GPU's debug-5
frame at the same state reads nearer the webs-off debug colour
than the fan's debug colour (0, twinG, 0); twinG is 0 at all 276),
`for` (foreign by material: webs-off RGB at quiet phase 3 differs
from the fan twin's out RGB by over 12; the same 6 pixels at every
phase), and `fit` (diagnostic per-pixel constant blend toward the
webs-off colour, fitted from the GPU itself - the most any
per-pixel keep built from the twin's colour and the webs-off
colour, per-sample or fan-only subsets included since their luma
lies on the same segment, can reach).

## Verdict: not found

What was ruled out, with the dump's numbers:

- Binary discard under the widest silhouette (`foot`, whole pixel
  and per-sample alike - every discarded sample's only under
  colour in the model is the pixel's webs-off colour, which kinds
  0 and 3 already read, so the two forms give one colour): the
  bright counts fall to 0, 0, 6, 10, 0. At the worst bright pixels
  the GPU sits between the two colours (t0 (180,70): GPU 218.2,
  fan twin 146.2, webs-off 226.6), not on the webs-off one.
- Discard where the fan in fact wrote nothing (`debug`): crossed
  at only 1, 1, 6, 6, 0 of the bright phases' pixels, and at the
  worst bright pixels the debug frame is the fan's own (0,0,0)
  against webs-off debug (0,202,0) to (0,220,0) - the fan fragment
  wrote there in the debug capture at the same pinned state, so
  no binary discard happened there.
- A foreign thread's material on top (`for`): it crosses only the
  same 6 edge pixels per phase and leaves every bright count at
  the without row. No other record's primitive covers any sample
  either (kind 3: 0 of 1104 samples), so no fan-over-fan
  silhouette is in the dump.
- Any per-pixel constant keep between the composite colour and
  the webs-off colour, gk-shaped or not: even fitted from the
  GPU's own lumas (keep 0.00 to 1.00 over the 36 pixels, median
  0.48) it reaches only 30, 30, 31, 30, 30 at the bright phases.
  29 of the 276 pixel-phases (6, 6, 5, 0, 0, 0, 6, 6 per phase)
  read darker than both source colours by over 2 luma - e.g. t0
  (170,72) GPU 87.4 against twin 140.2 and webs-off 132.1 along
  arm 1's outer edge (perp 0.16-0.21) - and no keep in [0, 1]
  reaches those at all; their fitted fractions fall outside
  [0, 1] at 10 of the 36 pixels.

What still differs / is not in the dump: ji 147's fan gap-slot ids
and, per slot, the over thread's SAMP centreline range, its
half-width and strength (the inputs a distance-based keep would
need); the per-sample under colours (the dump's frames are
MSAA-resolved screenshots); and whatever darkens the 29
edge pixel-phases above. A keep-like blend is consistent with
the fitted fractions, but from this dump it cannot be modelled
as gk, and even its best fit misses the bright-phase counts.

Arm 1's being unlit is unchanged: 19D's cause stands (no pulse
band declares it, rule (d)'s side factor is 0 at all 276 px, its
fan is the quiet base). This run only asked what paints the
bright GPU colours on those rim pixels; the answer is not found
in the cover model's missing gk input as the dump records it.
